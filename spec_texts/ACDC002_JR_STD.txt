Report ID: ACDC002_JR_STD

Report Title:
Reconciliation of ACDC versus AE form

Description:
This report will display inconsistencies between the subject status associated to COVID-19 recorded in ACDC form and the information in Adverse Event form.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Status Form

5. Form Date

6. Trial Segment

7. ACDC Response

8. Scenario

Logic:
Logic 1:
Logic 1.1: OEDRA form is present in study AND
(ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at the End of the Imaging/Efficacy Assessments Recorded on the OEDRA Form Associated With COVID-19? = Y) AND (OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = DISCONTINUED - ADVERSE EVENT) AND (There is no AE Term contains 'COVID' OR AE PT contains any of the words listed in Note 1)
OR
Logic 1.2: PMST form is present in study AND
(ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject at the End of All Study Treatments Recorded on the
PMST Form Associated With COVID-19? = Y) AND (PMST. Status of Subject at end of all Study
Treatments = DISCONTINUED - ADVERSE EVENT) AND (There is no AE Term contains 'COVID' OR AE PT that contains any of the words listed in Note 1)
OR
Logic 1.3:
For each row of the supporting document:
Matching ACDC question = Y AND (There is no AE Term contains 'COVID' OR AE PT contains any of the words listed in Note 1)
AND (Disposition form(PMS1). Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment= DISCONTINUED - ADVERSE EVENT OR Disposition form(PMS1A). Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment= DISCONTINUED - ADVERSE EVENT) for the corresponding trial segment
Example of Logic 1.3:
If in supporting document, there is a row as the following:
•	First Course, PMS1, Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of This First Course Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?
Then output will be produce if for the PMS1 with Trial Segment Reference as First Course the Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment is Discontinued – Adverse Event and in ACDC the question “Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of This First Course Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?” is Yes and in There is no AE with AE Term that contains 'COVID' OR with AE PT that contains any of the words listed Note 1.
Logic 2:
AE Term contains 'COVID' OR AE PT contains any of the words listed on Note 1 AND AE.Outcome of AE= FATAL
AND
(
Logic 2.1: OEDRA form is present in study AND
(OEDRA. Date of Assessment >= DS01.Date of Disposition) AND (ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at the End of the Imaging/Efficacy Assessments Recorded on the OEDRA Form Associated With COVID-19? WITHIN (N,U))
OR
Logic 2.2: PMST form is present in study AND
(PMST. Date of Assessment >= DS01.Date of Disposition) AND (ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject at the End of All Study Treatments Recorded on the PMST Form Associated With COVID-19? WITHIN (N,U))
OR
Logic 2.3:
(ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? WITHIN (N,U))
OR
Logic 2.4:
For each row of the supporting document:
Matching ACDC question WITHIN (N,U) AND (Disposition form(PMS1/PMS1A).Date of Assessment >= DS01.Date of Disposition) for the corresponding trial segment
Example of Logic 2.4:
If in supporting document there is a row as the following:
•	First Course, Cycle 1, Discontinuation Visit, PMS1, Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of This First Course Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?
Then output will be produce if for the PMS1 with Trial Segment Reference as First Course has a Date of Assessment equal or greater than DS01 date of disposition and the question “Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of This First Course Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?” is answered as No or Unknown
)
Note1:  consider any AE PT (codified term) that contains any of the following
- COVID-19
- SARS-CoV-2
- SARS COV2
- SARS Coronavirus 2
- SARS Coronavirus

*Report should identify the listed as key words e.g: If AE PT is “SARS-COV-2 sepsis” outputs is expected since it contains “SARS-CoV-2”

Note 2: Please exclude deleted AE records
Note 3: Please display one output for each sub-logic met (1.1 , 1.2 , 2.1…)
Note 4: For Logic 2 output should be produce if the first statement and one of the sub-logics is met
Note 5: Requires supporting document to be named ACDC002_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and  will contain the following columns and associated content: 1 row for each  variation of PMS1.
1) Trial Segment - As specified in the trial segment reference item on the PMS1 or PMS1A form
2) Disposition form - PMS1 or PMS1A
3) Matching ACDC question – Question of ACDC that corresponds to the PMS1 variation according to trial segment reference

E.g.
Trial Segment, Disposition form, Matching ACDC question
First Course, PMS1, Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of This First Course Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?
Follow-Up First Course, PMS1A, Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at End of This Follow-Up First Course Recorded on the PMS1A Form, Associated With COVID-19?
Second Course, PMS1, Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at End of This Second Course Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?

Report Scenarios:
A –Yes

There is a subject status associated to COVID-19 as "Yes" in ACDC form and Reason for Discontinuation in [PMS1, PMST and/or OEDRA] is "Adverse Event", but there is no COVID-19 adverse event in AE form with onset date prior or equal to the Date of Assessment in the disposition form.

B- No

There is a subject status associated to COVID-19 as "Yes" in ACDC form and Reason for Discontinuation in [PMS1, PMST and/or OEDRA] is "Adverse Event", and there is a COVID-19 adverse event in AE form with onset date prior or equal to the Date of Assessment in the disposition form.

C –Yes

There is a subject with a fatal COVID-19 adverse event but the subject status associated to COVID-19  in ACDC form is completed as “No” or “Unknown” for a PMS1, PMST, DS01 and/or OEDRA with a date after or on the same date than the start date of the AE.

D –No

There is a subject with a fatal COVID-19 adverse event but the subject status associated to COVID-19  in ACDC form is completed as “Yes” for a PMS1, PMST, DS01 and/or OEDRA with a date after or on the same date as the start date of the AE.

E –No

There is a subject with a fatal COVID-19 adverse event but the subject status associated to COVID-19  in ACDC form is completed as “No” or “Unknown” for a PMS1, PMST, DS01 and/or OEDRA with a date before the start date of the AE.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Form.Item Names:

4. DS01/OEDRA/PMST/PMS1/PMS1A.Form Mnemonic. Display the name of the form that caused the output (PMS1 or PMST or OEDRA or DS01 or PMS1X)

5. OEDRA/PMST/PMS1/PMS1A.Date of Assessment OR DS01.Date of Disposition. Display the corresponding date of assessment of the form that caused the output

6. OEDRA/PMST/PMS1/PMS1A.Trial Segment Reference OR For DS01 display NA. Display the corresponding Trial Segment Reference of the form that caused the output

7. ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? OR

ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at the End of the Imaging/Efficacy Assessments Recorded on the OEDRA Form Associated With COVID-19? OR

ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject at the End of All Study Treatments Recorded on the PMST Form Associated With COVID-19? OR

ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of the Study Medication for [Trial Segment], Recorded on the PMSX Form, Associated With COVID-19? OR

ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the [Trial Segment], Recorded on the [PMSX] Form, Associated With COVID-19?

Display the answer for the question on ACDC related to the form that caused the output

8. Display 'Scenario 1' if Logic 1 is met, 'Scenario 2' if Logic 2 is met
