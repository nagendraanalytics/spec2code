Report ID: RID340

Report Title:
Missing NMGA entry corresponding to Survival Follow-Up contact

Description:
This report will display output when Survival Follow-Up contact is recorded by site but New Malignancy Assessment (NMGA) entry with corresponding date is missing

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Screening Number

4. Subject Number

5. Form

6. Seq #

7. Date of Contact on SFU

Logic:
SFU_Form.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact <> NULL

AND there is no

NMGA_Form.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact = SFU_Form.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact

Note:

Note 1: Please exclude deleted records and erroneous visits from programming

Note 2: Report should compare each SFU eCRF entry with all NMGA entries

Note 3: Output is expected to be present for missing NMGA entry even if NMGA eCRF is non-initialized

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1

Output Yes:

SFU_Form.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact <>

ANY

NMGA_Form.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact

Output No:

SFU_Form.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact =

ANY

NMGA_Form.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
################

Form.Item Names:
1. SA. Site Country

2. SA. Site Mnemonic

3. SA. Screening Number

4. SA. Subject Number

5. SFU.Form Mnemonic

6. SFU.Form Index

7. SFU.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact
