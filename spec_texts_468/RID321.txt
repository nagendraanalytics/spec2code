Report ID: RID321

Report Title:
Reconcile response data

Description:
1)This report will display subjects that have a complete response and  high risk lesions identified on the LSNBC.

2) This report will display subjects that have a response of Progressive  Disease and no corresponding lesions identified on the LSNBC.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. OABC Seq

5. OABC - Overall Response

6. OABC - Date Response

7. LSNBC - Date of Lesion

8. LSNBC - Lesion Grade

9. Error Message

Logic:
Report should output subjects who meet the following criteria:

(Scenario 1)

Logic 1: OABC. 'Overall Response' = 'Complete Response' AND 'Date Response Determined' = LSNBC. 'Date of Lesion Evaluation' AND 'Lesion Grade' =  THREE SCALE SYSTEM-HIGH

OR

(Scenario 2)

Logic 2.1: [OABC. 'Overall Response' = 'Progressive Disease' AND there is not a LSNBC record with LSNBC. 'Date of Lesion Evaluation' = OABC. 'Date Response Determined'

OR

Logic 2.2: LSNBC = Null AND OABC. 'Overall Response' = 'Progressive Disease']

Note: Deleted records should not be considered in the programming and output.

Report Scenarios:
A - Yes

OABC. 'Overall Response' is equal to 'Complete Response' AND 'Date Response Determined' is equalt to LSNBC. 'Date of Lesion Evaluation' AND 'Lesion Grade' is equal to THREE SCALE SYSTEM-HIGH

B - Yes

OABC. 'Overall Response' is equal to 'Progressive Disease' AND 'Date Response Determined' is different to LSNBC. 'Date of Lesion Evaluation'

C - Yes

OABC. 'Overall Response' is equal to 'Progressive Disease' AND LSNBC is null

D - No

OABC. 'Overall Response' is equal to 'Complete Response' AND 'Date Response Determined' is equalt to LSNBC. 'Date of Lesion Evaluation' AND 'Lesion Grade' is different to THREE SCALE SYSTEM-HIGH

E - No

OABC. 'Overall Response' is equal to 'Progressive Disease' AND 'Date Response Determined' is equal to LSNBC. 'Date of Lesion Evaluation'

F - No

The records are deleted.

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject, Randomized Only

Form.Item Names:
1. SUBJECT ASSIGNMENT.Country

2. SUBJECT ASSIGNMENT. Site

3. SUBJECT ASSIGNMENT. Subject Number

4. OVERALL ASSESSMENT NON-MUSCLE INVASIVE BLADDER CANCER. Sponsor ID Number

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT NON-MUSCLE INVASIVE BLADDER CANCER. Overall Response

6. OVERALL ASSESSMENT NON-MUSCLE INVASIVE BLADDER CANCER.Date Response Determined

7. LESION EVALUATION - NON-MUSCLE INVASIVE BLADDER CANCER. Date of Lesion Evaluation

8. LESION EVALUATION - NON-MUSCLE INVASIVE BLADDER CANCER. Lesion Grade

9. Error Message.

Note: For 'Error Message', display 'Scenario 1' for Logic1; display 'Scenario 2' for Logic 2.
