Report ID: RID328

Report Title:
Baseline Lesion in LSNPC is Not Measurable

Description:
This report will list subjects who do not have at least one measureable Lesion at baseline.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Name

5. SM Form

6. Date of first dose

7. Lesion Number

8. Location (Organ)

Logic:
Logic 1: Date of first dose <> NULL

AND

Logic 2: LSNPC form. Tumor Identification = Target Nodal or Target Extra Nodal

AND

Logic 3: LSNPC.Date of Lesion Evaluation is within X days prior Date of First Dose (Only date is considered)

AND

Logic 4: Do not have at least one LSNPC. Lesion evaluation Entry where:

LSNPC form. Location (Organ) Contains “Lymph Node” and Target Lesion Measurement is greater than or equal to 15 mm

OR

LSNPC form. Location (Organ) not contains “Lymph Node” and Target Lesion Measurement is greater than or equal to 10 mm

Please exclude deleted record and erroneous visit from programming.

Note 1:  X is the number of days specified in the “Window of days” parameter.

Note 2: Date of First Dose is the Start Date/Time of the min SM/SMAU record were “Please Indicate if this is the First Dose for this Label Description” is checked.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A, Output: Yes

Subjects who have SM records but do not have at least one LSNPC Date of Lesion Evaluation with Date of Lesion Evaluation within X days prior to the Date of First Dose with either:

(1)Location (organ) contains “Lymph Node” and Target Lesion Measurement  >= 15 mm

OR

(2) Location (organ) contains “Lymph Node” and Target Lesion Measurement   is >= 10 mm.

Scenario B, Output: No

Subjects who have SM records and have at least one LSNPC Date of Lesion Evaluation with Date of Lesion Evaluation within X days prior to the Date of First Dose with either:

(1)Location (organ) contains  “Lymph Node” and Target Lesion Measurement  >= 15 mm

OR

(2) Location (organ) not contains “Lymph Node” and Target Lesion Measurement   is >= 10 mm.

Scenario C, Output: No

Deleted record

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Randomized Only, Window of days

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment. Site Country

2. Subject Assignment form.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects. Subject Number

4. Visit. Visit Mnemonic

5. SM/SMAU

6. Date of First dose

7. LSNPC. Lesion Number

8. LSNPC. Location (Organ)
