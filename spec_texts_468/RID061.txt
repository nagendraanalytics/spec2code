Report ID: RID061

Report Title:
Consistency between Subject Disposition and/or Protocol Milestone status and Eligibility Criteria

Description:
This report lists Screen Failures subjects that have data at PMS1 form and/or disposition data has not been entered at the DS01 or is not SF reason.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Screening Number

4. Subject Number

5. Did subject meet all eligibility criteria?

6. Date of randomization

7. SM available?

8. DS01 Subject Status

9. PMS1 Date of Assessment

10. Issue with eCRF

Logic:
1. This report lists Screen Failures subjects, defined as subjects was not randomized with Eligibility Criteria on the SA form equal to 'No' who meet any of below scenarios:

- PMS1 form is initialized

- DS01 form is not initialized

- DS01 form is initialized and DS01 Discontinuation reason <> Screen Failure OR Prescreen Failure

AND

2. This report will also list subjects randomized but never treated who meet any of the below scenarios:

- DS01 reason for discontinuation is not <Scenario 4. DS01 Disposition Reason>.

- PMS1 form is initialized

AND

3. This report will also list subjects randomized in error never treated that have DS01 reason for discontinuation is not <Scenario 5. DS01 Disposition Reason>.

Note:

1. Exclude erroneous visit and deleted record from programming.

2. Use with PMS1 and its variation form that collect the End of study medication (Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment) or collect the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment (Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment)

3. For scenario D and E, if no prompt is selected, output the subject DS01 reason=null

This report can use the optional PMS1 supporting document. It is only required in the following case: PMS1 and its variation are used to collect status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment (Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment)

Optional* supporting document should be named as DS003A_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1. eCRF – PMS1/PMS1A/PMS1B/PMS1C/PMS1D/PMS1E/PMS1F

2. Matching Item – Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment

Report Scenarios:
A Yes- Screen Failure subjects who have date entered in PMS1.

SA = No AND SM = Null AND PMS1 <> Null

B Yes- Screen Failure subjects who have not disposition data at DS01.

SA = No SR=Null AND SM = Null AND DS01 = Null

A Yes- Screen Failure subjects who have date entered in PMS1.

SA = No AND SM = Null AND PMS1 <> Null

B Yes- Screen Failure subjects who have not disposition data at DS01.

SA = No SR=Null AND SM = Null AND DS01 = Null

C Yes- Screen Failure subjects who have DS01 reason for discontinuation is not Screen Failure or Prescreen Failure.

SA = No AND SR = Null AND DS01 <> SF OR Prescreen Failure

D Yes- Subjects Randomized never treated who have DS01 reason for discontinuation is not Reason defined in DRP. e.g. Withdrawal by Subject.

SA = Yes AND SR <> Null AND SM = Null AND DS01 <> [Reason selected in Scenario 4. DS01 Disposition Reason]

E Yes- Subjects Randomized in error never treated that have DS01 reason for discontinuation is not Reason defined in DRP. e.g. Randomized By Mistake Without Study Treatment.

SA = No AND SR <> Null AND SM = Null AND DS01 <> [Reason selected in Scenario 5. DS01 Disposition Reason]

F Yes- Subjects Randomized never treated who have data entered in PMS1.

SA = Yes AND SR <> Null AND SM = Null AND PMS1 <> Null

G No- Screening Failure subject DS01 Discontinuation reason =Screen Failure and do not has disposition data on PMS1

H No- Subject meets all eligibility criteria

I No -Erroneous visit is checked

Note:

1. SM = null means Start date/time=null in SM, SM01, SM02, SM04, SMAU,SMAB or STR;

2. SR <> null means Date of Assessment<>null

3. PMS1 <> null means Date of Assessment <> null for PMS1 and its variation (that collects Study medication status or collect the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment)
