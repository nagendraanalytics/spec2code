Report ID: RID244

Report Title:
Immune related AE

Description:
This report will display subjects with incorrectly reported "Is the AE Immune related" question.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject number

4. AE Seq

5. AE Start date

6. Study Medication

7. Study Medication Start Date

8. Is the AE Immune related?

9. Cohort (if Applies)

10. Arm (If Applies)

Logic:
Logic 1: CV_SMAUFM/CV_SMABFM/CV_SM01FM Study Medication=Study Medication selected  AND

AE Onset date and time > or = than CV_SMAUFM/CV_SMABFM/CV_SM01FM First Dose AND

"Is the AE Immune related" = Not Applicable

Logic 2: CV_SMAUFM/CV_SMABFM/CV_SM01FM Study Medication=Study Medication selected  AND

AE Onset date and time < than CV_SMAUFM/CV_SMABFM/CV_SM01FM First Dose AND

"Is the AE Immune related" <> Not Applicable

Logic 3: CV_SMAUFM/CV_SMABFM/CV_SM01FM Study Medication<> Study Medication selected  AND

"Is the AE Immune related" <> Not Applicable

Note 1: Please exclude deleted records from programming

Note 2: For CV_SMAUFM and CV_SMABFM forms, item “Please Indicate if this is the First Dose for this

Label Description” (SMABEXFDINDCCB_SMABEXFDIND_1/SMAUEXFDINDCCB_SMAUEXFDIND_1) should be checked.

Note 3: "Study medication" should be derived from CV_SMPUFM/CV_SMPBFM form by using 'Dose Number+ Visit Number' between SMAUFM/CV_SMABFM and SMPUFM/CV_SMPBFM. For CV_SM01FM, Item is Study Medication due form design.

Note 4. For Columns 9 and 10, it is depending on the desing of the trial to show te information. If not applies one of the item, please left it in blank.

Note 5: For Logic 3, left blank columns 6 and 7.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A/Yes:

Subject is receiving 3475 AND AE Onset date is on or after first dose of Study Medication selected  AND "Is the AE Immune related" is "Not Applicable"

Scenario B/Yes

Subject is receiving 3475 AND AE Onset date is before first dose of Study Medication selected  AND "Is the AE Immune related" is not "Not Applicable"

Scenario C/Yes

Subject is NOT receiving Study Medication selected 5 AND "Is the AE Immune related" is "Not Applicable"
