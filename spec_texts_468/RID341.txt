Report ID: RID341

Report Title:
OABC reconciliation

Description:
Output is produced when subject was randomized and does not have a record in OABC every time a CTO is performed and when PMS1/PMST is completed as Persistent disease, progressive disease or recurrence/Relapsed Disease but there is no OABC with Persistent disease, progressive disease or recurrence/Relapsed Disease as overall response within selected window of days.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. CTO Seq#

5. CTO Date of Assessment

6. OABC Seq#

7. OABC Date Response Determined

8. OABC Overall Response

9. PMS1/PMST Visit

10. PMS1/PMST Date of Assessment

11. PMS1/PMST Status of Study Medication

12. Scenario

Logic:
LOGIC 1

Logic 1.1 (CTO.Date of assessment OR Cystoscopy Date <> NULL)

AND

(

Logic 1.2 There is no OABC record

OR

Logic 1.3 There is no OABC record where OABC. Date Response Determined = CTO.Date of assessment OR Cystoscopy Date mentioned in Logic 1.1

)

LOGIC 2

Logic 2.1

PMS1. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments = PMS1/PMST Status (as provided in supp doc)

AND There is no

OABC. Date Response Determined => PMS1.Date of Assessment/PMST.Date of Assessment - Window of days (as provided in supp doc) AND <= PMS1.Date of Assessment/PMST.Date of Assessment

OR

Logic 2.2

PMS1. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments = PMS1/PMST Status (as provided in supp doc)

AND

OABC. Date Response Determined => PMS1.Date of Assessment/PMST.Date of Assessment - Window of days (as provided in supp doc) AND <= PMS1.Date of Assessment/PMST.Date of Assessment

AND

OABC. Overall Response <> OABC Overall Response (as provided in supp doc for corresponding PMS1/PMST Status)

Requires supporting document to be named ONC_OABC_002_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

PMS1/PMST Status – provide PMS1. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments to be verified for Scenario 2

OABC Overall Response – provide OABC. Overall corresponding to the PMS1/PMST Status in the same row,

Window of days – provide max allowed window of days between OABC and PMS1/PMST for Scenario 2

For example:

PMS1/PMST Status, OABC Overall Response, Window of days

Stable Disease/Persistent Disease, Persistent Disease, 28

Progressive Disease, Progressive Disease, 28

Relapse/Recurrence, Recurrence/Relapsed Disease, 28

Note 1: Deleted records or erroneous visits will be excluded

Note 2: Leave column 6-11 blank when meet logic 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 ; Leave column 4-8 blank when meet logic 2.1; Leave column 4-5 blank when meet logic 2.2.

Note 3: Report only checks randomized subjects.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1

Output yes

CTO was performed but no record in OABC was found.

Output yes

CTO was performed and OABC was found but CTO date of assessment is different than OABC date of assessment.

Output No

CTO was performed and OABC was found and CTO date of assessment is equal to OABC date of assessment

Scenario 2

Output yes

PMS1/PMST was completed as provided in supporting doc and there is no OABC with Date within PMS1/PMST - window of days

Output yes

PMS1/PMST was completed as provided in supporting doc and there is OABC with Date within PMS1/PMST - window of days and OABC Overall Response is different than provided in supporting doc

Output No

PMS1/PMST was completed as provided in supporting doc and there is OABC with Date within PMS1/PMST - window of days and OABC Overall Response was completed as provided in supporting doc
