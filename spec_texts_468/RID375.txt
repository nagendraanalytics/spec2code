Report ID: RID375

Report Title:
Missing RECIST Overall Oncology Response in multi-phase studies

Description:
Report will display subjects who have Overall RECIST Response missing in ORIR when subject entered the next treatment phase. Once subject enters next treatment phase, the Oncologic Response should be assessed from re-baseline starting point using RECIST criteria.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. ORIR Seq #

5. ORIR Study Period

6. Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

7.  Overall RECIST Response

8. Overall iRECIST Response

9.  Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

Logic:
1. The Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) on Oncologic response form ORIR >= DOV date of  First Visitmnemonic of Treatment Phase (from study_phase_supp doc except Phase Order 1)

AND

2. The Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) = Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan Image Physical Exam)

AND

3. Overall RECIST Response = NULL.

AND

4. Overall iRECIST Response <> NULL.

Note 1: The report logics should be checked with LSNIR & ORIR records that have the same treatment phase defined in the supporting document.

This supporting document is only required to be created once per trial. If it was already created and uploaded, there is no need to duplicate it.

The supporting document should be named as study_phase_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

One row for each Treatment Phase

1. Phase Order: required, the order on which phases will appear on protocol

2. Treatment Phase: name of each study phase (Intial Treatment Phase, Neodjuvant Phase, Secon Course Phase)

3. First Visitmnemonic of Treatment Phase: required,the first visit on which treatment is expected to be administered on each phase.

e.g.

1, Initial Treatment Phase, Cycle 1

2, Second Course Phase, Cycle 1 Course 2

3, Crossover Phase, Crossover Cycle 1 "

Report Scenarios:
A – YES:

Subject entered the next treatment phase (Crossover phase or Second Course) and The Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) on Oncologic response form ORIR is the earliest date after entering the next phase and has Overall RECIST response missing while the iRECIST Overall response is completed.

 B – NO:

Subject entered the next treatment phase (Crossover phase or Second Course) and The Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) on Oncologic response form ORIR is the earliest date after entering the next phase and has Overall RECIST response completed.

C – NO:

Subject entered the next treatment phase (Crossover phase or Second Course) and The Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) on Oncologic response form ORIR is the earliest date after entering the next phase and has Overall RECIST response completed AND the row is deleted.

D – NO

Subject entered the next treatment phase (Crossover phase or Second Course) and The Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) on Oncologic response form ORIR is the earliest date after entering the next phase and has Overall RECIST response missing while the iRECIST Overall response is completed AND the row is deleted.

E - NO

Subject entered the next treatment phase (Crossover phase or Second Course) and The Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) on Oncologic response form ORIR is greater than the earliest date after entering the next phase and has Overall RECIST response missing while the iRECIST Overall response is completed.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, ORIR Seq #, Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam),

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized only (N/Y, default as N), Study Phase Page (N/Y, default as N)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA Form. Site Country

2. SA Form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA Form. Subject Number

4. Oncologic Response - Solid Tumor - RECIST/iRECIST.Sponsor ID Number

5. Oncologic Response - Solid Tumor - RECIST/iRECIST.Study Period

6. Oncologic Response - Solid Tumor - RECIST/iRECIST. Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

7. ORIR."OVERALL RECIST RESPONSE"

8. ORIR."OVERALL IRECIST RESPONSE"

9. LSNIR. Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)
