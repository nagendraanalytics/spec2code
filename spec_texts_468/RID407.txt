Report ID: RID407

Report Title:
Surgery Date Mismatches

Description:
This report will check consistency between date of surgery recorded in PTHA and OSOFL/OSOC/OSOF

Column Heading:
Table 1:

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Seq # (PTHA)

5. Date of Biopsy (PTHA)

6. Oncology Surgery form

7. Seq # (Oncology Surgery)

8. Date of Oncology Surgery

9. Scenario

Table 2:

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Seq # (PTHA)

5. Date of Biopsy (PTHA)

6. Oncology Surgery form

7. Date of Oncology Surgery

8. Scenario

Logic:
Scenario 1:

Logic 1: Oncology Surgery.Date = PTHA.Date of Biopsy AND Oncology Surgery.Intent of Surgery <> Curative.

Scenario 2:

Logic 2: Oncology Surgery.Date <> NULL AND Oncology Surgery.Intent of Surgery = Curative AND there is not a PTHA record with Oncology Surgery.Data = PTHA.Date of Biopsy.

Scenario 3:

Logic 3: PTHA.Date of Biopsy <> NULL AND there is not an Oncology Surgery.Date record with Oncology Surgery.Date = PTHA.Date of Biopsy.

Notes:

1. Do not output for deleted records or erroneous visits

2. Do not take time into account for comparisons

3. “Oncology Surgery” = OSOC, OSOF and OSOFL.

4. Oncology Surgery.Date = OSOC/OSOF/OSOFL.Date Performed/Date and Time Performed, OSOFL.Date of Surgery.

5. If there are more “Oncology Surgery” available generate output for all the forms.

6. Consider including scenario 1 and 3 in table 1 and scenario 2 in table 2.

7. For scenario 1 all the columns should be completed with the corresponding data.  For scenario 2 columns 4 and 5 should be blank. For scenario 3 columns 6 and 7 should be blank.

8. For UNK day and month, please consider the first day and first month.

9. If PTHA.Date of Biopsy is not exists or is blank, use PTHA.Date of Surgury replace.  If PTHA.Date of Biopsy and PTHA.Date of Surgury both not exist, use PTHA.Date of Assessment replace.

10. Blank value should also output for scenario 1.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1:

A – NO Oncology Surgery.Date = PTHA.Date of Biopsy AND Oncology Surgery.Intent of Surgery = Curative.

B – YES Oncology Surgery.Date = PTHA.Date of Biopsy AND Oncology Surgery.Intent of Surgery <> Curative.

Scenario 2:

A – NO Oncology Surgery.Date <> NULL AND Oncology Surgery.Intent of Surgery = Curative AND  PTHA.Date of Biopsy = Oncology Surgery.Date

B – YES Oncology Surgery.Date <> NULL AND Oncology Surgery.Intent of Surgery = Curative AND  PTHA.Date of Biopsy is missing

Scenario 3:

A – NO PTHA.Date of Biopsy <> NULL AND Oncology Surgery.Date = PTHA.Date of Biopsy AND Oncology Surgery.Intent of Surgery = Curative.

B – YES PTHA.Date of Biopsy <> NULL AND Oncology Surgery.Date is missing.
