Report ID: RID336

Report Title:
NAPS, PMS1, PMS1A, PMST listing

Description:
This report will list subjects who have data in PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NAPS.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Mnemonic

5. Form Mnemonic

6. Date of Assessment

7. Reason for Discontinuation

8. Next Trial Segment

9. NAPS Seq #

10. Indicate the Phase Not Started by the Subject

11. Reason Phase Not Started

Logic:
If PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NAPS.Date of Assessment AND PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments AND PMS1/PMS1A/PMST.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment AND NAPS. Indicate the Phase Not Started by the Subject AND NAPS. Reason Phase Not Started are entered

Notes:

1. PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment should only be considered if present in the study.

2. NAPS.Sponsor ID Number, NAPS. Indicate the Phase Not Started by the Subject and NAPS. Reason Phase Not Started columns should be blank for PMS1, PMS1A and PMST forms in the report. (columns 9, 10 and 11)

3. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments should be blank for NAPS form in the report. (column 7)

4. Deleted records and erroneous visit should not be considered

5. PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments should only be considered if present in the study.

Report Scenarios:
A- Yes

PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NAPS.Date of Assessment AND PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments AND PMS1/PMS1A/PMST.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment AND NAPS. Indicate the Phase Not Started by the Subject AND NAPS. Reason Phase Not Started are entered

B- No

One or more of the following times is missing: PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NAPS.Date of Assessment,  PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments, PMS1/PMS1A/PMST.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment OR NAPS. Indicate the Phase Not Started by the Subject OR NAPS. Reason Phase Not Started are missing

C- No

NAPS.Records are deleted or NULL

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Date of Assessment

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (N/Y, default as N).

Form.Item Names:
1. SA. Site Country

2. SA. Site Mnemonic

3. SA. Subject Number

4. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NAPS. Visit Mnemonic

5. Form Mnemonic

6. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NAPS. Date of Assessment

7. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments

8. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NATS.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment

9. NAPS.Sponsor ID Number

10. NAPS.Indicate the Phase Not Started by the Subject

11. NAPS. Reason Phase Not Started
