Report ID: RID022

Report Title:
Relationship to Study Medication reconciliation with Dose Amount

Description:
Study Medication Dose Amount is Null or zero however, Relationship to Study Medication is checked as Related

Column Heading:
1 Country

2. Site Number

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq

5. AE Term

6. AE Onset Date

7. AE relationship

8. SM Seq

9. Study Medication/TACE

Logic:
Output the subjects who meet the following criteria.

Logic 1: AE relatonship to study medication/TACE= related

and

[Logic 2: Dose Amount on SM form = null or zero and study medication matches study medication in the item of AE relationship to study medication

OR

Logic 3: Date of TACE on TACE form = null

Note 1: Deleted record is not considered in program or output.

Note 2: If study is with multiple study medications on SM form, report needs to check each of AE relationship to study medication.

Note 3: Do not output if SM is not initialized.

Note 4: Logic 3 used for study using TACE form.
