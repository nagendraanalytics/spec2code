Report ID: RID043

Report Title:
Concomitant Medication Stop Date to Study Medication reconciliation

Description:
Comparison of concomitant medication stop date to start of Minimum First dose of study medication

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. CM Seq

5. Medication

6. Stop Date

7. CM taken prior to SM first dose?

8. Min SM Visit

9. Min SM form

10. SM Seq

11. Min first dose Start Date

12. Issue

Logic:
Scenario 1

Logic 1. CM.Stop Date = SMAB/SMAU/SM.Start Date/Time of first dose entry

AND

Logic. 1.1 Time is not present or time is UNK

AND

Logic 1.2 Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication? is blank

OR

Logic 2. CM Stop Date= SMAB/SMAU/SM.Start Date/Time of first dose entry

AND

Logic 2.1 Time is present

AND

[

Scenario 2

Logic 2.2 Time on CM < Time on SM

AND

Logic 2.3 Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication? <> YES

]

OR

[

Scenario 3

Logic 2.4 Time on CM > Time on SM

AND

Logic 2.5 Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication? <> NO

]

OR

Logic 3 CM.Stop Date<> SMAB/SMAU/SM.Start Date/Time of first dose entry.

AND

[

Scenario 4

Logic 3.1 Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication? = YES

AND

[

Logic 3.2 CM.Stop Date/time > SMAB/SMAU/SM.Start Date/Time of first dose entry.

OR

Logic 3.3 CM.Stop Date/time = ‘Continuing’

]

OR

[

Scenario 5

Logic 3.4 Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication? = NO

AND

Logic 3.5 CM.Stop Date/time < SMAB/SMAU/SM.Start Date/Time of first dose entry.

]

Notes

1.	Do not include deleted/erroneous visit data into programming.

2.	For scenario 4 and 5:If CM Stop date= UNK, but Month and Year are available, CM Month and Year will be compared with SMAB/SMAU/SM.Start Date of first dose entry Month and Year, if same, there will be no output; if not same, there will be output based on logic scenario 4 and 5.

3.	SM/SMAU/SMAB can exist in a same study, also cannot.

4.	If the start date/time of first dose in SM/SMAU/SMAB are same and are min first dose, please display in separate row in output.

5.	For scenario 2/Scenario 3: If the min SM start date (the other first dose have start date>min first dose) have time=unk, please don't output no matter what Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication? is entered.

6.	Apply for all scenarios: If more than one first dose has same start date and is min start date, and CM stop date=this min SM Start date, when there is time=unk for any of the min start date records, then don't include this SM into comparison.

Report Scenarios:
1.	If medication stopped on the first day of study medication: CM and SM start days are equal, and:

a.	Time not present or UNK

Scenario 1 - "Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication?" is blank

b.	Time present

Scenario 2 -  Time on CM is less than time on SM; and "Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication?" is not checked as YES  (output if the question is answered NO or is blank)

Scenario 3 - Time on CM is greater than time on SM; and "Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication?" is not checked as NO (output if the question is answered YES or is blank)

2.	If CM stop date and SM start date are different, question "Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication?" should not be completed, but if completed :

Scenario 4 : "Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication?" = YES, and CM Stop Date/time >  SM First dose entry OR CM Stop Date/Time = ‘Continuing’

Scenario 5 : "Did Medication Stop Prior to the First Dose of Study Medication?" = NO, and Stop Date/time is < SM  First dose entry}
