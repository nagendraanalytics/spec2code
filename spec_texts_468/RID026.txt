Report ID: RID026

Report Title:
Associated AE Sequence Number(s) in DEATH vs Outcome of AE

Description:
This report lists subjects with mismatch between AE sequence number(s) in Death form and the AE sequence number that has outcome of AE as fatal.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq

5. Outcome of AE

6. Scenario

Logic:
This report will list subjects with mismatches in fatal AEs and DEATH.

Display if:

1) None of the Associated AE Sequence Numbers on the DEATH form corresponds to an AE with Outcome of AE = Fatal

2) There is an AE with Outcome of AE = Fatal that has not been included on the DEATH form.

Deleted records should not be considered.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output = YES

Any of the Associated AE Sequence Numbers on the DEATH Form does not correspond to an AE with Outcome of AE = Fatal

2) Output = YES

AE with Outcome of AE = Fatal has not been included on the DEATH Form

3) Output = NO

At least one of the Associated AE Sequence Numbers on the DEATH Form corresponds to an AE with Outcome of AE = Fatal

4) Outcome = NO

All AE with Outcome of AE = Fatal have been included on the DEATH Form

5) Output = NO

Deleted records

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, AE Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject, Randomized Only, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. ADVERSE EVENTS Form.formidx / DEATH Form.Associated AE Sequence Numbers

5. ADVERSE EVENTS Form.Outcome of AE

6. Hardcoded to 1 or 2 according to the logic
