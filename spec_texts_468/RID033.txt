Report ID: RID033

Report Title:
APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction and APAE Onset Date and Time reconciliation

Description:
This report lists APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction after APAE Onset Date and Time.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. APDVA Seq

6. APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction

7. Associated APAE Sequence Number(s)

8. APAE Seq

9. APAE Onset Date

Logic:
The report should display output when following conditions are met:

APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction > APAE Onset Date which is associated by APAE Sequence Number in APDVA form

Note 1 Deleted records should not be taken into account.

Note 2 APDVA and APAE data should be checked within the same visit

Note 3 In APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction:

     -For UNK Day please consider first day of month

     -For UNK Month plrease consider first month of that year

     -For UNK:UNK Time - 00:00

*If both APAE and APDVA have the time component, then time should be taken into account. If one or both of the forms do not have the time component, then time should not be considered.

Note 4 If there are more than one associated APAE Seqence number in a APDVA record, please display in column 7 by using comma to concatenate.

Report Scenarios:
A-Yes

APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction > APAE Onset Date which is associated by APAE Sequence Number in APDVA form

B-No

APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction <= APAE Onset Date which is associated by APAE Sequence Number in APDVA form

C-No

APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction > APAE Onset Date which is NOT associated by APAE Sequence Number in APDVA form.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject, APDVA Seq, APAE Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject , Randomized Only

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment. Site Country

2. Subject Assignment. Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects Assignment. Subject Number

4. Date of Visit Form.Visit Mnemonic

5. APDVA. Sponsor ID

6. APDVA. APDVA Date/Time of Device Malfunction

7. APDVA. Associated APAE Sponsor ID Number(s).APDVACEAESPID1/ APDVACEAESPID2/ APDVACEAESPID3/ APDVACEAESPID4/ APDVACEAESPID5/ APDVACEAESPID6/ APDVACEAESPID7/ APDVACEAESPID8

8. APAE. Sponsor ID

9. APAE. Onset Date and Time (Lab Collection Date if Lab Adverse Events)
