Report ID: RID465

Report Title:
Mismatches between DOV and first entry of VST

Description:
This report will output mismatches between DOV and VST dates for Vital Status

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. VST seq

5. VST Date of Contact

6. Vital Status DOV

Logic:
Logic:

Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit is different from the earliest VST. Date of Contact

Note

Note 1: Do not output for erroneous visits or deleted records

Note 2: Please order dates from earliest to latest date.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1 – Yes

Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit <> NULL AND the earliest VST. Date of Contact  <> Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit

Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit =1-Sep-2018

VST seq#1 date of Contact = 7-Oct-2018

Scenario 2 – No

Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit <> NULL AND the earliest VST. Date of Contact  = Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit

Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit =1-Sep-2018

VST seq#1 date of Contact = 7-Oct-2018

VST seq#2 date fo contact = 1-Sep-2018

Scenario 3 – No

Deleted records or erroneous visits

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, VST seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Country

2. SA.Site

3. SA.Subject number

4. VST.Sponsor ID Number

5. VST.Date of Contact

6. Vital Status DOV. Date of Visit
