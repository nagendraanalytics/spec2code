Report ID: RID202

Report Title:
Duplicated Dose Number

Description:
This report displays duplicated dose numbers entered for the subject to ensure that every SMP entry has appropriate, unique dose number entered.

Column Heading:
1) Country

2) Site

3) Subject Number

4) Visit

5) SMA eCRF/ SMP eCRF

6) Dose Number

7) Study Med

8) # in SMA/# in SMP

Logic:
This report will output inconsistencies in Dose Numbers in SMAU, SMAB, SMPU, SMPB:

- # of records in SMP forms > 1

- # of records in SMA forms doesn't fulfils the selection in the prompt page.

The output will depend on the user:

- SMPB related rows will only be shown to "UNBLINDED" or "NO VIEW ACCESS" groups.

- SMAU, SMAB and SMPU related rows will be visible to all

The user name will be included in the footer.

The # in SMA and # in SMP columns refer to the count of records by subject, visit, form and dose number.

The # in SMA on the prompt page is defaulted to >1.

Deleted records are not considered.

Records in erroneous visits will not be included.

Note: Please do not show output if the SMA or SMP eCRF are not initialized

Report Scenarios:
1) Output YES

# in SMA does not satisfies the condition selected in the # SMA Records prompt.

2) Output YES

# in SMP > 1

3) Output NO

# in SMA satisfy the condition selected in the # SMA Records prompt.

4) Output NO

# in SMP = 1

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Visit, Dose Number

Parameter Selection:
Randomized Only, Country, Site, Subject, List of Subjects, # SMA Records

Form.Item Names:
1) Sites.Country

2) Sites.Site Name

3) Subjects.Subject Number

4) STUDY MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION form / STUDY MEDICATION PREPARATION form.Visitmnemonic

5) STUDY MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION form.Formmnemonic/STUDY MEDICATION PREPARATION form.Formmnemonic

6) STUDY MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION form / STUDY MEDICATION PREPARATION form.Dose Number

7) STUDY MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION form / STUDY MEDICATION PREPARATION form.Study Medication

8) Count of records in STUDY MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION form by Subject, Dose Number and Visit/Count of records in STUDY MEDICATION PREPARATION form by Subject, Dose Number and Visit
