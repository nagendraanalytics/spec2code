Report ID: RID099

Report Title:
External Data received after subject has discontinued/completed the study

Description:
To verify that the status of a subject is accurate, as no external data should be collected after a subject has either discontinued/completed the study.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. External Data

5. Vendor

6. Specimen ID

7. Kit ID

8. Collection Date/Time

9. Subject Status

10. Date of Last Contact

Logic:
This report will produce output when:

External data (LABS, PFT, GEFCF, CECG, CIMG, SME) is collected after subject has discontinued/completed the study.

Compare only the Date from the items list in below External Data forms with 'Date of Last Contact' in DS01 form, output if the Date is greater than Date of Last contact (User will provide the study required modules and specify the format for below terms from required vendor).

LABS: Actual collection Date and time

GEFCF: Date/time of measurement

PFT: Date and Time

CECG: Collection Date and Time

CIMG: Date of Imaging

SME: Start Date/Time

* Don't include deleted data in programming.

* If ""Randomized only"" parameter selection checkbox is checked, the report should only output subjects with Randomization number that is not null.

Report Scenarios:
A-No

No status in DS01

B-No

No date from LABS/GEFCF/PFT/ECCG/SME is after Date of Last Contact

C-Yes

The status of DS01 should be discontinued or completed and there is date from LABS/GEFCF/PFT/ECCG/SME is after Date of Last Contact

Sorting:
