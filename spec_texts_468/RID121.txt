Report ID: RID121

Report Title:
Eligibility criteria met but IE form completed

Description:
This report will display subjects who met all eligibility criteria according to SA form but IE form is completed

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. eCRF

Logic:
This report displays subjects who meet all eligibility criteria but there is data on the IE form.

It is considered that subjects meet all eligibility criteria if SA question "Did subject meet all eligibility criteria?"=YES.

In case of multiple IE forms, all of the them will be evaluated for any data entered.

Erroneous visits should not be considered.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=YES

SA question "Did subject meet all eligibility criteria?"=YES and there is data on any of the items on IE

2) Output=NO

SA question "Did subject meet all eligibility criteria?"=YES and there is no data on any of the items on IE

3) Output=NO

SA question "Did subject meet all eligibility criteria?"=NO

4) Output=NO

Data on erroneous visit

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Randomized Only, Country, Site, Subject Number, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. Forms.Formmnemonic
