Report ID: RID107

Report Title:
Visits Missing External Vendor eCOA Data / External Vendor eCOA Data with No Corresponding Visit Date / Date Mismatch between InForm vs Vendor

Description:
1. Scheduled visit date is entered at particular visit in Inform but there are no corresponding eCOA/ePRO data records with a collection date equal to the visit date or within allowed window per protocol.

2. Data from both Inform & external vendor is present with same visit description but different dates

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Screening Number

4. Subject Number

5. Vendor

6. Category

7. InForm Visit

8. InForm DOV

9. Visit ID or Number

10. Measurement Date/Time

11. Completed/Missmode

12. Issue

Logic:
Scenario 1: No External data

Output is produced if patient has a Date of Visit entered for a scheduled visit and there are no corresponding ePRO/eCOA records per Category in the GEFCF eCRF with the same Measurement Date within the allowed window.

Hardcode the issue as "Missing External Data"

Scenario 2: Date Mismatch

Output is produced if data from both Inform & external vendor is present with the same visit description but  Date/Time of Measurement is not within Date of Visit +/- allowed window.

Hardcode the issue as "Date Mismatch"

Requires supporting document to be named EVD031_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1)visitmnemonic  - as defined in InForm. It is the visit at which ePRO/eCOA is expected.

2)vendor – provide vendors.

3)category – provide Categories acording to PSA

4)eCRF -  enter the eCRF that will define the date of visit DOV or for Post-Follow-up visits with VS02

5)VisitDate -  Item on the eCRF will have the date (DOV - Date of visit) VS02 (Date/Time of Assessment)

6)mindays – allowed number of days prior to the DOV when the ePRO/eCOA may be performed. May be zero.

7)maxdays - allowed number of days after the DOV when the ePRO/eCOA may be performed. May be zero.

8)VendorVisit - as defined by the vendor in ePRO/eCOA.

Eg :

Visitmnemonic,vendor,category,eCRF,VisitDate,mindays,maxdays,VendorVisit

CYCLE 1,CRFH,EORTC QLQ-C30,DOV,Date of Visit,7,7,CYCLE 1

CYCLE 3,CRFH,EORTC QLQ-STO22,DOV,Date of Visit,1,7,CYCLE 3

FOLLOW-UP 1,CRFH,EQ-5D-5L,VS02,Date/Time of Assessment,7,14,FOLLOW-UP 1

Note:

1. Visits should have the Erroneous visit checkbox unchecked.

2. Report should not consider deleted records.

3. For scenario 2, NO output if at least one Date/Time of Measurement is within Date of Visit +/- allowed window.

4. For Post- Follow-up visit that required eCOA/ePRO data use the appropriate eCRF that collect the visit date E.G VS02

5. export data to csv format instead of excel

ePRO measurement date are not within the allowed window of DOV in the same visit; or DOV of a visit in Inform exist but no corresponding visit lable in ePRO/eCOA.

B-No.

ePRO/eCOA measurement date are within the allowed window of DOV in the same visit.

C-No.

erroneous visit
