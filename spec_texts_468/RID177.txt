Report ID: RID177

Report Title:
Dose Frequency vs Dosage Prescribed reconciliation

Description:
This report will show visits in which the Study Medicaiton didn't used as specified Dosage Prescribed and Dosage Frequency.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. Dose number

6. Dose Frequency

7. Dosage Prescribed

Logic:
Logic 1:

SMAU.Dose Number = [Study Medication] AND SMAU. Dose Frequency=[Dose Frequency1] AND SMPU. Dosage Prescribed<> [Dosage Prescribed 1].

Logic 2:

SMAU.Dose Number = [Study Medication] AND SMAU. Dose Frequency=[Dose Frequency2] AND SMPU. Dosage Prescribed<> [Dosage Prescribed 2].

Note:

1. Exclude deleted data and erroneous visit from programming.

2. Only output when expected visit DOV.date of visit is not null.

3. If Study Medication is provided, only output specified study medication. If not provided, output all eligible study medication.

4. At least one Dose Frequency1/2 and Dosage Prescribed 1/2 should be provided in parameter.

Report Scenarios:
A- Yes

SMAU. Dose Frequency=[Dose Frequency1] AND SMPU. Dosage Prescribed<> [Dosage Prescribed 1].

B- Yes

SMAU. Dose Frequency=[Dose Frequency2] AND SMPU. Dosage Prescribed<> [Dosage Prescribed 2].

C- No

SMAU. Dose Frequency=[Dose Frequency1] AND SMPU. Dosage Prescribed= [Dosage Prescribed 1].

D- No

SMAU. Dose Frequency=[Dose Frequency2] AND SMPU. Dosage Prescribed= [Dosage Prescribed 2].

E- No

Deleted record and erroneous visit.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Study Medication.

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site No., Last Site No., First Subject No., Last Subject No., List of Subjects, Randomized Only, Study Medication, Dosage Prescribed 1, Dose Frequency 1,  Dosage Prescribed 2, Dose Frequency 2,

Form.Item Names:
1. SA Form. Site Country

2. SA Form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA Form. Subject Number

4. Visit. Visit Mnemonic.

5. SMAU. Dose number

6. SMAU. Dose Frequency

7. SMPU. Dosage Prescribed
