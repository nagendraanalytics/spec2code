Report ID: RID021

Report Title:
Reconciliation between AE relationship to Study Medication and Study Medication

Description:
Relationship to Study Medication vs Study medication record.

Column Heading:
1 Country

2. Site Number

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq

5. AE Term

6. AE Onset Date

7. AE relationship

8. SM form

9. Visit Mnemonic

10.SM Start Date

11.SM Seq

12. Study Medication

13. Error Message

Logic:
Logic:

Output the subjects who meet the following criteria.

Logic 1: AE relationship = related and Number of units taken on SM/SMAU/SMAB form with first dose checked = blank or zero; or AE relationship = related and Dose Amount on STR form form with first dose checked = blank or zero; or AE relationship = related and Chemotherapy Dose of the minimum Date of TACE = blank or zero

OR

Logic 2: AE relationship = related and AE Onset date is before Start date on SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR form with first dose checked;

or AE relationship = related and AE Onset date is before Date of TACE of the minimum Date of TACE

Note 1: Erroneous visit or deleted record is not considered in program or output.

Note 2: If SM/SMAU/SMAB was not initiated at all, it should be output. Please consider null when TMS_SMEXENDTCIT items are not available.

Note 3: AE overall relationship will be used when study is with a single study medication. AE relationship with study medication will be used when study is with multiple study medications.

Note 4a: IF SM Label Description and SMAU/SMAB Dose Number are exactly matched within forms For SM/SMAU/SMAB Study Medication, use SM.Label Description/SMAU.Dose Number/SMAB.Dose Number directly.

Note 4b:If SM Label Description and SMAU/SMAB Dose Number are not exactly matched with Study Medication, using the optional supporting doc study_med_match_supp_.csv to define the mapping.

The optional supporting document is only required in the following cases:

- SM Label Description and SMAU/SMAB Dose Number are not exactly matched with Study Medication.

This supporting document is only required to be created once per trial. If it was already created and uploaded, there is no need to duplicate it.

Optional supporting document should be named as study_med_match_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1 row for each Label Description or Dose Number present in SM/SMAU/SMAB eCRFs. 

 

1. eCRF – SM/SMAU/SMAB

2. Study Medication – Value of Study Medication

3. Matching Item – SM Label Description or SMAU/SMAB Dose Number

4. Matching Value – Value of SM Label Description or SMAU/SMAB Dose Number

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A- Yes

AE relationship = related and Number of units taken on SM/SMAU/SMAB form with first dose checked = blank or zero

or

AE relationship = related and Dose Amount on STR form with first dose checked = blank or zero

or

AE relationship = related and Chemotherapy Dose of the minimum Date of TACE = blank or zero

Scenario B- Yes

AE relationship = related and AE Onset date is before Start date on SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR form with first dose checked

or

AE relationship = related and AE Onset date is before Date of TACE of the minimum Date of TACE

Scenario C- No

AE relationship = related and Number of units taken on SM/SMAU/SMAB form with first dose checked <> blank or zero and AE Onset date is equal to or greater than start date on SM/SMAU/SMAB form with first dose checked

or

AE relationship = related and Dose Amount on STR form with first dose checked <> blank or zero and AE Onset date is equal to or greater than start date on STR form with first dose checked

or

AE relationship = related and and Chemotherapy Dose on TACE form of the minimum Date of TACE <> blank or zero and AE Onset date is equal to or greater than Date of TACE of the minimum Date of TACE
