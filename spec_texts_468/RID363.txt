Report ID: RID363

Report Title:
ORAJ entries with Disease Recurrence Indicator as "No" after recurrence

Description:
This report will show the ORAJ entries with Disease Recurrence Indicator of "No" after a previous recurrence.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Sequence

5. Date of Assessment

6. Disease Recurrence Indicator

Logic:
Output is produced when:

ORAJ.Date of Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) > MIN [ORAJ. Date of Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) with Disease Recurrence Indicator = [Y]]

AND

ORAJ.Disease Recurrence Indicator = [N]

Note: please disregard the deleted entries

For example:

•	ORAJ sequence 1, Disease Recurrence Indicator = Yes, Date of Assessment = 25MAY2024

•	ORAJ sequence 2, Disease Recurrence Indicator = No, Date of Assessment = 25JUL2024

The report should output the ORAJ sequence 2, because once the subject has disease recurrence, it is considered to have recurrence in the following time points of evaluation.

Note 2: the dates should be displayed in the format "dd-Mmm-yyyy" (i.e. 01-May-2025).

Report Scenarios:
Output = Yes

ORAJ entries that have disease recurrence indicator as "No" after a previous evaluation completed as "Yes".

Output = No

ORAJ entries that have disease recurrence indicator as "Yes" after a previous evaluation completed as "Yes".

Deleted entries.

Sorting:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject number

4. Date of Assessment

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Site Country

2. SA.Site Mnemonic

3. SA.Subject Number

4. ORAJ.Form Sequence

5. ORAJ.Date of Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

6. ORAJ.Disease Recurrence Indicator
