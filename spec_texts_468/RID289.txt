Report ID: RID289

Report Title:
GVHD forms gaps and overlaps

Description:
This report lists subjects with gaps or overlaps in Stage/Grade Onset and Stop Dates.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. GVHD Seq

5. GVHD form

6. Previous GVHD Seq

7. Previous GVHD Grade/Stage Onset Date

8. Previous GVHD Grade/Stage Stop Date

9. Current GVHD Seq

10. Current GVHD Grade/Stage Onset Date

11. Current GVHD Grade/Stage Stop Date

12. Scenario

Logic:
This report lists subjects with the following conditions:

1) Add entry GVHD Grade/Stage Date gaps. Gap: When a day is not contained on the sequences compared.

Ex:

Seq 1. Onset date: 5 Jan 2018 / Stop Date: 7 Jan 2018.

Seq 2. Onset date: 9 Jan 2018 / Stop Date: 10 Jan 2018.

There is a Gap since the 8 Jan 2018 is not contained in sequences compared.

2) Add entry GVHD Grade/Stage Date overlaps. Overlap: When a day is contained in both sequences compared.

Ex:

Seq 1. Onset date: 5 Jan 2018 / Stop Date: 7 Jan 2018.

Seq 2. Onset date: 7 Jan 2018 / Stop Date: 10 Jan 2018.

There is an overlap since the 7 Jan 2018 is contained in both sequences compared.

There is no an overlap and no a gap when:

Ex:

Seq 1. Onset date: 5 Jan 2018 / Stop Date: 6 Jan 2018.

Seq 2. Onset date: 7 Jan 2018 / Stop Date: 10 Jan 2018.

Seq 3. Onset date: 11 Jan 2018 / Stop Date: 12 Jan 2018.

Note 1:

For OAGVH the item dates are: GVHD Grade Onset Date and GVHD Grade Stop Date

For AOGVH the item dates are: GVHD Stage Onset Date and GVHD Stage Stop Date

Note 2:

Report should not display deleted records.

Note 3:

The date comparison is between same record.

Report Scenarios:
A=Yes

Add entry GVHD Grade/Stage Date gaps.

B= Yes

Add entry GVHD Grade/Stage Date overlaps.

C= No

Add entry GVHD Grade/Stage Date no gaps.

D= No

Add entry GVHD Grade/Stage Date no overlaps.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Previous GVHD Grade/Stage Onset Date

Parameter Selection:
Country(ies), Site(s),Subject(s), List of Subject(s), Randomized Only

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assessment.Country

2. Subject Assessment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subject Assessment.Subject Number

4. Sponsor ID Number corresponding to the form used taking into account note 1.

5. Display “OAGVH” or “AOGVH” according to the form used in the output.

6. Previous GVHD Add Entry Seq Formidx (Itemsetidx)

7. Previous GVHD Grade/Stage Onset Date according to the output on logic 1 or 2 and taking into account note 1.

8. Previous GVHD Grade/Stage Stop Date according to the output on logic 1 or 2 and taking into account note 1.

9. Current GVHD Add Entry Seq Formidx (Itemsetidx)

10. Current GVHD Grade/Stage Onset Date according to the output on logic 1 or 2 and taking into account note 1.

11. Current GVHD Grade/Stage Stop Date according to the output on logic 1 or 2 and taking into account note 1.

12.  Show “Scenario 1” if meet conditions in logic 1 or show “Scenario 2” if meet conditions in logic 2.
