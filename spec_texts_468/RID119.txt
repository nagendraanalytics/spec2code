Report ID: RID119

Report Title:
Potential Ghost Patients

Description:
This report displays patients that are determined to be potential ghost patients, which are defined as either:

Table 1: Subject screened/randomized in error with data in the Final Status visit. .

Subjects who have a screening number and may or may not have a date of birth entered,  and may or may not have a randomization number, with data entered in Subject Disposition (DS01) or Protocol Milestone Status (PMS1) or Date of Last Contact (DOLC) and all visits entered for the subject have the Erroneous Visit Checkbox

Table 2: Subject screened and not randomized with no disposition status.

Subjects who have a screening number and may or may not have a date of birth entered, but no corresponding randomization number and  no disposition status on DS01

Table 3: Subject  randomized in error and have Erroneous Visit checkbox marked on all visits  with no disposition status.

Subjects who have a screening number and may or may not have a date of birth entered, have corresponding randomization number and  no disposition status on DS01

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Screening Number

4. Subject Number

5. Date of Birth

6. Error message

Logic:
Logic 1:

Screening Number <> Null

And

Treatment/Randomization Number = Null

And

DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study = Null

Or

Logic 2:

Treatment/Randomization Number <> Null

And

Erroneous Visit for ALL initialed Visits are completed

And

DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study = Null

Or

Logic3:

Screening Number <> Null

And

Erroneous Visit for ALL initialed Visits are completed

And

DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Null OR

DOLC. Date of Last Contac <> Null OR

PMS1. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment <> Null

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=Yes

Subject who have a screening number

And without Randomization number and no DS01 was completed

2) Output=Yes

Subject who have randomization number

And Erroneous visit checkbox selected on all visits and no final status in DS01

3) Output=Yes

Subject who have a screening number

And Erroneous visit checkbox selected on all visits

And data entered in Subject Disposition (DS01) or Protocol Milestone Status (PMS1) or Date of Last Contact (DOLC)

4) Output=No

Subject who have a screening number

And without Randomization number and DS01 was completed

5) Output=No

Subject who have a screening number

And also have Randomization number and no study status DS01 was completed

6) Output=No

Subject who have a screening number

And also have Randomization number and DS01 was completed

7) Output=No

Subject who have randomization number

And Erroneous visit checkbox is not selected for ALL visits and have final status in DS01

Sorting:
Country, Site Number and Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assigment Country

2. Subject Assignment Site Mnemonic

3. Subject Assignment.Screening Number

4. Subject Assignment.Subject Number

5. Demographics. Date ~ DOB

6. Display “Potential ghost patient” when meet Logic 1 and Logic 2; display “DS01/DOLC/PMS1 completed for potential ghost patient” when meet logic 3
