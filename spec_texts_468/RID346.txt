Report ID: RID346

Report Title:
Missing last line of therapy entered at ODB with reason of discontinuation specified per protocol.

Description:
This report lists subjects whose last Line of therapy entered on the oncologic drugs and biologics form (ODB/ODBH), is not the one specified per protocol.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. ODB Start Date

5. ODB Stop Date

6. Reason for Discontinuation

Logic:
Logic 1: ODB.STOP DATE OF MEDICATION /STOP DATE OF REGIMEN  = ODB. Max STOP DATE OF MEDICATION /STOP DATE OF REGIMEN AND Logic 2: ODB.Reason <> Value selected in "Reason for Discontinuation" parameter

Note 1: ODB.STOP DATE OF MEDICATION /STOP DATE OF REGIMEN: use Imputed Stop Date of Regimen/ Maximum Imputed Stop Date of Medication

Note 2: ODB.Max Stop Date: Maximum of ODB.Stop Date (clarified in Note 2) prior Date of Randomization in SR/SRIVR. If the study does not have SR/SRIVR form then the report will not consider the date of randomization.

Note 3: Using system date if ODB.Stop Date is blank

Note 4: ODB.Reason: REASON FOR DISCONTINUATION FROM THIS REGIMEN

Note 5: For "Reason for Discontinuation" parameter: if All is selected: if PD nested radio button is available we do not expect an output when ODB.Reason = Progression of Disease By Imaging OR Toxicity, if PD nested radio button is not available we do not expect an output when ODB.Reason = Progressive Disease OR Toxicity. if one of the other option is selected then we do not expect an output only when ODB.Reason = option selected.

Note 6: Progression of Disease By Imaging is considered when radio button of Progression of Disease is selected and also nested radio button By Imaging is selected.

Note 7: For trials that use both Stop Date of Regimen and Stop Date of Medication items in ODB use imputed Stop date of regimen                                                                                                                                                                                   Note 8: This report is using the Global Supp Doc, items STOP DATE OF MEDICATION /STOP DATE OF REGIMEN and REASON FOR DISCONTINUATION FROM THIS REGIMEN  are from ODB global Supp Doc.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1: Yes

ODB. Reason for discontinuation from most recent course/Reason for Discontinuation from This Regimen <> Progression of Disease By Imaging OR Toxicity OR Progressive Disease. AND Max date ODB.Start Date of Medication/ODB.Start Date of Regimen or Stop Date of Medication.

Scenario 2: No

ODB.Reason for discontinuation from most recent course/Reason for Discontinuation from This Regimen = Progression of Disease By Imaging OR Toxicity OR Progressive Disease. AND Max date ODB.Start Date of Medication/ODB.Start Date of Regimen or ODB.Stop Date of Regimen.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized only (N/Y, default as N)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Country

2. SA.Site

3. SA.Subject Number

4. ODB.START DATE OF MEDICATION/ODB.START DATE OF REGIMEN

5. ODB.STOP DATE OF MEDICATION /ODB.STOP DATE OF REGIMEN

6. ODB.REASON FOR DISCONTINUATION FROM THIS REGIMEN. display the reason for discontinuation for the max ODB date.
