Report ID: RID453

Report Title:
Missing comment for reason for dose variation as Other

Description:
This report will output subjects that have "other" as reason for dose variation but a bubble comment is not provided

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. STR Visit

5. STR Sequence

Logic:
This report displays STR records with Reason for Dose Variation="Other" and there is no item-level comment on Reason for dose variaiton Item.

Item-level comments on other items or form-level comments are not considered.

Deleted records should not to be considered.

Records in erroneous visits should not be considered.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=YES

STR Reason for Dose Variation="Other" and

No comment entered on this record

2) Output=NO

STR Reason for Dose Variation="Other" and

There is at least one comment on this record

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Visit Mnemonic

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, List of subjects

Form.Item Names:
1. SA Form. Site Country

2. SA Form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA Form. Subject Number

4. STR. Visit Mnemonic

5. STR. Sponsor ID Number
