Report ID: RID353

Report Title:
Status of Disease at screening reconciliation

Description:
This report output subjects who selected relapsed as the Status of Disease at Screening however there are no previous treatment for primary diagnosis selected.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject number

4. Relapse Form

5. Status of Disease at Screening

Logic:
[For ODD/CDDU/CDDB/CDDH: Status of Disease at Screening = Relapse

OR

CDDHN: Current Disease Status = Relapsed

OR

CDDHN. Disease Presentation Type = {Recurrent OR Recurrent and Metastatic}]

AND

[OS is not initialized, or ORAD is not initialized, or ODB/ODBH/ODBHN is not initialized

OR

all records in OS, ORAD, and ODB/ODBH/ODBHN are deleted]

Note 1: Please exclude deleted ODD/CDDU/CDDB/CDDH records from programming.

Note 2: Please show output when for a single patient, ALL of the forms are missing, if 1 is present please don’t show mismatch.

Note 3: Please take into account that a trial will only have 1 oncologic drugs and biologics module, not all listed in the spec.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A/Yes:

For ODD/CDDU/CDDB/CDDH: Status of Disease at Screening = Relapse OR CDDHN: Current Disease Status = Relapsed OR CDDHN. Disease Presentation Type = {Recurrent OR Recurrent and Metastatic}]; however, the treatment forms OS/ORAD/ODB/ODBH/ODBHN are Not initialized

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1. SA form. Country

2. SA form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA form. Subject number

4. ODD/CDDU/CDDB/CDDH/CDDHN Form. formmnemonic

5. ODD/CDDU/CDDB/CDDH Form. Status of Disease at Screening OR CDDHN Form. Current Disease Status OR CDDHN. Disease Presentation Type
