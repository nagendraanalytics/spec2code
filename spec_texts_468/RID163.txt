Report ID: RID163

Report Title:
Procedure vs. AE

Description:
The report displays subjects who have Procedures due to an SAE or ECI, but are missing an SAE or ECI record.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Procedure Seq

5. Procedure Term

6. Associated AE Seq

Logic:
The report should display output if a subject has a Procedure on the PROC eCRF with the questions "Is this Procedure Related to an SAE?" or "Is this Procedure Related to an SAE or ECI?" is selected and none of the "Associate AE Seq Number(s)" on the PROC eCRF matches with any AE sequence number that is an SAE or ECI on the AE eCRF.

An AE is considered an SAE if any of the 8 serious AE criteria is selected.

An AE is considered an ECI if the question "Is the AE an Event of Clinical Interest?"=Yes.

Report should not display deleted records.

Report Scenarios:
A-Yes

A Procedure on the PROC eCRF with the questions "Is this Procedure Related to an SAE?" or "Is this Procedure Related to an SAE or ECI?" marked Yes and none of the "Associate AE Seq Number(s)" on the PROC eCRF matches with any AE sequence number that is an SAE or ECI in the AE eCRF.

B-No

A Procedure on the PROC eCRF with the questions "Is this Procedure Related to an SAE?" or "Is this Procedure Related to an SAE or ECI?" marked Yes and at least one of the "Associate AE Seq Number(s)" on the PROC eCRF matches with any AE sequence number that is an SAE or ECI in the AE eCRF.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Procedure Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject, Randomized Only, List of Subjects
