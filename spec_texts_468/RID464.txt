Report ID: RID464

Report Title:
Missing DEATH form when Vital Status is Dead

Description:
This report will output subjects that have the item Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject completed as Dead but DEATH form is missing or OEDRA is missing and the subject is not in Survival Follow up Phase.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. VST seq

5. VST Date of Contact

6. Subject Status

7.Scenario

Logic:
Logic 1:

VST. Date of Contact <> NULL AND VST. Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject = [Dead]

AND DEATH. Date/Time of Death = NULL

Logic 2:

VST. Date of Contact <> NULL AND VST. Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject = [Dead] AND SFU.Date of Contact = NULL AND There is not at least one OEDRA with Date of Assessment

Note

Note 1: Do not output for deleted records or erroneous visits

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1

– Yes

VST. Date of Contact <> NULL AND VST. Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject = [Dead] AND DEATH. Date/Time of Death = NULL

– No

VST. Date of Contact <> NULL AND VST. Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject = [Dead] AND DEATH. Date/Time of Death <> NULL

– No

VST.Date of contact = NULL AND DEATH. Date/Time of Death = NULL

Scenario 2

– Yes

VST. Date of Contact <> NULL AND VST. Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject = [Dead] AND SFU. Date of Contact = NULL AND OEDRA.Date of Assessment = NULL

– No

VST. Date of Contact <> NULL AND VST. Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject = [Dead] AND SFU. Date of Contact = NULL AND OEDRA.Date of Assessment <> NULL

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (Y/N set by default to Y)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Country

2. SA.Site

3. SA.Subject number

4. VST.Sponsor ID Number

5. VST.Date of Contact

6. VST. Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject

7. Please display "Scenario 1" if logic 1 is fulfilled or "Scenario 2" if logic 2 is fulfilled.
