Report ID: RID225

Report Title:
Date or Date/Time Comparison Report

Description:
Date/Date Time on first eCRF compared with a specified window from the Time Range or Date/Date Time on the second eCRF.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Form (eCRF 1)

5. Visit (eCRF 1)

6. Seq# (eCRF 1)

7. Date or Date/time (eCRF 1)

8. Date or Date/time Value (eCRF 1)

9. Form (eCRF 2)

10. Visit (eCRF 2)

11. Seq# (eCRF 2)

12. Symbol 1

13. Date or Datetime 1 (eCRF 2)

14. Date or Datetime 1 Value (eCRF 2)

15. Window 1

16. Operator

17. Symbol 2

18. Date or Date/time 2 (eCRF 2)

19. Date or Date/time 2 Value (eCRF 2)

20. Window 2

21. Unit

22. Error Message

Note 9 : Items 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 14, 19 are derived from Inform database, if item 6 and item 11 does not sequence number it will display as 1.

Logic:
Scenario A:

Form 1 Date Item 1 at Form 1 Visit <SYMBOL1> Form 2 Date Item 1 at Form 2 Visit + Window 1 Unit

AND (this is the operator)

Form 1 Date Item 1 at Form 1 Visit <SYMBOL2> Form 2 Date Item 2 at Form 2 Visit + Window 2 Unit

means output is produced when

"Date/Date Time on first eCRF at Form 1 Visit is within a specified window from the Time Range on the second eCRF at Form 2 Visit (as specified in the supporting document and all the conditional items meet the criteria)"

Scenario B:

NOT

[Form 1 Date Item 1 at Form 1 Visit <SYMBOL1> Form 2 Date Item 1 at  Form 2 Visit + Window 1 Unit

AND (this is the operator)

Form 1 Date Item 1 at Form 1 Visit <SYMBOL2> Form 2 Date Item 2 at Form 2 Visit + Window 2 Unit]

means output is produced when

"Date/Date Time on first eCRF at Form 1 Visit is not within a specified window from the Time Range on the second eCRF at Form 2 Visit (as specified in the supporting document and all the conditional items meet the criteria)"

Scenario C:

Form 1 Date Item 1 at Form 1 Visit <SYMBOL1> Form 2 Date Item 1 at Form 2 Visit + Window 1 Unit

Operator is NA

means output is produced when

"Date/Date Time on first eCRF at Form 1 Visit meet a specified time point compared with Date/Date Time on the second eCRF at Form 2 Visit (as specified in the supporting document and all the conditional items meet the criteria)"

Note 1: If unit is Day, logic will check Date only, no matter it has time or not.

Note 2: If any of Date Item has UNK Time, logic will default the time to 00:00; if any of Date Item has UNK Day, logic will default the Date to 1st of the month; if any of Date Item has UNK Month, logic will default the Date and Month to 1/Jan.

Note 3: For scenario A, SYMBOL is limited to <, >, <=, >=. For scenario B, SYMBOL1 is limited to <, <=; SYMBOL2 is limited to >, >=. For scenario C, SYMBOL is limited to =, <, >, <=, >= and <>.

Note 4: For scenario C, if the Form 1 and form 2 are same eCRF and located in same visit, please reconcile the date in same eCRF seq.

Note 1 to Note 4 are for the report logic.

Note 5: For the condition and target criteria that an ITEM is not missing, please put 'NOT' for SYMBOL and 'MISSING' for RESULT.

Note 6: For the condition and target criteria that an ITEM is missing, please put 'IS' for SYMBOL and 'MISSING' for RESULT.

Note 7: If Form 1 Date Item 1, Form 2 Date Item 1, Form 2 Date Item 2 are different date items from the same table, eCRF and visit, just compare the date within the same record, no need cross check.

Note 8:  Please do not contain double quotes in error message.

Note 5 to Note 8 are for the supporting document.
