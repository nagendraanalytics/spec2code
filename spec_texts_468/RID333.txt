Report ID: RID333

Report Title:
Overall response vs lesion Response

Description:
This report will list subjects who have inconsistencies reported between irRECIST New Lesions and Prior irRECIST new lesions  in LSRIR and RRIR

Column Heading:
1.	Country

2.	Site

3.	Subject Number

4.	RRIR Seq#

5.	Date Response Determined

6.	Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline

8.	LSRIR Seq#

8. 	Date of Assessment /Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

9.	Tumor Identification

10. 	Scenario

Logic:
Report will check LSRIR against RRIR Form irRECIST section

Scenario 1

Logic 1 RRIR Form. Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline = Not applicable AND there is a LSRIR form. Tumor Identification = New Lesion where LSRIR form. Date of Assessment/Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)  < RRIR form. Date Response Determined

Code as Scenario 1

Scenario 2

Logic 2 RRIR Form. Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline <> Not applicable AND There is not any LSRIR form. Tumor Identification = New Lesion where LSRIR form. Date of Assessment/Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)  < RRIR form. Date Response Determined

Code as Scenario 2

Note 1 Dont include records deleted on LSRIR and RRIR

Note 2 On Scenario 1 if there are more than one LSRIR entry that meet conditions then display them all bundle if meet the next rules.

Note 3 For Scenario 2 Exclude Null value on programing to evaluate Logic 2 on item Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline <> Not applicable

Date of Assessment or Date of Initial Lesion Identification equal and Tumor Identification equal and Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1 - Yes On RRIR Form Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline = Not applicable AND There ARE entries on LSRIR form where LSRIR form.Tumor Identification = New Lesion AND LSRIR form.Date of Assessment/Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) < RRIR form. Date Response Determined

Scenario 2 - Yes On RRIR Form. Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline <> Not applicable AND There ARE NOT entries on LSRIR identified where LSRIR form. Tumor Identification = New Lesion AND Date of Assessment/Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) < RRIR form. Date Response Determined

Scenario 3  –  No  On RRIR Form. Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline = Not applicable AND there ARE NOT any entry on LSRIR form where LSRIR form.Tumor Identification = New Lesion AND LSRIR form.Date of Assessment/Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) < RRIR form. Date Response Determined

Scenario 4  – No On RRIR Form. Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline <> Not applicable AND There ARE entries on LSRIR identified where LSRIR form. Tumor Identification = New Lesion AND Date of Assessment/Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) < RRIR form. Date Response Determined

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number,RRIR items,LSRIR items,Scenario.

Parameter Selection:
