Report ID: RID259

Report Title:
IMDC Risk Evaluation VS Prognosis Factors

Description:
The report will identify IMDC risk factor evaluation incorrectly selected according to prognosis risk factors.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. IMDC Risk Category

5. Prognostic Factors

6. Scenario

Logic:
Logic 1.1 If DDRCC.IMDC Risk Category = Favorable (No risk factors)

AND

Logic 1.2 DDRCC.Prognostic Factors.None = Null

OR

1 or more of the following options <> NULL: Baseline Karnofsky Performance Status < 80%;  Interval Between the Date of Initial Diagnosis of RCC to Date of Randomization In This Study < 1year; Baseline Hemoglobin < Lower Limit of Normal;

Baseline Platelet Count > Upper Limit of Normal; Baseline Corrected Calcium > Upper Limit of Normal; Baseline Neutrophil > Upper Limit of Normal

OR

Logic 2.1 If DDRCC.IMDC Risk Category = Intermediate (1 or 2 risk factors)

AND

Logic 2.2 DDRCC.None <> Null

OR

3 or more of the following options <> NULL: Baseline Karnofsky Performance Status < 80%; Interval Between the Date of Initial Diagnosis of RCC to Date of Randomization In This Study < 1year; Baseline Hemoglobin < Lower Limit of Normal; Baseline Platelet Count > Upper Limit of Normal; Baseline Corrected Calcium > Upper Limit of Normal; Baseline Neutrophil > Upper Limit of Normal

OR

Logic 3.1If DDRCC.IMDC Risk Category = Poor (3 or more risk factors))

AND

Logic 3.2 DDRCC.Prognostic Factors.None <> Null

OR

1 or 2 of the following options <> NULL: Baseline Karnofsky Performance Status < 80%; Interval Between the Date of Initial Diagnosis of RCC to Date of Randomization In This Study < 1year; Baseline Hemoglobin < Lower Limit of Normal; Baseline Platelet Count > Upper Limit of Normal; Baseline Corrected Calcium > Upper Limit of Normal; Baseline Neutrophil > Upper Limit of Normal

Scenario 2:

Logic 4 Interval Between the Date of Initial Diagnosis of RCC to Date of Randomization In This Study < 1year <> NULL and subject is not randomized.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1

A output = No

IMDC Risk Category = Favorable (No risk factors) and Prognostic Factors = None OR IMDC Risk Category = Intermediate (1 or 2 risk factors) and Prognostic Factors = 1 or 2 choices selected OR IMDC Risk Category = Poor (3 or more risk factors)

 and Prognostic Factors = 3 or more choices are selected

B output = Yes

IMDC Risk Category = Favorable (No risk factors) and Prognostic Factors <> None OR IMDC Risk Category = Intermediate (1 or 2 risk factors) and Prognostic Factors = None OR IMDC Risk Category = Intermediate (1 or 2 risk factors) and Prognostic Factors = 3 or more options are selected  OR IMDC Risk Category = Poor (3 or more risk factors) and Prognostic Factors = None OR IMDC Risk Category = Poor (3 or more risk factors) and Prognostic Factors = 2 or less options are selected

Scenario 2

A output = No

Prognostic Factors = Interval Between the Date of Initial Diagnosis of RCC to Date of Randomization In This Study < 1year and subject is randomized

B output = Yes

Prognostic Factors = Interval Between the Date of Initial Diagnosis of RCC to Date of Randomization In This Study < 1year and subject is not randomized

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, IMDC Risk Category, Prognostic Factors, Scenario

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, List of Subjects, Randomized only

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment form.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. Display "Favorable (No risk factors)" if Favorable (No risk factors) <> NULL; Display "Intermediate (1 or 2 risk factors)" if Intermediate (1 or 2 risk factors) <> NULL; Display "Poor (3 or more risk factors)" if Poor (3 or more risk factors) <> NULL

5. Display the number of options selected in DDRCC.Prognostic Factors

6. Display "Scenario 1" if logic 1, logic 2 or logic 3 is met; Display "Scenario 2" if logic 4 is met
