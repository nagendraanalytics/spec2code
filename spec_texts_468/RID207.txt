Report ID: RID207

Report Title:
Number of records per form per visit

Description:
Report lists patients that has not the correct number of records per form per visit or when the date of assessment does not match with the corresponded Visit Date.

Column Heading:
Scenario 1

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. DOV

6. Form name

7. Date field name

8. Condition within form

9. Total number

10. Acceptable number

Scenario 2

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. DOV

6. Form name

7. Seq#

8. Date field Name

9. Date

10. Condition within form

Logic:
According to the values in the ““Supporting document””:

<Criteria Form1>.<Criteria Itemquestion1> = <Criteria Value1>  AND

<Criteria Form2>.<Criteria Itemquestion2> = <Criteria Value2> AND

<Criteria Form3>.<Criteria Itemquestion3> = <Criteria Value3> AND

Report output subjects who meet the following criteria.

Scenario 1

The number of records for the specified form per visit and any condition/restriction within the form as applicable<> specified acceptable number of records for specific groups.

User provides information:

Provide 1.) group information 2. ) the acceptable number of records per form per visit and any condition/restriction within the form as applicable for each specific group

<Group><Form> <Date> <Condition within the form> <number of record allowed>

Eg:

SR- Cohort-Cohort A

1-PK04A --Date and Time Samples Obtained- Scheduled Time-2

1-HDA --Date of Assessment-Lesion Category-2

2-SR- Cohort-Cohort B

2-PK04A --Date and Time Samples Obtained-Scheduled Time-2

2-VB --Date/Time of Assessment -1

Scenario 2

Date of Assessment (Date/Time of Assessment) on the specified form (only consider a date in programming)<> the current date of visit or out of acceptable days range from DOV (date margin is inclusive) for specific groups.

User provides information:

Provide the group information（it is not applicable to Scenario 2)

Provide the acceptable days range from DOV

Provide the repeating forms to include in the review with the specific Date to be included and the Condition if applicable

<Form> <Date> <Condition within the form>

Eg:

SR- Cohort-Cohort A

HDA --Date of Assessment - 3

PK04A --Date and Time Samples Obtained

SR- Cohort-Cohort B

PK04A --Date and Time Samples Obtained-Scheduled Time

VB --Date/Time of Assessment -3

Deleted records and Erroneous visits will not be considered in programming and logic.

Notes:

Missing records should only be considered when the form has been initialized. The forms that have not been initialized should be removed, i.e. do not have at least one record at the given visit.

Column ‘Condition within form’ for two tables should be masked as ‘xxx’ if the form is masked as a blinded table in the supp doc (forms Blinded Flag=Y)

The form can be selected in the parameter, please enter form names with commas separated; If not any form name is provided in the prompt (i.e. leave blank), then it runs for all forms present in the supporting document.

1 row for each group of the acceptable number of records per form per visit and any condition/restriction within the form as applicable.

Condition is just used to check the duplicate scenario. If more than one condition is applied to duplicate scheduled time, i.e. Scheduled time is duplicated per the same study medication and study period, then all those rows for the same scheduled time for specific visits should have the same Group Number. Otherwise, each form and scenario for specific visits has a unique Group Number.

For Scenario 1, take into account the number of records listed in the supporting document regardless of the DOV of the visit

For Scenario 2, If the form Date of visit is within at least one of the windows listed in the supporting document no output is required. (Date not fulfill the ranges provided within the group, output is required)

1) Group - If more than 1 row could be considered as one group in supp doc, as long as meet one of them, it means it's satisfied with the logic and would not be outputted.
