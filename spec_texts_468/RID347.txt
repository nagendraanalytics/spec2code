Report ID: RID347

Report Title:
Prior treatments best response and duration of response are inconsistent.

Description:
Duration of Response for prior oncologic treatments cannot be reported for failed treatments, it is only required for positive responses: Complete Remission and Partial Response.

This report will also list patients for which Complete Remission or Partial Response were reported, however the Duration of Response is missing.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject number

4. ODB Visit

5. ODB Sequence Number

6. ODB Best Response

7. ODB Duration of Response

8. Error Message

Logic:
LOGIC 1.

ODB Best Response =  Progressive Disease (PD)

AND

Duration of Response = non-zero.

LOGIC 2

ODB Best Response = Stable Disease (SD)

AND

Duration of Response = non-zero.

LOGIC 3

ODB Best Response = Complete Remission OR Partial Response

AND

Duration of Response is missing.

Note 1: Please exclude deleted records from programming

Note 2: For Duration of Response = missing, please show "blank" message in Column 6 in the report.

Report Scenarios:
A. Yes

ODB "Best Response" is equal to Progressive Disease (PD) AND "Duration of Response" is non-zero.

B. Yes

ODB "Best Response" is equal to Stable Disease (SD) AND "Duration of Response" is non-zero.

C. Yes

ODB "Best Response" is equal to Complete Remission OR Partial Response AND Duration of Response is missing.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Form.Item Names:
1. SA form. Country

2. SA form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA form. Subject number

4. ODB form. Visit

5. ODB form. Sponsor ID Number

6. ODB form. Best Response

7. ODB form. Duration of  Response

8. Scenario A or B or C
