Report ID: RID237

Report Title:
Continuing Adverse Events at the time of Second Course of Treatment

Description:
This report displays subjects who have started the Second Course phase and have continuing AEs with a toxicity grade greater than the one specified in the protocol and an onset date previous to Cycle 1 Course 2 DOV.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Cycle 1 Course 2 DOV

5. AE Seq

6. AE Term

7. AE Onset Date

8. AE Toxicity Grade

9. AE End Date

Logic:
Report should output subjects who meet the following criteria:

Logic1: Cycle 1 Course 2 visit DOV <> null

and

Logic2: AE form. toxicity grade > X

and

Logic3: Toxicity Grade Onset Date < Cycle 1 Course 2 visit DOV

and

(

Logic4: Toxicity Grade Stop Date = blank

or

Logic5: Toxicity Grade Stop Date >= Cycle 1 Course 2 visit DOV

)

Note:

1. Deleted record and Erroneous visit should be excluded from programming.

2. For AE end date and time, only consider date in the programming.

3. X is the value selected in "Toxicity Grade" prompt

4. Logic 4 and Logic 5 should be reviewed only for the same add entry of Logic 2

5. Logic 4 is specified as Toxicity Grade Stop Date is blank, please consider the same in scenario B; Logic 5 is specified as Toxicity Grade Stop Date is after or equal to Cycle 1 Course 2 visit DOV, please consider the same in scenario A

Report Scenarios:
A‐ Yes

Subjects have started the Second Course phase but have AEs with a grade > X and Toxicity Grade Onset Date < Cycle 1 Course 2 visit DOV <= Toxicity Grade Stop Date

B‐ Yes

Subjects have started the Second Course phase but have AEs with a grade > X and AE Toxicity Grade Onset Date < Cycle 1 Course 2 DOV and Toxicity Grade Stop Date is ongoing

C‐ No

AE toxicity grade < = X

D‐ No

Cycle 1 Course 2 visit is not initialized

E‐No

Toxicity Grade Stop Date <> ongoing or Toxicity Grade Stop Date < Cycle 1 Course 2visit DOV

F‐No

Toxicity Grade Onset Date >= Cycle 1 Course 2 DOV

G‐ No

deleted data or erroneous visit

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, AE Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, Randomized Only, List of Subjects, Toxicity Grade(5 Radio buttons: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment Form.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment Form.Site Mnemonic

3. Subject Assignment Form.Subject Number

4. Date of Visit ( DOV). Display the DOV of Cycle 1 Course 2 Visit

5. ADVERSE EVENTS (AE).Sequence Number (From top of AE form)

6. ADVERSE EVENTS (AE).AE Term (From top of AE form)

7. ADVERSE EVENTS (AE).Toxicity Grade Onset Date (From Add entry section of AE form)

8. ADVERSE EVENTS (AE).Toxicity Grade (From Add entry section of AE form)

9. ADVERSE EVENTS (AE).Toxicity Grade Stop Date (only display date) (From Add entry section of AE form)
