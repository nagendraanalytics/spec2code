Report ID: RID030

Report Title:
Adverse Event and Other Suspect Therapy Reconciliation

Description:
This report reconciles Adverse event with AEOST Form.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. AEOST Seq

5. AEOST Assoc AE Seq

6. AE Seq

7. AE Relationship

Logic:
This report outputs subjects with Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form not consistent with the Sequence Number on the AE form.

The scenarios are:

1) Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form does not exist on the AE form.

2) Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form is equal to the Sequence Number on the AE form, but the AE Relationship of event to any Other Suspect Therapy?=NOT RELATED or blank

3) AE Relationship of event to any Other Suspect Therapy?=RELATED but the AE Sequence Number has not been captured on any Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form

Deleted records should not be considered.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=NO

Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form = Sequence Number on the AE form and

AE Relationship of event to any Other Suspect Therapy?=RELATED

2) Output=YES

Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form does not exist on the AE form.

3) Output=YES

Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form = Sequence Number on the AE form and

AE Relationship of event to any Other Suspect Therapy?=NOT RELATED or blank

4) Output=YES

AE Relationship of event to any Other Suspect Therapy?=RELATED and

there is no Associated AE Sequence Number on the AEOST form = AE Sequence Number

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, AEOST Seq, AE Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Randomized Only, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. OTHER SUSPECT THERAPY form.formidx

5. OTHER SUSPECT THERAPY form.Associated AE Sequence Number

6. ADVERSE EVENTS form.formidx

7. ADVERSE EVENTS form.Relationship of event to any Other Suspect Therapy?
