Report ID: RID127

Report Title:
Listing of Local Lab Data with Normal Ranges

Description:
This report displays subject level data from local lab (CLAB, HLAB, OLAB) eCRFs and determines the normal ranges for each test from the Site-level Normal Range eCRF (SLCNR, SLHNR, SLONR) based on the laboratory name, test, collection date, age at collection, sex and the normal range effective date. The report output will be used to enter data into the subject level local lab normal range eCRFs. This listing will also be used by Clinical Scientists to review Local Lab data.

Logic:
Determine the age at collection date for each subject on the local lab eCRF from Date of Birth on the Demographics eCRF.

For each subject on the local lab eCRF, match each site, test, specimen type (where applicable), laboratory name, age at collection, units and sex with site, test, specimen type, laboratory name, age range, units, sex, and closest effective date on the site level local lab normal range eCRF.

Closest normal range effective date would be the greatest date that is prior or equal to the collection date of the lab test.

If a match is not found for the local lab test, leave the normal range fields blank. Text comparison should not be case sensitive.

Only records with non-null numeric result or units will be considered.

Each HLAB and CLAB test is a field on the local lab eCRF, with the field name specifying the test.

The following units need to be standardized to avoid mismatches between forms:

- 'GM' to 'G'

- 'GRAM' to 'G'

- 'MOLE' to 'MOL'

- 'MICRO' to U'

- 'MILLI' to 'M'

- 'UG/L' to 'NG/ML'

Do not include deleted records.

Do not include data from Erroneous Visits.

Report output will be displayed as .csv format.
