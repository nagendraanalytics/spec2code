Report ID: RID024

Report Title:
AE with Action of Reduced/Increased and no reduced/increased SM record

Description:
The report generates a listing of subjects who had AEs that caused them to reduce/increase the dose of study medication but who did not have at least one reduced/increased dose of study medication recorded for the duration of the AE or X days after AE End Date.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq

5. AE Term

6. Study Medication

7. AE Onset Date

8. AE End Date

9. AE Action Taken

Logic:
Logic 1: AE Form. Action Taken with Study Medication [x] = Reduced

AND

Logic 2: There is not at least one none zero Study Medication[x] with reduced dose within AE duration or [X] days after AE end date.

OR

Logic 3: AE Form. Action Taken with Study Medication [x] = Increased

AND

Logic4: There is not at least one Study Medication[x] with increased dose within AE duration or [X] days after AE end date.

Notes:

1. Requires supporting document to be named AE036_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1) Group - Unique key for group of SM records, NOT NULL. If two or more dose amount for same SM is acceptable, then should be considered as one group.

2) SM Form - SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU

3) Study Medication - Value for SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU Study Medication

4) SM Dosage: Protocol Dose Level/ Number of unit taken/ Intended Dose/Dosage Prescribed from corresponding ecrf to indicate the dosage.

5) Protocol expected dose amount: provide the expected dose amount that defined in protocol

6) Window of days – required number of days allowed since AE stop date to SM start date with dose reduction/increase (e.g., duration of the cycle)

2. Check for reduced/increased SM/SMAB/SMAU/SMPU and SM Start Date >= AE Onset date and SM Start Date <=  X days after AE End date or AE is ongoing;

Check for reduced/increased STR Start Date >= AE Onset data and SM Start Date <=  X days after AE End date or AE is ongoing.

3. If two dose amounts are provided by protocol. for example, Methdadone 5 and Methadone 10. The actual dose amount is less than 5 should be considered as "Reduced" and greater than 10 should be considered as "Increased".

4. For studies with single study medication, Overall Action Taken is checked and a match on Study Medication name is not performed. Also, studies with single study medication will not display Study Medication in the report output.

5a: If SM Label Description and SMAU/SMAB/SMPU Dose Number are exactly matched within forms. For SM/SMAU/SMAB Study Medication and for SMPU.Label Description use SM.Label Description/SMAU.Dose Number/SMAB.Dose Number/SMPU.Dose Number directly

5b: If SM Label Description and SMAU/SMAB/SMPU Dose Number are not exactly matched with Study Medication or SMPU.Label Description, using the optional supporting doc study_med_match_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv to define the mapping.

1 row for each Label Description or Dose Number present in SM/SMAU/SMAB/SMPU eCRFs.

2. Study Medication – Value of Study Medication

3. Matching Item – SM Label Description or SMAU/SMAB/SMPU Dose Number

5. If the time component of AE or the corresponding SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU is UNK, only consider the dates.

6. [x] ranges from 1 to 10.

7. Exclude deleted data and erroneous visit from programming

8. If "Randomized only" parameter selection checkbox is checked, the report should only output subjects with Randomization number that is not null.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=YES

AE Action Taken with Study Medication[x] = Reduced/Increased during start date and end date/ongoing/null and SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU for Study Medication[x] with constant dose during the AE timeframe or [X] days after AE end date

2) Output=YES

AE Action Taken with Study Medication[x] = Reduced during start date and end date/ongoing/null and SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU for Study Medication[x] with increasing dose during the AE timeframe or [X] days after AE end date

3) Output=YES

AE Action Taken with Study Medication[x] = Increased during start date and end date/ongoing/null and SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU for Study Medication[x] with decreasing dose during the AE timeframe or [X] days after AE end date.

4) Output=YES

AE Action Taken with Study Medication[x] = Increased during start date and end date/ongoing/null and No SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU for Study Medication[x] during the AE timeframe or [X] days after AE end date

5) Output=NO

AE Action Taken with Study Medication[x] = Reduced during start date and end date/ongoing/null and SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU for Study Medication[x] with at least one decreasing dose during the AE timeframe or [X] days after AE end date

6) Output=NO

AE Action Taken with Study Medication[x] = Increased during start date and end date/ongoing/null and SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU for Study Medication[x] with at least one increasing dose during the AE timeframe or [X] days after AE end date

7) Output=YES

AE Action Taken with Study Medication[x] = Reduced during start date and end date/ongoing/null and No SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR/SMPU for Study Medication[x] with at least one none zero dose during the AE timeframe or [X] days after AE end date
