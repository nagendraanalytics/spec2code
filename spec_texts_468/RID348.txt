Report ID: RID348

Report Title:
Incorrect/Missing Line of Therapy recorded

Description:
This report outputs subjects who have XL or XL of therapy recorded on ODB OR have missing XL of therapy as stated per protocol.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Stratification

5. ODB form

6. Seq #

7. Line of Therapy / Treatment Category

8. Scenario

Logic:
Logic 1: ‘ODB form’. ‘Line/Line of therapy’ = Line of Therapy or Treatment Category – not allowed treatments prompt

OR ‘ODB form’.’Treatment Category’ = Sub Line of Therapy or Treatment Category – not allowed treatments prompt

Logic 2: ‘ODB form’. ‘Line/Line of therapy’ = Line of Therapy or Treatment Category - allowed treatments prompt is missing

OR ‘ODB form’. ’Treatment Category’ = Sub Line of Therapy or Treatment Category - allowed treatments prompt is missing

Logic 3: ‘Criteria Form’, ‘Criteria item’and ‘Criteria Value’ are met.

Note 1: Do not take into account deleted rows

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1, Output: Yes

Subjects who have entered X ‘Line/Line of therapy’ in ‘ODB form’ as it is not allowed per protocol.

Scenario 2, Output:  No

Subjects who have not entered X ‘Line/Line of therapy’ in ‘ODB form’ as it is not allowed per protocol.

Scenario 3, Output: Yes

Subjects who do not have a record with X ‘Line/Line of therapy’ as required per trial in ‘ODB form’.

Scenario 4, Output: No

Subjects who have a record with X ‘Line/Line of therapy’ as required per trial in ‘ODB form’.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Country

2. SA.Site

3. SA.Subject Number

4. Display Criteria form.Criteria itemquestion provided in Supporting Document. If Not provided display as “NA”.

5. ‘ODB Form’. form mnemonic selected from ODB_supp_doc.csv

6. ‘ODB Form’. Sponsor ID number from “ITEMQUESTION” column on ODB_supp_doc.csv depending on the ODB Form selected.

7. ‘ODB Form’. ‘Line/Line of therapy’ OR   ‘ODB Form’.’Treatment Category’

8. Display: If logic 1 is met "Scenario 1” OR If logic 2 is met " Scenario 2”
