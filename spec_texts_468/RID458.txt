Report ID: RID458

Report Title:
Study medication Beyond irRECIST disease progression

Description:
This report will list subjects who have selected No in the item  The Investigator Believes the Benefit/Risk Assessment Favors Continued Treatment with Study Medication Beyond irRECIST Disease Progression in TBIRP, but there is an studye medication record after TBIRP Date of Assessment.

Column Heading:
COLUMN HEADINGS:

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. TBIRP Visit Mnemonic

5. TBIRP Date of Assessment

6. The Investigator Believes the Benefit/Risk Assessment Favors Continued Treatment with Study Medication Beyond irRECIST Disease Progression

7. SM Visit Mnemonic

8. SM Form

9. SM Seq#

10. SM Start date

Logic:
LOGIC SCENARIO 1 [Output = Yes]

Logic 1: [The Investigator Believes the Benefit/Risk Assessment Favors Continued Treatment with Study Medication Beyond irRECIST Disease Progression] in TBIRP is = No

AND

Logic 2: Date of Assessment in [TBIRP] for record in logic 1< any after Start Date/Time of Study medication in [SMAU or SMAB or SM]

Note 1: Only output of randomized patients

Note 2: Erroneous visit or deleted record will not be considered in the programming or output

Report Scenarios:
Will output if the item [The Investigator Believes the Benefit/Risk Assessment Favors Continued Treatment with Study Medication Beyond irRECIST Disease Progression] in TBIRP is equal to No AND Date of Assessment in [TBIRP]  is before to any after Start Date/Time of Study medication in [SMAU or SMAB or SM] form.

Note 1: Only output of randomized patients

Note 2: Erroneous visit or deleted record will not be considered in the programming or output.

Sorting:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

Country, Site, Subject Number, List of Subjects
