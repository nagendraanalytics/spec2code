Report ID: RID322

Report Title:
SCAN/CTO data reconciles with LSNBC

Description:
This report will display subject that have a inconsistencies between SCAN/CTO and lesion evaluation form LSNBC.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. SCAN Start Date/Time of Procedure

5. SCAN Findings/Results

6. CTO Date of assessment

7. CTO Cystoscopy Result

8. LSNBC Lesion number

9. LSNBC Date of Lesion Evaluation

10. Error Message

Logic:
Report should output subjects who meet the following criteria:

(Scenario 1)

[Logic 1.1: SCAN. ‘Findings/Results’ = ’Abnormal’ AND ‘Abnormal Findings’ = ['Malignant Renal Mass' OR ‘Malignant Bladder Mass’ OR 'Benign Renal Mass' OR 'Benign Bladder Mass'] AND 'Start Date/Time of Procedure' has no corresponding ‘Date of Lesion Evaluation’ in LSNBC

 OR

Logic 1.2: SCAN. ‘Findings/Results’=’Abnormal’ AND ‘Abnormal Findings’= ['Malignant Renal Mass' OR Malignant Bladder Mass' OR 'Benign Renal Mass' OR 'Benign Bladder Mass'] AND LSNBC has not been initialized]

OR

(Scenario 2)

[Logic 2.1: CTO. ‘Cystoscopy Result’ =’Abnormal’ AND ‘Date of Assessment’ has no corresponding ‘Date of Lesion Evaluation’ in LSNBC.

 OR

Logic 2.2: CTO. ‘Cystoscopy Result’ = ’Abnormal’ AND LSNBC has not been initialized]

OR

(Scenario 3)

[Logic 3.1: LSNBC. ‘Date of Lesion Evaluation’ has no corresponding ‘Date of Assessment’ in CTO form OR 'Start Date/Time of Procedure' in SCAN form.

OR

Logic 3.2: LSNBC. ‘Date of Lesion Evaluation’ <> Null

AND CTO form has not been initialized

OR

SCAN form has not been initialized]

OR

(Scenario 4)

[Logic 4.1: LSNBC. 'Was the Lesion Evaluable?' = 'Yes' AND ‘Date of Lesion Evaluation’ has a corresponding 'Start Date/Time of Procedure' in SCAN form AND ‘Findings/Results’ = 'Normal'

OR

Logic 4.2: LSNBC. 'Was the Lesion Evaluable?' = 'Yes' AND ‘Date of Lesion Evaluation’ has a corresponding ‘Date of Assessment’ in CTO form AND ‘Cystoscopy Result' = 'Normal'

Note: Erroneous visits and deleted records should not be considered in the programming and output.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1 Output: Yes

SCAN  form has Findings/Results of 'Abnormal' and Abnormal Findings of 'Malignant Renal Mass' or Malignant Bladder Mass' or 'Benign Renal Mass' or 'Benign Bladder Mass' and the LSNBC does not have a Date of Lesion Evaluation that is the same as the SCAN form Start Date/Time of Procedure

Scenario 2 Output: Yes

CTO form has Abnormal Cystoscopy Result and the LSNBC does not have a Date of Lesion Evaluation that is the same as the CTO form Date of Assessment

Scenario 3 Output: Yes

there is an LSNBC Date of Lesion Evaluation and no corresponding SCAN or CTO form with the same Start date/time of procedure or Assessment.

Scenario 4 Output: Yes

there is a LSNBC (as lesion evaluable) =YES  with  Date of Lesion Evaluation and a corresponding SCAN or CTO form with the same Start date/time of procedure or Assessment and the Findings/Results is Normal.

Scenario 5 Output: No

SCAN  form has Findings/Results of 'Abnormal' and Abnormal Findings of 'Malignant Renal Mass' or Malignant Bladder Mass' or 'Benign Renal Mass' or 'Benign Bladder Mass' and the LSNBC does have a Date of Lesion Evaluation that is the same as the SCAN form Start Date/Time of Procedure

Scenario 6 Output: No

CTO form has Abnormal Cystoscopy Result and the LSNBC does have a Date of Lesion Evaluation that is the same as the CTO form Date of Assessment

Scenario 7 Output: Yes

there is an LSNBC Date of Lesion Evaluation and there is corresponding SCAN or CTO form with the same Start date/time of procedure or Assessment.

Scenario 8 Output: Yes

there is a LSNBC (as lesion evaluable) =YES  with  Date of Lesion Evaluation and a corresponding SCAN or CTO form with the same Start date/time of procedure or Assessment and the Findings/Results is not Normal.

Sorting:
Country, Site Number and Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site Number,Subject Number, Randomized Only, List of Subject

6. CTO. Date of assessment

8. LSNBC. Lesion Number

9. LSNBC. Date of Lesion Evaluation

10. If Logic 1 is satisfied please display "Scenario 1", if Logic 2 is satisfied please display "Scenario 2", If Logic 3 is satisfied please display "Scenario 3", if Logic 4 is satisfied please display "Scenario 4"
