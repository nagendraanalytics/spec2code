Report ID: RID345

Report Title:
Missing/Incorrect/Out of order oncologic drugs and biologic therapy reporting

Description:
This report lists subjects who have an unacceptable gap, between different line of treatment done before the first dose of study medication AND there are missing line of therapy based on the sequential order.

AND

mismatch between dates of regimen and the order in lines of therapies.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. ODB Form

5. ODB Seq #1

6. Line of Therapy/Treatment Category #1

7. Start Date of Regimen #1

8. Stop Date of Regimen #1

9. ODB Seq #2

10. Line of Therapy/Treatment Category #2

11. Start Date of Regimen #2

12. Stop Date of Regimen #2

13. Scenario

Logic:
Scenario A:

Logic 1: ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS  form. Line of Therapy/Treatment category <> NULL and Imputed Stop Date of Regimen in ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form is less than or equal to min Start Date and Time in SM/SMAU/SMAB/SM01/SM02/SM04. (Date consider only)

AND

Logic 2: The difference between previous ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form. Line of Therapy/Treatment category  Imputed Stop Date of Regimen and consecutive next ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form. Line of Therapy/Treatment category  Imputed Sart Date of Regimen is ">","<", "≤", "≥" than Specified Gap in Days.

Scenario B

Logic 3: ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form. Line of Therapy/Treatment category <> NULL and Imputed Start Date of Regimen in ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form is less than or equal to min Start Date and Time in SM/SMAU/SMAB/SM01/SM02/SM04. (Date consider only)

AND

Logic 4:  there is at least one entry missing with Sequential order less than greatest sequence number of Line of Therapy/Treatment category.

Scenario C

Logic 5: ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form. Line of Therapy/Treatment category <> NULL and Imputed Start Date of Regimen in ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS  form is less than or equal to min Start Date and Time in SM/SMAU/SMAB/SM01/SM02/SM04. (Date consider only)

AND

Logic 6: ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form. Imputed Start Date of Regimen or ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS form. Imputed Stop Date of Regimen is greater than (sequential order + 1) Line of Therapy/Treatment category Imputed Start Date of Regimen or Imputed Stop Date of Regimen. (Cross check between Imputed Start Date of Regimen and Imputed Stop Date of Regimen)

Note 1: for Logic 4, for example, if maximim Line of Therapy/Treatment category entered in ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS is Fifth Line or Greater AND At least one of previous Line of therapies is missing (Fourth Line, Line of Therapy, Third Line, Second Line, First Line) there should be an output.

Note 2: Please exclude deleted records and erroneous visits from programming

Note 3: for scenario B, please display the missing line of therapy

Note 4: for ODBCS please consider RECURRENT INCURABLE and METASTATIC INCURABLE independently

Note 5: Studies can add: fifth line, sixth line, seventh line, eight line, ninth line, and tenth line or greater, if it is required. The report should work for all this lines of therapy

Note 6: For Scenario B Display on columns 5, 6, 7, 8, information for the line of therapy previous to the missing line of therapy and on columns 9, 10, 11, 12, information for the next line of therapy afer the missing line of therapy. for example if there is first line and fourth line, then second and third line are missing, output would display first line information on columns 5, 6, 7, 8, and information of fourth line on columns 9, 10, 11, 12.

Report Scenarios:
Report Scenarios:

Scenario A, Output = Yes

If difference between two consecutive Line of therapy ( e.g. first to second, second to third, third to fourht, fourth to fifht, etc.) is ">","<", "≤", "≥" X Days (according to specified selection in prompt Allowed Gap in Days’) .

Scenario B, Output= Yes

There is no sequential order in Lines of therapy entered, e.g. There is first line and third line but no second line.

Scenario C= Yes

For any numerical Line of therapy "X", Imputed Start and Stop dates of regimen “entries” are > than Imputed Start and Stop dates of regimen on any other Line of therapy that is in sequential order after Line of therapy "X".

Scenario D, Output= No

If Line of Therapy is not completed as xxx line of therapy (xxx is first, second), including "fifth or greater"

Scenario E, Output = No

If there is only one “entry” on ODB/ODBH/ODBHN/ODBCS  AND Line of Therapy is completed as “First Line”
