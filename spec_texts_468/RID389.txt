Report ID: RID389

Report Title:
Reconciliation between Lesion Evaluation and Scan forms

Description:
The report will display Subjects who have a Lesion recorded in Lesion Evaluation Form with a Date of Lesion Evaluation and Method of Assessment not matching the Date of Scan and Type of Image in the OSCN form.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject

4. Lesion Evaluation Form

5. Lesion Evaluation Seq#

6. Date Item

7. Lesion Evaluation Date

8. Method of Assessment

Logic:
Logic 1.1: There is a record in 'Lesion Evaluation Form'. 'Date of Lesion Evaluation' and 'Lesion Evaluation Form'. Method of Assessment <> NULL

AND

Logic 1.2: There is no record in OSCN that has the OSCN.Scan Date = 'Lesion Evaluation Form'.'Date of Lesion Evaluation' and OSCN.Type of Image = 'Lesion Evaluation Form'.Method of Assessment of the lesion evaluation record in Logic 1.1

Note 1. Do not take into account deleted records and erroneous visit.

Note 2: Use the global supporting document OSCN_Method_Supp_Doc for the mapping the methods in Lesion Evaluation eCRF and OSCN.

Note 3: If Physical Exam or Other is selected as the Method of Assessment, please do not output.

Note 4: For Column #5 display Sequence numbers separated by commas (,) for those outputs that share the same date (#7) and method of assessment (#8).

Note 5: Lesion Evaluation Form: LSRIR/LSNEV/LSNEI/LSNPC/LSNHM/LSNLG/HDA/LSNPI/LSNIR/LSNAJ/LOEL/LSNRN/NTSAC/LSNWM/LSNCL.

Note 6: Date of Lesion Evaluation: Date of Lesion Evaluation/Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)/Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)/Date Response Determined/Date of Lesion/Organ Evaluation.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A- Yes:

There is no record in OSCN that has the OSCN. Scan Date = LSRIR/LSNEV/LSNEI/LSNPC/LSNHM/LSNLG/HDA/LSNPI/LSNIR/LSNAJ/LSNWM/LOEL/LSNCL.Date of Lesion Evaluation/Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)/Date of Lesion/Organ Evaluation of the lesion evaluation record on L1 and OSCN.Type of Image = LSRIR/LSNEV/LSNEI/LSNPC/LSNHM/LSNLG/HDA/LSNPI/LSNIR/LSNAJ/LSNWM/LOEL/LSNCL. Method of Assessment of the lesion evaluation record on L1

Scenario B - No:

There is a record in OSCN that has the OSCN. Scan Date = LSRIR/LSNEV/LSNEI/LSNPC/LSNHM/LSNLG/HDA/LSNPI/LSNIR/LSNAJ/LSNWM/LOEL/LSNCL.Date of Lesion Evaluation/Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) /Date of Lesion/Organ Evaluation of the lesion evaluation record on L1 and OSCN.Type of Image = LSRIR/LSNEV/LSNEI/LSNPC/LSNHM/LSNLG/HDA/LSNPI/LSNIR/LSNAJ/LSNWM/LOEL/LSNCL. Method of Assessment of the lesion evaluation record on L1

Scenario C - No: Deleted records in Lesion Evaluation form.
