Report ID: RID124

Report Title:
Reconciliation between calculated QTCB and InForm derived QTCB

Description:
This report outputs if there is a >10 msec difference between the recalculated QTCB and the InForm derived QTCB.

Column Heading:
1.  Country

2.  Site

3.  Subject Number

4.  Visit

5. DOV

6. eCRF

7. Seq

8. QTCB

9. Recalculated QTCB

10. Difference

Logic:
The report will output LECG records where the absolute difference between the Inform derived QTCB and the calculated QTCB is greater than 10.

The Recalculated QTCB is defined as follows:

(QT Interval / (60/ Ventricular Rate ))^(1/2)

Deleted records should not be outputted.

Records in erroneous visits should not be considered.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output = YES

Recalculated QTCB - InForm QTCB > 10

2) Output = YES

InForm QTCB - Recalculated QTCB > 10

3) Output = NO

Recalculated QTCB - InForm QTCB <= 10

4) Output = NO

InForm QTCB - Recalculated QTCB <= 10

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Visit, eCRF, Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject, Randomized only, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1.  Subject Assignment.Site Country

2.  Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

3.  Subjects.Subject Number

4.  Electrocardiogram form.Visitmnemonic

5.  Date of Visit form.DOV

6.  Electrocardiogram form.Formmnemonic

7.  Electrocardiogram form.formidx

8.  Electrocardiogram form.QTCB

9.  Recalculated QTCB=(QT Interval / (60/ Ventricular Rate ))^(1/2)

10. ABS(Recalculated QTCB - QTCB)
