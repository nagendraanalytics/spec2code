Report ID: AE001EDMR_STD

Report Title:
Adverse Events with Relative Days to Period

Description:
The report generates a listing of Adverse Events with relative days to period.  Last visit is the final visit where there is a date of visit recorded.  Time Since Last Dose is is the amount of days, hours, and minutes that have elapsed since the last dose begin date where the dose amount is greater than 0.  Days Relative to Start of Trial are the number of days from the first visit date to the onset date of the AE.  Days Relative to Start of Period are the number of days from the date of visit of the period in which the AE occurred to the onset date of the AE.  PLEASE NOTE:  The phase and period in which the AE occurred is based upon the AE Onset Date in relation to the Date of Visit.  The timing of any study medication administration at that period is not considered.  The AE will fall into the phase and period where the AE onset date is equal to or after the date of visit for that visit, and prior to the date of visit of the next visit.

Logic:
Listing of AEs incl Tx group, rel days, duration. This report calculates time since last dose based on study medication records that have a dose amount>0. Last visit is the final visit where there is a date of visit recorded. Time Since Last Dose is the amount of days, hours, and minutes that have elapsed since the last dose begin date where the dose amount is greater than 0. Days Relative to Start of Trial are the number of days from the first visit date to the onset date of the AE. Days Relative to Start of Period are the number of days from the date of visit of the period in which the AE occurred to the onset date of the AE. The AE Term {LLT}{Pref Term} column is highlighted in yellow.  Grouped by Country, Site, Subject Number, Gender, Race, Age Deleted items should not be considered

Note! The AE onset date should take the entire date into account when sorting ascending within a visit.
