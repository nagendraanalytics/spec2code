Report ID: RID429

Report Title:
Reconciliation between SFU and AE when Subject Have a New Graft Versus Host Disease

Description:
This report will display subjects who have inconsistencies between SFU record with "Did the Subject Have a New Graft Versus Host Disease Event Since the Last Contact Date?" and a corresponding Adverse Event between the previous SFU and the current.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject number

4. Visit

5. SFU seq #

6. Date of contact or last attempt of contact

7. SFU answer

8. Form name of GVHD form

9. Seq# GVHD form

10. GVHD date

11. Scenario

Logic:
Scenario 1

Logic 1.1: ‘Did the Subject Have a New Graft Versus Host Disease Event Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact Date?’ = “Yes”

AND

Logic 1:2: There is not at least one entry in AOGVH or CGVHD or HGVHD or OAGVH forms form with [(AOGVH. GVHD Stage Onset Date) OR (CGVHD. Chronic GVHD Grade Start Date) OR (HGVHD. Start Date of GVHD) OR (OAGVH. GVHD Grade Onset Date)] before first SFU. Date of contact or last attempt of contact

OR

Logic 1.3: There is not an entry in AOGVH or CGVHD or HGVHD or OAGVH form with [(AOGVH. GVHD Stage Onset Date) OR (CGVHD. Chronic GVHD Grade Start Date) OR (HGVHD. Start Date of GVHD) OR (OAGVH. GVHD Grade Onset Date)] greater than previous SFU. Date of contact or last attempt of contact and less or equal to current SFU. Date of contact or last attempt of contact in logic 1.1.

Scenario 2

Logic 2.1: ‘Did the Subject Have a New Graft Versus Host Disease Event Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact Date?’ = “No”

AND

Logic 2:2: There is at least one entry in AOGVH or CGVHD or HGVHD or OAGVH forms form with [(AOGVH. GVHD Stage Onset Date) OR (CGVHD. Chronic GVHD Grade Start Date) OR (HGVHD. Start Date of GVHD) OR (OAGVH. GVHD Grade Onset Date)] before first SFU. Date of contact or last attempt of contact

OR

Logic 2.3: There is an entry in AOGVH or CGVHD or HGVHD or OAGVH form with [(AOGVH. GVHD Stage Onset Date) OR (CGVHD. Chronic GVHD Grade Start Date) OR (HGVHD. Start Date of GVHD) OR (OAGVH. GVHD Grade Onset Date)] greater than previous SFU. Date of contact or last attempt of contact and less or equal to current SFU. Date of contact or last attempt of contact in logic 2.1.

Notes:

Note 1:  Previous SFU is defined as the previous SFU entry based on dates.

Note 2: Please exclude deleted records and erroneous visits from programming Note 3: Time can be excluded from comparisson

Note 4: If there is UNK Date, please use: first day of each month if day is UNK, first month of each year if month is UNK.

Note 5: For logic 1.2: first SFU contact should be reviewed according to reported dates.

Note 6: For the GVHD form parameter, display all available forms. For each form selected in the prompt, please check logics in scenarios 1 and 2. if no form is selected, check all.

Note 7: The report should find at least one record within the expected data in one of the forms.
