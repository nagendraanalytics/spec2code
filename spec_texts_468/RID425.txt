Report ID: RID425

Report Title:
Mismatch between DOV for survival follow up visit and first entry on the SFU form based on dates.

Description:
This report will display subjects that have date mismatches between DOV for survival follow up visit and Date of contact or last attempt of contact in the first entry for SFU form based on dates.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Date of Visit (DOV) Survival Follow-up

5. Seq#

6. Date of contact or last attempt of contact

Logic:
Date of Visit of Survival follow up visit is <> to the Minimun Date of contact or last attempt of contact in all [SFU] records.

Note 1: Only output of randomized patients

Note 2: Erroneous visit or deleted record will not be considered in the programming or output.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario [Output = Yes]

Will output if the Date of Visit of Survival follow up visit is different than the Minimun Date of contact or last attempt of contact on the SFU form.

Scenarios [Output = No]

Will not output if the Date of Visit of Survival follow up visit is equal to the Minimun Date of contact or last attempt of contact on the SFU form.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Seq#

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Site Country

2. SA.Site Mnemonic

3. SA.Subject Number

4. Date Visit- Survival Follow-up

5. SFU.SFUCFSPID

6. SFU.SFUCFDTC
