Report ID: RID427

Report Title:
Progression reconciliation between SFU and Oncologic Drugs and Biologics Follow-up

Description:
This report displays subjects who have mismatch between SFU "Date of Progression" and Oncologic Drugs and Biologics Follow-up "Date of Progression After this Treatment Phase" or "Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)"

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. SFU Seq

5. SFU - Date of Progression

6. ODBF form

7. ODBF Seq

8. ODBF - Date of Progression/Recurrence

9. SFU - Status of the Subject

10. Error Message

Logic:
Scenario 1

Logic 1: SFU. 'Date of Progression' / 'Date of Progression/Recurrence'/ ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ <> Null

AND

Logic 2: There is no ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE = SFU. 'Date of Progression' / 'Date of Progression/Recurrence'/ ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ record of logic 1.

OR

Scenario 2

Logic 3: ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE = 'No Progression/Recurrence Seen' AND SFU.'Current disease status of subject'/Current Status of Primary Disease/ Current Status of the Disease under Study = 'Progressive Disease' (PD) /Entered SFU with Disease and Now Progression / Entered SFU with No Disease and Now New Lesions (RECURRENCE)

OR

Scenario 3

Logic 4.0 There is ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE <> NULL

AND

There is no SFU. 'Date of Progression' / 'Date of Progression/Recurrence'= ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE

OR

Logic 4.1 There is ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE <> NULL

AND

There is no

(

SFU. ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’= ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE

AND SFU.'Current disease status of subject'/Current Status of Primary Disease/Current status of the Disease under Study in ('Progressive Disease' (PD) /Entered SFU with Disease and Now Progression / Entered SFU with No Disease and Now New Lesions (RECURRENCE) )

)

Notes:

1. Deleted records and erroneous visits should not be considered in the programming and output.

2. For two forms (SFU and ODBF and Variations): UNK dates should be exactly matched.

3. The report will use master supporting document ODBF_supp_doc. This supporting document should be uploaded only once at TA level per the GDO Oncology TAST authorized members and will be used to facilitate programming of the report. Report builder will use all the columns provided in the excel spreadsheet to create the programming code. ODBF refers to the forms included in the ODBF_supp_doc.

4. For ODBNF, 'No Progression/Recurrence Seen' is changed for 'No Progression Seen'

5. Please do not output when ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE is entered as ‘No Progression/Recurrence Seen’ for scenario 1 and scenario 3.

6. Please do not output if ODBF. Form Mnemonic has not been initialized.

7. For column 7, separate all ODBF. Form Mnemonic by commas.

8. Scenario 1: Only show output when ODBF. Form Mnemonic form and the SFU form are completed

9. Scenario 1: when SFU. Form mnemonic has ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ item only show output when  SFU.Current Status of the Disease under study” has been completed as: “Entered SFU with Disease and Now Progression (PD)” OR “Entered SFU with No Disease and No New lesions (RECURRENCE)

10. Please apply logic 4.1 only when eCRF has SFU.‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ item

Report Scenarios:
A - Yes

SFU. 'Date of Progression'/ ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ is different to ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE

B - No

SFU. 'Date of Progression' / ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ is equal to ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE

C - Yes

ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE is equal to 'No Progression/Recurrence Seen' AND SFU. 'Current disease status of subject'/Current Status of Primary Disease/ Current Status of the Disease under Study = 'Progressive Disease' (PD) /Entered SFU with Disease and Now Progression / Entered SFU with No Disease and Now New Lesions (RECURRENCE)

D - No

ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE is equal to 'No Progression/Recurrence Seen' AND SFU. 'Current disease status of subject'/Current Status of Primary Disease/ Current Status of the Disease under Study <> 'Progressive Disease' (PD) /Entered SFU with Disease and Now Progression / Entered SFU with No Disease and Now New Lesions (RECURRENCE)

E - No

ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE is different to 'No Progression/Recurrence Seen' AND SFU. 'Current disease status of subject'/Current Status of Primary Disease/ Current Status of the Disease under Study = 'Progressive Disease' (PD) /Entered SFU with Disease and Now Progression / Entered SFU with No Disease and Now New Lesions (RECURRENCE)

F - Yes

ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE is completed AND SFU.Date of Progression/ ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ is equal to Null

G- No

ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE is equal to Null AND SFU.Date of Progression/ ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ is equal to Null

H- No

ODBF. DATE OF PROGRESSION AFTER THIS TREATMENT PHASE is different to Null AND SFU.Date of Progression/ ‘Date of Disease Status Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)’ is different to Null

I- No

Records are deleted
