Report ID: ACDC001_JR_STD

Report Title:
COVID 19 relatedness vs. subject status

Description:
This report displays subjects with inconsistencies between ACDC form and subject/study medication status forms.

Column Heading:
1.  Country
2.  Site
3.  Subject Number
4.  Status form
5. Visit
6. Trial segment
7. Date of assessment
8.  Status of Subject/Study Med
9. ACDC Question
10. Associated with COVID - 19
11. Scenario

Logic:
Scenario 1:

ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? <> Null
AND
DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> “Discontinued”.

OR

ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at the End of the Imaging/Efficacy Assessments Recorded on the OEDRA Form Associated With COVID-19? <> Null
AND
OEDRA.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment <> “Discontinued” in one of the visits entered in supporting document.

OR

ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject at the End of All Study Treatments Recorded on the PMST Form Associated With COVID-19? <> Null
AND
PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments<> “Discontinued” in one of the visits entered in supporting document.

OR

Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of [Trial Segment] Recorded on the [PMS1X] Form, Associated With COVID-19?<> Null
AND
Corresponding PMS1/PMS1A. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment <> “Discontinued” in one of the visits entered in supporting document.

OR
ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at End of This [Trial Segment] Recorded on the [PMS1X] Form, Associated With COVID-19?<>Null
AND
Corresponding PMS1/PMS1A. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment <> “Discontinued” in one of the visits entered in supporting document.

OR
ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at End of This [Trial Segment] Recorded on the [PMS1X] Form
AND
Corresponding PMS1/PMS1A. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment <> “Discontinued” in one of the visits entered in supporting document.

Scenario 2:

DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study = “Discontinued”  AND DS01.Date of Disposition => 1-Dec-2019
AND
ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? = Null

OR

OEDRA.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = “Discontinued” AND OEDRA.Date of Assessment => 1-Dec-2019
AND
ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at the End of the Imaging/Efficacy Assessments Recorded on the OEDRA Form Associated With COVID-19? = Null

OR

PMST. Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments = “Discontinued” AND PMST.Date of Assessment => 1-Dec-2019
AND
ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject at the End of All Study Treatments Recorded on the PMST Form Associated With COVID-19? = Null

OR

PMS[X].Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment= “Discontinued” AND PMS[X].Date of Assessment => 1-Dec-2019
AND
Corresponding  ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of [Trial Segment] Recorded on the [PMS1X] Form, Associated With COVID-19?= Null

OR
PMS[X].Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment= “Discontinued” AND PMS[X].Date of Assessment => 1-Dec-2019
AND
Corresponding  ACDC. Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at End of This [Trial Segment] Recorded on the [PMS1X] Form, Associated With COVID-19? = Null

Note 1: Report uses Mandatory supporting document to correlate the PMS1[X] with the matching trial segment, visit and ACDC item.

Note 2: The report will only consider Randomized Subjects: The report will only consider Randomized Subjects: Subjects who have "--" in their Subject Number

Note 3: The column 9 will populate the corresponding ACDC question text of the item that was completed or is expected to be completed

Note 4: For scenario 1, exclude OEDRA.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = "Not Applicable"or "Not Done"; exclude PMS1A. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment = "Not Applicable" or "Not Done".

Note 5: The number of rows in the supporting document will be represented in the Report Header as "# of rows for Supporting doc".

Requires supporting document to be named ACDC001_supp_</Vxxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

  1. Disposition Form – Enter PMS1 or form variation and/or OEDRA. No need to provide supporting document for DS01.
2. Visit - Enter the visit Mnemonic per T&E in which the disposition form is expected
3. Trial Segment Reference –Enter the “Trial Segment Reference” as it is in the ACDC Question. (In most cases this matches the “Trial Segment Reference” in the PMS1 or form variation). Only applicable for PMS1 or form variation, for other forms enter NA.
4. ACDC Question – Enter the ACDC Question from the ACDC form that is associated with the “Disposition Form” and “Trial Segment Reference”

e.g.
Disposition Form, Visit,Trial Segment Reference, ACDC Question

PMS1,Discontinuation Visit,Treatment,Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of This Treatment Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?
PMS1,Crossover End of Treatment, End of Cross Over Treatment,Was the Discontinued Status of Study Medication at the End of This Cross Over Treatment Recorded on the PMS1 Form, Associated With COVID-19?
PMS1A,Follow-up Day 30, End of First Course Follow-up,Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at End of This First Course Follow-up Recorded on the PMS1A Form, Associated With COVID-19?
PMS1A,Crossover Follow-up Day 30, End of Cross Over Follow-up,Was the Discontinued Status of Subject at End of This Cross Over Follow-up Recorded on the PMS1A Form, Associated With COVID-19?

If supporting doc is not provided, display the message as "ACDC001_JR_STD Supporting Document Not Present for This Trial".

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1a: Output Yes

Discontinued Status is completed in ACDC form AND corresponding Status in DS01/OEDRA/PMST/PMS1/PMS1A is “Completed” or Blank.

Scenario 1b: Output No

Discontinued Status is completed in ACDC form and corresponding Status in DS01/OEDRA/PMST/PMS1/PMS1A is “Discontinued” AND Date of assessment is on or after 1-Dec-2019.

Scenario 2a: Output Yes

Status in DS01/OEDRA/PMST/PMS1/PMS1A is “Discontinued” with Date of assessment on or after 1-Dec-2019 and the corresponding Discontinued Status in ACDC form is blank.

Scenario 2b: Output No

Status in DS01/OEDRA/PMST/PMS1/PMS1A is “Completed” or Blank or  Date of assessment is prior to 1-Dec-2019.

11. Display "Scenario 1" for scenario 1 and "Scenario 2" for scenario 2
