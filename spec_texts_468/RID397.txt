Report ID: RID397

Report Title:
Missing PFS2 assessment

Description:
This report displays subjects who have missing an PFS2 Date of Contact.

Column Heading:
Table 1:

1.Country

2.Site

3.Subject Number

4.OEDRA Visit

5.Date of Assessment

6.OEDRA Status

7.New Anti-cancer Therapy Form

8.New Anti-cancer Therapy Seq#

9.New Anti-cancer Therapy Date

10.Elapse days since PD or New Anti-cancer Therapy

Table 2:

1.Country

2.Site

3.Subject Number

4.PFS2 Visit

5.PFS2 Seq#

6. Max PFS2 Date of Contact

7.Days Elapse since PFS2

Logic:
Please output:

Logic 1:

Logic 1.a: [(System Date) minus (Oncologic Date <> NULL)] > Number of days AND

Logic 1.b: OEFU. Date of Assessment = NULL AND

Logic 1.c: There is no PFS2.Date of Contact <> NULL

Logic 2:

Logic 2.a: (System Date) - (Max. PFS2.Date of Contact) > (Number of days) AND

Logic 2.b: OEFU. Date of Assessment = NULL

Note 1: Please do not include deleted entries

Note 2: Please do not evaluate visits where ‘Erroneous Visit’ checkbox is selected.

Note 3: Please consider the Oncologic Date as the MINIMUM of the following: ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF. Imputed Start Date of Medication or If not present please consider Start Date of Regimen ; ORAD2. Start Date; OSOF. Admission Date; (OEDRA. Date of Assessment AND OEDRA. ‘Status of Subject at End of this Trial Segment’ = ‘Progressive Disease’ OR ‘Clinical Progression’ OR ‘New Non-Study Anti-Cancer Therapy’)

Note 5: If any date is entered as UNK please consider the maximum day of the month and the maximum month of the year.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A – Output: Yes

There is no PFS2 Date of Contact AND more than (Number of days) days have elapsed since the earliest of: [ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF Start Date of Regimen OR ORAD2 Start Date OR OSOF Admission Date OR OEDRA Date of Assessment with Status at End of this Trial Segment= ‘Progressive Disease’ or ‘Clinical Progression’ or ‘New Non-Study Anti-Cancer Therapy’] AND OEFU Date of Assessment is not completed.

Scenario B – Output: Yes

This report displays subjects who are missing PFS2 Date of Contact and more than 70 days (maximum = 8 weeks + 14 days) have elapsed since the previous PFS2 Date of Contact.

Scenario C – Output: No

There is a PFS2 Date of Contact AND more than (Number of days) days have elapsed since the earliest of: [ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF Start Date or regimen OR ORAD2 Start Date OR OEDRA Date of Assessment with Status at End of this Trial Segment= ‘Progressive Disease’ or ‘Clinical Progression’ or ‘New Non-Study Anti-Cancer Therapy’] AND OEFU Date of Assessment is not completed.

Scenario D – Output: No

There is no PFS2 Date of Contact AND more than (Number of days) days have elapsed since the earliest of: [ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF Start Date or regimen OR ORAD2 Start Date OR OEDRA Date of Assessment with Status at End of this Trial Segment= ‘Progressive Disease’ or ‘Clinical Progression’ or ‘New Non-Study Anti-Cancer Therapy’] AND OEFU Date of Assessment is completed.

Scenario E – Output: No

More than (Number of days) days have elapsed since the Maximum Date of Contact in PFS2. BUT OEFU Date of Assessment is completed

Sorting:
Default
