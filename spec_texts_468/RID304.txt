Report ID: RID304

Report Title:
Incorrect NTL status at baseline or after baseline

Description:
This report generates a list of subjects whose NTL status is incorrectly entered depending on whether the lesion is baseline or not.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Lesion Evaluation Form

5. Lesion Number

6. Date of Lesion Evaluation

7. Non-Target Lesion Status

8. Scenario

Logic:
Logic 1: Lesion Identification or Tumor Identification = Non-Target Extra Nodal or Non-Target Nodal or Enhancing Non-Target

AND

(

Scenario 1:

Logic 1.1: Date of Lesion Evaluation <> Lesion Date and Non Target Lesion Status = N/A Baseline

OR

Scenario 2

Logic 1.2:  Date of Lesion Evaluation = Lesion Date and Non Target Lesion Status <> N/A Baseline

)

Notes:

Note 1: Lesion Evaluation Form prompt should display: LSNHM, LSNEV, LSRIR, LSNPC, LSNPI, LSNRN, LSNLG, LSNIR

Note 2: "Lesion Evaluation form" should be considered depending on what has been selected in "Lesion Evaluation Form" parameter.

Note 3: For LSNHM, LSNEV, LSNPC, LSNLG,please use Lesion Identification for logic 1; For LSRIR, LSNPI, LSNIR please use Tumor Identification for logic 1.

Note 4: “Date of Lesion Evaluation” should be considered depending on what has been selected in "Lesion Evaluation Form" parameter, consider: Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) OR Date of Lesion Evaluation

Note 5: “Lesion Date” should be considered depending on what has been selected in "Lesion Evaluation Form" parameter, consider: Date of Assessment OR Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

Note 6: Report should not consider erroneous visit or deleted records.

Note 7: If multiple forms are selected on “Lesion Evaluation form” parameter, please consider looking in all forms.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1

A: Yes

Date of Lesion Evaluation <> Lesion Date, Tumor Identification = Non-Target Extra Nodal or Non-Target Nodal and Non Target Lesion Status = N/A Baseline

B: No

Date of Lesion Evaluation <> Lesion Date, Tumor Identification = Non-Target Extra Nodal or Non-Target Nodal and Non Target Lesion Status <> N/A Baseline

Scenario 2

C: Yes

Date of Lesion Evaluation = Lesion Date, Tumor Identification = Non-Target Extra Nodal or Non-Target Nodal and Non Target Lesion Status <> N/A Baseline

D: No

Date of Lesion Evaluation = Lesion Date, Tumor Identification = Non-Target Extra Nodal or Non-Target Nodal and Non Target Lesion Status = N/A Baseline

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, Randomized Only, List of Subjects, Lesion Evaluation Form (required, multiple selection allowed)

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment. Site Country.

2. Subject Assignment. Site Mnemonic.

3. Subject Assignment. Subject Number.

4. Lesion Evaluation Form selected on prompts

5. “Lesion Evaluation Form”. Lesion Number

6. Lesion Evaluation Form”. Date of Lesion Evaluation

7. “Lesion Evaluation Form”. Non Target Lesion Status

8. IF 'Logic 1' =True THEN Output='Scenario 1'. IF 'Logic 2' =True THEN Output='Scenario 2'
