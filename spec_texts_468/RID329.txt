Report ID: RID329

Report Title:
Baseline Lesion in LSNPI is Not Measurable

Description:
This report will list subjects who do not have at least one measurable Lesion at baseline or the Tumor Identification is not consistent with the Short Axis or Longest Diameter reports at baseline.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Name

5. SM Form

6. Date of first dose

7. Lesion Number

8. Tumor Identification (Organ)

9. Longest Diameter

10. Short Axis

11. Error message

Logic:
Logic 1.1: Date of First Dose <> NULL

AND

Logic 1.2: LSNPI form. Tumor Identification = Target Nodal OR Target Extra Nodal

AND

Logic 1.3: LSNPI. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) is within X days prior Date of First Dose (Only date is considered)

AND

Logic 1.4: Do not have at least one LSNPI. Lesion evaluation Entry where:

LSNPI form. Tumor Identification is “Target Nodal” and Short Axis is greater than or equal to 15 mm

OR

LSNPI form. Tumor Identification is “Target Extra Nodal” and Longest Diameter is greater than or equal to 10 mm

OR

Logic 2:

LSNPI form. Tumor Identification = Target Nodal and Short Axis at baseline (the minimum Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)) is not >= 15 mm

OR

LSNPI form, Tumor Identification = Target Extra Nodal and Longest Diameter at baseline (the minimum Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)) is not >= 10 mm.

Please exclude deleted record and erroneous visit from programming.

Note 1:  X is the number of days specified in the “Window of days” parameter.

Note 2: Date of First Dose is the Start Date/Time of the min SM/SMAU/SMAB record where “Please Indicate if this is the First Dose for this Label Description” is checked.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A, Output: Yes

Subjects who have SM records but do not have at least one LSNPI Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) within X days prior to the Date of First Dose with either:

(1)	Tumor Identification is “Target Nodal” and Short Axis  >= 15 mm

OR

(2) Tumor Identification is “Target Extra Nodal” and Longest Diameter is >= 10 mm.

Scenario B, Output: No

Subjects who have SM records and have at least one LSNPI Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) within X days prior to the Date of First Dose with either:

(2)	Tumor Identification is “Target Nodal” and Short Axis  >= 15 mm

OR

(2) Tumor Identification is “Target Extra Nodal” and Longest Diameter is >= 10 mm

Scenario C, Output: Yes

Subjects who have SM records and have at least one LSNPI Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) but it is not within X days prior to the Date of First Dose with either:

(3)	Tumor Identification is “Target Nodal” and Short Axis  >= 15 mm

OR

(2) Tumor Identification is “Target Extra Nodal” and Longest Diameter is >= 10 mm

Scenario D, Output: Yes:

Subject who at baseline lesions have Tumor Identification as Target Nodal and Short Axis is not equal or greater to 15 mm.

OR

Subjects who at baseline lesions have Tumor Identification as Target Extra Nodal and Longest Diameter is not equal or greater to 10 mm.

Scenario E, Output: No:

Subject who at baseline lesions have Tumor Identification as Target Nodal and Short Axis is equal or greater to 15 mm.

OR

Subject who have Tumor Identification as Target Extra Nodal and Longest Diameter at baseline is equal or greater to 10 mm.

Please exclude deleted record and erroneous visit from programming.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Randomized Only, Window of days (28 days)

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment. Site Country

2. Subject Assignment form.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects. Subject Number

4. DOV. Date of visit. Visit Mnemonic

5. SM/SMAU/SMAB.

6. SM/SMAU/SMAB. Start Date and Time

7. LSNPI. Lesion Number

8. LSNPI. Tumor Identification

9. LSNPI. Longest Diameter

10. LSNPI. Short Axis

11. Display as

‐‐ “Measurable Lesion missing” when Logic 1 is met;

-- “Tumor Identification is not consistent”, when Logic 2 is met
