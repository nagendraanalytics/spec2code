Report ID: RID260

Report Title:
Mismatch between cause of death and dates between DEATH, SFU and DS01

Description:
This report will display subjects with inconsistencies in Cause of Death/dates between SFU, DEATH and DS01 forms

Column Heading:
Table 1

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. SFU Seq

5. SFU Cause of Death

6. DEATH LLT

7. DS01 Subject status

8. Scenario

Table 2

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. SFU Seq

5. Survival Status

6. SFU Date of Death

7. DEATH Date/Time of Death

8. Date of Disposition

9. Scenario

Logic:
Logic 1.1: If SFU.Cause of Death = Progressive Disease AND DEATH.LLT <> Malignant Neoplasm Progression

OR

Logic 1.2: If DEATH.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND there is no at least one SFU.Cause of Death = Progressive Disease

OR

Logic 2: If (DEATH.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression OR If SFU.Cause of Death = Progressive Disease) AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Death

OR

Logic 3: If SFU.Survival Status = Dead AND SFU.Date of Death <> DS01.Date of Disposition

OR

Logic 4: If DS01.Date of Disposition <> DEATH.Date/Time of Death AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Withdrawal by Subject

Note 1. Please output if "UNK" dates are entered

Note 2. Please Ignore Time for comparison

Note 3. For logic "DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Death", please ensure that when DS01 = BLANK output is also shown.

Note 4. Logic 1.1 and 1.2 should only output when both forms are initialized.

Note 5. For Logic 1.1, 1.2 no output should be shown if eCRF does not contain field Cause of Death in SFU form;

Note 6. For Logic 2 if eCRF does not contain field Cause of Death in SFU form ignore this field

Note 7. For Logic 3 no output should be shown if eCRF does not contain SFU.Date of Death

Note 8: For Logic 4 only output if both forms are initialized and DEATH.Date/Time of Death is entered

Report Scenarios:
A output = Yes SFU.Cause of Death = Progressive Disease AND DEATH.LLT <> Malignant Neoplasm Progression

B output = Yes DEATH.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND There is not at least one SFU record with Cause of Death = Progressive Disease AND SFU Cause of Death <> NULL

C output = No There is at least one SFU.Cause of Death = Progressive Disease AND DEATH.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression

D output = Yes DEATH.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Death

E output = Yes SFU.Cause of Death = Progressive Disease AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Death

F output = No DEATH.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study = Death

G output = No SFU.Cause of Death = Progressive Disease AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study = Death

H output = Yes SFU.Survival Status = Dead AND SFU.Date of Death <> DS01.Date of Disposition

I output = No SFU.Survival Status <> Dead

J output = No SFU.Survival Status = Dead AND SFU.Date of Death = DS01.Date of Disposition

K output = Yes DS01.Date of Disposition <> DEATH.Date/Time of Death AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Withdrawal by Subject

L output = No DS01.Date of Disposition = DEATH. Date/Time of Death

M output = No DS01.Date of Disposition <> DEATH.Date/Time of Death AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study = Withdrawal by Subject

N output= No DS01.Date of Disposition <> NULL AND DEATH.Date/Time of Death= NULL

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects
