Report ID: RID401

Report Title:
NON-STUDY ANTI-CANCER THERAPY selected Vs Other treatment forms entries

Description:
The report displays subjects who have started new anticancer therapy as reason for

Discontinuation on any status form and no corresponded therapy form is present.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. Form

6. Date of assessment

7. Status

Logic:
Logic 1:

There is a "Status form" with "Itemmnemonic" = "Reason for Discontinuation"

AND There is no at least one record on the new anti cancer therapy form

Note 1: "Status form", "Itemmenocmic" (Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment, Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment) and "Reason for discontinuation" are values from supporting document.

Note 2: ORAD2, OSOF, OSOFL, ODBF, ODBHF, ODBNF, ODBCF are the new anti cancer therapy forms, please take into account only the ones present in the protocol.

Note 3: Applicable ONLY when ORAD2 is the only eCRF completed and ODBF, ODBHF, ODBNF, ODBCF, OSOF are empty: If all records of reason for radiation in ORAD2 are selected as 'palliative treatment or Symptom Control' or 'palliative treatment or Symptom Control of metastatic disease' then output. If one record is palliative and the other is something different than palliative, then no output.

Note 4: This report use supporting document.

If supporting doc is not provided, display the message as "ONC_PMS1_001_JR_STD Supporting Document Not Present for This Trial".

Requires supporting document to be named ONC_PMS1_001_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1. Status form – Required, enter PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/OEDRA

2. Itemmnemonic – Required, enter the corresponding question text that captures the reason why subject completes PMS1/PMST/OEDRA/OEFU

3. Reason for Discontinuation – Required, enter the actual reason that represents the subject is receiving a new anti cancer therapy

Eg.

Status form, Itemmnemonic, Reason for Discontinuation

PMS1, Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment, Need for Excluded Medication Discontinuation Criteria

PMS1, Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment, New Non-Study Anti-Cancer Therapy

PMST, Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment, Need for Excluded Medication Discontinuation Criteria

The number of rows in the supporting document will be represented in the Report Header as "# of rows for customization".

The actual information provided by the supporting doc can be outputted by selecting Y in the parameter "Supporting doc Page".

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1:

Output = NO

There is at least one record entered in one of the New anti-cancer therapy forms.

Scenario 2:

Output= NO

The eCRFs provided in the supporting Doc. ’Status form’ are not initialized.

Scenario 3:

Output = YES

Per supporting Doc. ‘Reason for Discontinuation‘  has been completed for in the ‘status form‘ and there is no at least one record in one of the new anti cancer forms.

Note: Exclude deleted records and records completed entirely as Unknown, Not Applicable or Not Done.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Visit, Form

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Supporting Doc Page (N/Y, Default as N), Randomized Only (Y/N set default to N)

Form.Item Names:
1. Status form.Site Country

2. Status form.Site Mnemonic

3. Status form.Subject Number

4. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/OEDRA form.Visit Mnemonic

5. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/OEDRA form.Form Mnemonic

6. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/OEDRA form.Date of Assessment

7. PMS1A/OEDRA Form.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment OR PMS1 Form.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment OR PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments
