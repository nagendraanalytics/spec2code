Report ID: RID402

Report Title:
Protocol Milestone Status forms entered listing

Description:
This report will list subjects who have data  in PMS1/PMS1A

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Mnemonic

5. Form Mnemonic

6. Date of assessment

7. Reason for Discontinuation

8. Next trial Segment.

Logic:
If PMS1/PMS1A.Date of Assessment AND PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment AND PMS1/PMS1A.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment are entered

Note 1: PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment should only be considered if present in the study.

Note 2: For column 7. Reason for Discontinuation if nested items are not present then display output as it is shown in the next example: ‘Discontinued – Progressive Disease’

Note 3: For column 7. Reason for Discontinuation if nested items are present then display output as it is shown in the next example: ‘Discontinued – Progressive Disease – Centrally verified’

Report Scenarios:
A- Yes PMS1/PMS1A.Date of Assessment AND PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment AND PMS1/PMS1A.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment are entered

B- No One ore more of the following times is missing: PMS1/PMS1A.Date of Assessment , PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment , PMS1/PMS1A.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment is missing

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only

Form.Item Names:
1. SA. Site Country
