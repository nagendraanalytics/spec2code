Report ID: RID261

Report Title:
Missing DEATH eCRF

Description:
This report will show subjects that died during treatment phase or within SAE reporting period but DEATH form is missing.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. eCRF

5. Seq#

6. Date of Disposition or Contact

7. New Therapy eCRF

8. Start Date

9. SM Last Dose

Logic:
(Logic 1.1: DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study = Death

OR

Logic 1.2: VST.Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject = Dead)

AND

Logic 1.3: DEATH.Date/Time of Death = NULL

AND

(Logic 1.4:  ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF/ORAD2 form has not been initialized and DS01.Date of Disposition or VST.Date of Contact

<= Maximum SM04/SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR.Start Date and Time + 90 days

OR

Logic 1.5: ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF.Start Date of Regimen OR  minimum ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF.Start Date of Medication <> NULL and DS01.Date of Disposition or VST.Date of Contact <= Maximum SM04/SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR.Start Date and Time + 30 days

OR

Logic 1.6: ORAD2.Start Date <> NULL AND ORAD2.Reason for Radiation <> “Palliative Treatment or Symptom Control” OR “Palliative Treatment or Symptom Control of Metastatic Disease”

AND  DS01.Date of Disposition or VST.Date of Contact <= Maximum SM04/SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR.Start Date and Time + 30 days)

Note 1: Deleted records and erroneous visits should not be taken into account.

Note 2: For Logic 4 if ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF.Start Date of Regimen OR minimum ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF.Start Date of Medication OR ORAD2.Start Date > Maximum SM04/SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR.Start Date and Time + 90 days, do not show output.

Note 3: If Logic 1.1 and Logic 1.2 are met for the same subject, only display output for DS01.+

Note 4: For logic 4, apply ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF Imputed Start Date of Regimen/ Imputed Start Date of Medication, using Imputed Start Date of Regimen if both exist.

Note 5: For UNK day and month, please consider the first day and first month.

Note 6: Ignore the logic related to VST form if VST form not exists.

Report Scenarios:
A – Yes

DS01 completed as DEATH during SAE reporting period and DEATH form is missing

B – No

DS01 completed as DEATH after SAE reporting period and DEATH form is missing

C – Yes

VST completed as Dead during SAE reporting period and DEATH form is missing

D – No

VST completed as Dead after SAE reporting period and DEATH form is missing

Sorting:
1. Country 2. Site 3. Subject number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (Y/N set default to N)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Country

2. SA.Site

3. SA.Subject number

4. DS01/VST. Form Mnemonic

5.VST. Seq #. Display blank for DS01.

6. DS01.Date of Disposition/VST.Date of Contact

7. ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF/ORAD2. Form Mnemonic

8. minimum ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF.Start Date of Regimen /minimum ORAD2.Start Date/minimum ODBF/ODBHF/ODBNF/ODBCF.Start Date of Medication

9. maximum SM04/SM/SMAU/SMAB/STR.Start Date and Time.
