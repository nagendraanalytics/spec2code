Report ID: RID298

Report Title:
New Lesion and New FDG-Positive Lesions Reconciliation Between OTPRL and LOEL Forms

Description:
This report will list subjects who have inconsistencies reported between New Lesions and/or New FDG-Positive Lesions in LOEL form and New Lesions item in OTPRL form.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. OTPRL form

5. Sequence

6. Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

7. New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator

8. LOEL form

9. Seq#

10. Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial(Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

11. Lesion Identification

12. Date of Lesion/Organ Evaluation

13. 5 (5PS) Score

14. Scenario

Note: For Scenario 1, please leave column 8 to 13 as blank. For Scenario 2, please leave leave column 4 to 7  as blank.

Logic:
Scenario 1:

Logic 1.1: OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes

AND

Logic 1.2: There is no LOEL.Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan Image Physical Exam) equal to OTPRL.Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan Image Physical Exam) of logic 1.1 AND with LOEL.Lesion Identification = New

AND

Logic 1.3: There is no LOEL.Date of Lesion/Organ Evaluation of Lesion Evaluation Form equal to OTPRL.Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan Image Physical Exam) of logic 1.1 AND with LOEL.5 (5PS) Score= “New FDG-Positive Lesions” OR “Both High Uptake and New Positive Lesions”

Scenario 2:

(Logic 2.1: LOEL.Lesion Identification= New

AND

Logic 2.2: There is no OTPRL.Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan Image Physical Exam) equal to LOEL.Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial (Scan Image Physical Exam) of logic 2.1 AND with OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes)

OR

(Logic 2.3: LOEL.5 (5PS) Score= “New FDG-Positive Lesions” OR “Both High Uptake and New Positive Lesions”

AND

Logic 2.4: There is no OTPRL.Date of Procedure for Overall Response (Scan Image Physical Exam) equal to LOEL.Date of Lesion/Organ Evaluation of logic 2.3 AND with OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes)

Report Scenarios:
Scenarios 1:

A. Output = Yes

OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes and there is no LOEL.Lesion Identification=New and no LOEL.5 (5PS) Score= New FDG-Positive Lesions or Both High Uptake and New Positive Lesions on the same date.

B. Output = No

OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes and there is LOEL.Lesion Identification=New or LOEL.5 (5PS) Score= New FDG-Positive Lesions or Both High Uptake and New Positive Lesions on the same date.

Scenarios 2:

A. Output = Yes

LOEL.Lesion Identification=New and there is no OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes on the same date

B. Output = Yes

LOEL.5 (5PS) Score= New FDG-Positive Lesions or Both High Uptake and New Positive Lesions and there is no OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes on the same date.

C. Output = No

LOEL.Lesion Identification=New and there is OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes on the same date

D. Output = No

LOEL.5 (5PS) Score= New FDG-Positive Lesions or Both High Uptake and New Positive Lesions and there is OTPRL.New Lesions Since Baseline Indicator=Yes on the same date.

Sorting:
Default

Parameter Selection:
Default

9. LOEL. Sponsor ID Number

10. LOEL. “Date of Initial Lesion Identification for This Trial(Scan/Image/Physical Exam)”. It should be blank if logic 2.1 not satisfies.

11.LOEL. Lesion Identification

12. LOEL. “Date of Lesion/Organ Evaluation”. It should be blank if logic 2.3 not satisfies.

13. LOEL. 5 (5PS) Score

14. Display Scenario 1 or Scenario 2 depending on which scenario is met

Note: For Scenario 1, please leave column 8 to 13 as blank. For Scenario 2, please leave leave column 4 to 7  as blank.
