Report ID: RID463

Report Title:
Duplicate VST records

Description:
This report lists subjects where VST Date of Contact is duplicated in other form/visit.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. VST sequence

5. VST Date of Contact

6. Visit Mnemonic

7. Sequence

8. Duplicate eCRF

Logic:
Report Logic: Logic 1:

VST.Date of Contact is not null AND VST.Indicate the Vital Status of the Subject <> Dead

AND

(DOV.Date of Visit is not null OR SFU.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact is not null)

AND

Logic 2

(VST.Date of Contact = DOV.Date of Visit

OR

VST.Date of Contact = SFU.Date of Contact or Last Attempt of Contact AND SFU.Survival Status <> Dead)

Note 1: Deleted records and Erroneous Visits should not be considered for programming

Note 2: Search into all sequences reported in both VST and SFU AND into all visits in which DOV is completed

Note 3: If duplicate is found between VST and DOV, Leave blank Column #7 "Sequence"

Note 4: If duplicate is found between VST and SFU, Display sequence number of SFU in which the duplicate is present in Column #7 "Sequence"

Note 5: Duplicate between VST entry and DOV of Vital Status should not appear in output, since are within the same visit mnemonic"

Report Scenarios:
Output – Yes: VST is equal to the date in DOV or SFU

Output – No: VST is not equal to the date in DOV or SFU

Output – No: Deleted records or erroneous visits

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, VST sequence, VST Date of Contact, Visit Mnemonic, Sequence, Duplicate eCRF

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Country

2. SA.Site

3. SA.Subject number

4. VST.Sponsor ID Number

5. VST.Date of Contact

6. Display Visit Mnemonic found in Logic 2

7. Display sequence of the eCRF found in Logic 2 – Notes 3 and 4

8. Display the duplicate eCRF found in Logic 1 (DOV or SFU)
