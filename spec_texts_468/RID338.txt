Report ID: RID338

Report Title:
NATS, PMS1, PMS1A, PMST listing

Description:
This report will list subjects who have data in PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NATS.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Mnemonic

5. Form Mnemonic

6. NATS Seq #

7. Date of Assessment

8. Reason for Discontinuation

9. Next Trial Segment

10. Study Treatment Not Received

11. Reason Study Treatment Not Received

Logic:
If PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NATS.Date of Assessment AND PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments AND PMS1/PMS1A/PMST.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment AND NATS. Study Treatment Not Received AND NATS.Reason Study Treatment Not Received are entered

Notes:

1. PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment should only be considered if present in the study.

2. NATS.Sponsor ID Number (NATS.Associated Sponsor ID Number), NATS.Study Treatment Not Received and NATS.Reason Study Treatment Not Received columns should be blank for PMS1, PMS1A and PMST forms.

3. PMS1/PMS1A/PMST. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments and PMS1/PMS1A/PMST.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment columns should be blank for NATS form.

4. Deleted records and erroneous visit should not be considered

5. PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments should only be considered if present in the study.

Report Scenarios:
A- Yes

PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NATS.Date of Assessment AND PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments AND PMS1/PMS1A/PMST.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment AND NATS. Study Treatment Not Received AND NATS.Reason Study Treatment Not Received are entered

B- No

One or more of the following times is missing: PMS1/PMS1A/PMST/NATS.Date of Assessment,  PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMS1A.Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments, PMS1/PMS1A/PMST.Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment AND NATS. Study Treatment Not Received AND NATS.Reason Study Treatment Not Received are missing

C- No

NATS.Records are deleted

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Date of Assessment

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number and List of Subjects
