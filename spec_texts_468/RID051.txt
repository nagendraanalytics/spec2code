Report ID: RID051

Report Title:
Potential Duplicate Subject Information

Description:
This report generates a list of subjects within a country who have identical birth dates, gender, race and ethnicity values and at least one patient is randomized.  This report checks for potential duplicate subjects within a protocol.

Column Heading:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. Vitals.Height

5. Vitals.Weight

6. Demographics Form.Date of Birth

7. Demographics Form.Gender

8. Demographics Form.Race

9. Demographics Form.Ethinicity

10. Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

11. Subjects.Subject Number

12. Vitals.Height

13. Vitals.Weight

Logic:
This report will output subjects where Country, Date of Birth, Gender, Race and Ethnicity fields are identical.

The output should contain the subject and the comparison subject that have duplicated information.

The Height and Weight outputted are those with the minimum date of collection.

Duplicate pairs corresponding to two non-randomized subjects will not be outputted.

Duplicate pairs corresponding to one non-randomized subject and one randomized subject from different sites will not be outputted.No need to consider Screening number(same/different).

Report should not display deleted records.

Report should not consider vitals in erroneous visits.

Report Scenarios:
There are two subjects from same country and site, have randomization numbers and have same Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - Yes

There are two subjects from same country and site, have randomization numbers but at least one of the following is different: Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - No

There are two subjects from same country and site, only one subject has randomization number, they have same Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - Yes

There are two subjects from same country and different sites, only one subject has randomization number, they have same Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - No

There are two subjects from same country and different sites, have randomization numbers and have same Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - Yes

There are two subjects from same country and site, they do NOT have randomization numbers and have same Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - No

There are two subjects from same country and different sites, they do NOT have randomization numbers and have same Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - No

There are two subjects from same country and different sites, only one subject has randomization number, and the two subjects have same Date of Birth, Gender, Race, Ethnicity - No

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Date of Birth

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject Number, Last Subject Number
