Report ID: RID005

Report Title:
DEATH vs ACDC

Description:
This report will display those subjects who died due to COVID but who do not have the corresponding ACDC question answered as Yes

Column Heading:
1.	Country

2.	Site

3.	Subject Number

4.	Primary Reported Cause of Death

5.	LLT Term

6.	Date/Time of Death

7.	Date of Disposition

8.	Reason for Discontinuation

9.	Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19?

Logic:
Logic 1.1: DEATH.LLT = Any of the terms from the "COVID-19 Terms" column in ACDC005_global_supp_doc.csv

AND

Logic 1.2: DEATH.Date/Time of Death =< DS01.Date of Disposition

AND

Logic 1.3: ACDC.Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? Is NOT NULL AND it is different than "Yes"

Notes:

1-	The report will use an internal supporting document to be named ACDC005_global_supp_doc.csv that will contain 1 row for each COVID-19 term or related syndrome. This supporting document is to be uploaded only once at standards level and can be modified each time a new COVID-19 term is added to the MedDRA dictionaries.

e.g.

COVID-19 Terms

Asymptomatic COVID-19

Community-related COVID-19 exposure

COVID-19

COVID-19 aggravated

COVID-19 pneumonia

COVID-19 pneumonia aggravated

COVID-19 pneumonitis

COVID-19 recurrent

COVID-19 respiratory infection

Exposure to COVID-19

Interstitial pneumonia due to COVID-19

Novel COVID-19-infected pneumonia

Occupational exposure to COVID-19

Post-acute COVID-19 syndrome

Suspected COVID-19

Travel-associated COVID-19 exposure

2019 novel coronavirus infection

2-	Report will be used for randomized only subjects, i.e. subjects who have "--" in the subject number

3-             For Logic 1.3, only consider the ACDC question when is NOT NULL and different than "Yes"

Report Scenarios:
Output Yes: Subject died during the study due to COVID-19 or any related COVID-19 syndrome/infection and the ACDC question Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? Is not answered as "Yes"

Output No: Subject died during the study due to reasons other than COVID-19 or related syndrome/infection and the ACDC question Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? Is not answered as "Yes"

Output No: Subject died after the discontinuation from the study due to COVID-19 or any related COVID-19 syndrome/infection and the ACDC question Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? Is not answered as "Yes"

Output No: Subject died during the study due to COVID-19 or any related COVID-19 syndrome/infection and the ACDC question Was the Discontinued Status of the Subject for the Study, Recorded on the DS01 Form, Associated With COVID-19? Is answered as "Yes"

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects
