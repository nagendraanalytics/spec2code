Report ID: RID243

Report Title:
CM vs Immune related AE reconciliation report

Description:
This report will display subjects with an irAE (immune related AE) with Toxicity 2 or greater and no corticosteriod CM related

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq #

5. AE Term

6. AE Toxicity Grade

Logic:
Logic 1: AE form. Is the AE immune related? = Yes AND

Logic 2: AE Toxicity Grade is equal to or greater than 2 AND

Logic 3: There is no CM of corticosteroid taken for the same period of time.

Note: CM of corticosteroid: encoded drug name/ATC2 term/ medication class/ Standardized medication name contains 'Corticosteroids' Note: Only associated AE and CM will be checked. Please exclude deleted record from programming.

Note 2: For logic 3 The overlaps to consider that CM was given for a particular AE should be:

1.	CM dates (Start /Stop date) within AE range dates

2.	CM start date < AE start date and CM stop date > AE stop date

Report Scenarios:
REPORT SCENARIOS:

Scenario A, Output: Yes Subjects with an Immunorelated AE and AE toxicity grade is 2 or greater than 2 who have no CM of corticosteroid taken for the same period of time

Scenario B, Output: No Subjects with an Immunorelated AE and AE toxicity grade is 2 or greater than 2 who have CM of corticosteroid taken for the same period of time

Scenario C, Output: No Is the AE immune related? <> Yes

Scenario D, Output: No AE toxicity grade is less than 2

Scenario E, Output: No CM and AE are not association

Scenario F, Output: No Deleted record

Sorting:
(Defaulted to Country, Site Number and Subject Number if not specified):Country, Site, Subject Number, AE seq#

Parameter Selection:
(Defaulted to Country, Site Number and Subject Number if not specified): Country, Site, Subject Number, encoded drug name, medication class, Standardized medication name, ATC2 Term
