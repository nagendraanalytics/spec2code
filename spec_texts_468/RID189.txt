Report ID: RID189

Report Title:
Study Medication  vs TRIG or and SR or TRIGA

Description:
This report lists subjects who have different information between Cohort /arm /Stratus (entered in SR or TRIG or TRIGA) and Study Medication administered.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. eCRF

6. Seq

7. Study Medication

8. Error Message

Logic:
<Criteria Form1>.<Criteria Itemquestion1> = <Criteria Value1>  AND

<Criteria Form2>.<Criteria Itemquestion2> = <Criteria Value2> AND

<Criteria Form3>.<Criteria Itemquestion3> = <Criteria Value3> AND

This report displays subjects with mismatching and/or missing study medications based on the assigned treatment arm/cohort/stratum.

-Error Message: Mismatch

The study medication on SMAU, SMAB, SM, SM02, STR at a given visit does not match the study medication expected for the arm/cohort/stratum selected on TRIG/SR/TRIGA

Records in erroneous visits should not be considered.

Deleted records should not be considered.

The study medication records on common logs, i.e. SM and SM02, should be assigned to the non-erroneous visit equal or immediately prior to the study med start date (date of visit <= start date of study med < next date of visit or today).

Notes:

1) If visit column on supporting document is completed as NA, check should be done across all entries for the corresponding Formmnemonic and Study Medication

2) Requires Supporting Document to be named SM021_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1. Criteria Form1 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Form Mnemonic SR/SRIVRS/TRIG/TRIGA. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA

2. Criteria Itemquestion1 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Itemquestion1. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Itemquestion1 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

3. Criteria Value1 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Value. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the itemValue1 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

4. Criteria Form2 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Form Mnemonic SR/SRIVRS/TRIG/TRIGA. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, or is not needed enter NA

5. Criteria Itemquestion2 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Itemquestion2. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Itemquestion2 is not applicable for the protocol, or is not needed enter NA

6. Criteria Value2 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Value2. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Value2 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

7. Criteria Form3 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Form Mnemonic SR/SRIVRS/TRIG/TRIGA. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, or is not needed enter NA

8. Criteria Itemquestion3 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Itemquestion3. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Itemquestion3 is not applicable for the protocol, or is not needed enter NA

9. Criteria Value3 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Value3. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Value3 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

10. Visitmnemonic - If check should be done across all visits use NA

11. Formmnemonic

12. Study Medication

The number of rows in the supporting document will be represented in the Report Header as "# of rows for customization".

The actual information provided by the supporting doc can be outputted by selecting Y in the parameter "Supporting doc Page".

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=No (Mismatch)

The Study medication appears according to the visit, eCRF and arm/cohort/stratum as provided by the requestor

2) Output=Yes (Mismatch)

The Study medication appears at a different visit, eCRF and/or arm/cohort/stratum from the schema provided by the requestor

3) Output=No (Mismatch)

Deleted Study medication should not be considered
