Report ID: RID012

Report Title:
Adverse Event and Medical History reconciliation

Description:
This report lists Adverse Events that do not indicate worsened or exacerbated and match a Medical History event that is ongoing

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq

5. AE Verbatim

6. APAE Seq

7. APAE Verbatim

8. MH Seq

9. MH Verbatim

10. APMH Seq

11. APMH Verbatim

12. LLT

13. Associated Partner LLT

Logic:
Output is produced if Adverse Event/Associated Partner Adverse Event LLT matches Medical History/Associated Partner Medical History LLT and  Medical History/Associated Partner Medical History entry indicates "YES" for "Is the medical history disease/condition or event still ongoing?", and AE/APAE verbatim term  does not indicate  a worsening or exacerbation of or increasing or increasing in frequency or increasing episodes or accelerated deteriorating or more bothersome or flare up or aggravated within the term.

LLT should not be null.

Do not output deleted records.

Report Scenarios:
Output is produced if Adverse Event/Associated Partner Adverse Event LLT matches Medical History/Associated Partner Medical History LLT and  Medical History/Associated Partner Medical History  entry indicates "YES" for "Is the medical history disease/condition or event still ongoing?", and AE/APAE verbatim term  does not indicate  a worsening or exacerbation of or increasing or increasing in frequency or increasing episodes or accelerated deteriorating or more bothersome or flare up or aggravated within the term.

LLT should not be null.

Do not output deleted records.
