Report ID: ONC_LSRIR_007_T

Report Title:
Missing LSRIR´s organ location at applicable Current Disease Detail form

Description:
This report identifies lesions present at baseline and reported on the LSRIR form, whose organ locations has not been reported on any of the Location of Metastases at the CDDB form.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Current Disease Details Visit

5. Current Disease Details Form

6. LSRIR Visit Mnemonic

7. Lesion Number

8. Date of Assessment

9. Location

Logic:
Report should output subjects who meet the following criteria:

Logic 1: Location

(Organ) <> blank on LSRIR form with Date of Assessment is prior or equal to min <SM, SMAU, SMAB> start date (ignore time)

and

Logic 2:< There is not a Location of metastases on <CDDB, CDDG, CDDHN, CDDO, CODD, CDDM> form = Location (Organ) (in logic 1).

OR

There is not a Location of metastases at diagnosis on CDDU form = Location (Organ) (in logic 1).>

Note: For locations entered in the field "Other" at LSRIR, verify if the field <"Other Location of Metastases" for <CDDB, CDDHN, CCDO, CCDU, CDDM, CODD>> OR <"Location of Metastases Other" for CDDG> contains the location entered at "Other" LSRIR. No case sensitive for this comparison.

Note: Erroneous visits and deleted records should not be considered in the programming and output.

Note: For logic1, if there are multiple LSRIR record meeting the criteria, please check them all.

Note: Report user needs to specify which forms (CDDB, CDDG, CDDHN, CDDO, CDDU, CDDM, CODD) will be involved at  Form.Item Names and Logic.

Note: Columns 4 and 5 are optional depending on study requirements. LCDM to remove from 'Column Headings' and 'Form.Item Names' if not applicable.

Report Scenarios:
