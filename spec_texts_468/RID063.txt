Report ID: RID063

Report Title:
Death form reconciliation with Subject Disposition

Description:
This report will identify subjects with DEATH form completed and missing or incorrect  DS01 option selected

Column Heading:
1.Country

2.Site

3.Subject Number

4.DS01 Status

Logic:
Logic1: Date of Death in DEATH form is completed   AND

Logic2: DS01 form not initialized or DS01 initialized but ‘Select the Status of the Subject for the Study’ <>Discontinued due to DEATH

Report Scenarios:
A-	Yes

Date of Death in DEATH form is completed and DS01 form not initialized.

B-	Yes

Date of Death in DEATH form is completed and DS01 form is initialized, but item ‘Select the Status of the Subject for the Study’ incomplete.

C-	Yes

Date of Death in DEATH form is completed and DS01 form is initialized, but item ‘Select the Status of the Subject for the Study’ <> Discontinued due to DEATH

D-             No

Date of Death in DEATH form is completed and DS01 form is item ‘Select the Status of the Subject for the Study’ =Discontinued due to DEATH

Sorting:
Country,Site,Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country(ies), Site(s), Subject(s), Randomized only, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1.SA Form. Site Country

2.SA Form. Site Mnemonic

3.SA Form. Subject Number

4.DS01 Form. Select the Status of the Subject for the Study (include discontinue reason)
