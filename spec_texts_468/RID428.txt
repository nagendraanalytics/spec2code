Report ID: RID428

Report Title:
Missing/Incorrect SCTF and SFU information

Description:
This report lists subjects with missing or inconsistent Steam Cell Transplant information between SFU and SCTF.

Column Heading:
For Table 1:

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Mnemonic

5. SFU Seq #

6. SFU Date of Contact

7. SFU Answer

For Tables: 2, 3:

1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Mnemonic

5. SFU Seq #

6. SFU Date of Contact

7. SFU Answer

8. SCTF Seq #

9. Date of Transplant

Logic:
LOGIC: Logic 1: SFU form, 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?' = Yes

AND

Logic 2: There is no at least one entry in SCTF with Date of Transplant before or equal the minimum SFU Date of contact or last attempt of contact in logic 1

OR

Logic 3: SFU form 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?'= Yes

AND

Logic 4: SFU form, 'Date of contact or last attempt of contact' <> the minimum SFU Date of contact or last attempt of contact in logic 3.

AND

Logic 5: There is no an entry in SCTF form with Date of Transplant greater than previous SFU contact and less or equal to current SFU contact in logic 4.

OR

Logic 6: There is at least one entry in SCTF form with Date of Transplant after the last (maximum) (SFU form, 'Date of contact or last attempt of contact')

OR

Logic 7: SFU form, 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?'= No

AND

Logic 8: There is at least one entry in SCTF form with Date of Transplant before or equal the minimum SFU Date of contact or last attempt of contact in logic 7

OR

Logic 9: SFU form 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?'= No

AND

Logic 10: SFU form, 'Date of contact or last attempt of contact' <> the minimum SFU Date of contact or last attempt of contact in logic 9

AND

Logic 11: There is an entry in SCTF form with Date of Transplant greater than previous SFU contact and less or equal to current SFU contact in logic 10.

Note 1: Previous SFU is defined as the previous SFU entry based on dates.

Note 2: Please exclude deleted records and erroneous visits from programming

Note 3: Please exclude all SCTF records from programming if SCTF Date of Transplant is less than max SM/SMAU/SMAB. Start Date with Last Dose is checked or SM04. Start Date recorded.

Note 4: Time can be ignored for the comparison.

Note 5: Date of transplant please use the "Imputed Date of transplant" in SCTF form.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1: A-Yes: Subjects who have 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?' equal to Yes in the minimum SFU contact, but have no entries in the SCTF form before or equal to the minimum SFU contact.

B-Yes: Subjects who have 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?' equal to Yes in the current SFU form, but have no entries in the SCTF between the previous and the current SFU contact.

Scenario 2: C-Yes: Date of contact or last attempt of contact in the last (maximum) SFU is less than start date in SCTF.

Scenario 3: D-Yes: Subjects who have 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?' equal to No in the minimum SFU contact, but have entries in the SCTF form before or equal to the minimum SFU contact.

E-Yes: Subjects who have 'Has the Subject Received a Stem Cell Transplant Since the Last Visit/Phone Contact?' equal to No in the current SFU form, but have entries in the SCTF between the previous and the current SFU contact.

F- No: Correct data entry.
