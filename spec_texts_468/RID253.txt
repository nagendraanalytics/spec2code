Report ID: RID253

Report Title:
BUN/Urea duplicate reconciliation

Description:
This report will list duplicates between Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN) and Urea tests

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. Form

6. Seq#

7. Result/Normal ranges

8. Units

9. Scenario

Logic:
Scenario 1:

Logic 1: Urea and Blood Urea Nitrogen results and units are the same AND entered on the same CLAB row and visit

Scenario 2:

Logic 2: Urea and Blood Urea Nitrogen normal ranges and units are the same AND entered on the same SLCNR entry

Notes:

1.	Do not consider deleted and erroneous visit records

2.	Only output if:

-	CLAB: both the result and units are equal

-	SLCNR: upper and lower ranges and units are equal

3.	For column 7, please display: SLCNR. Test Lower Limit of Norma – SLCNR. Test Upper Limit of Normal

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1

Scenario A: YES

CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Result = CLAB. Urea Result AND CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units = CLAB. Urea Units

Scenario B: NO

CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Result <> CLAB. Urea Result AND CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units = CLAB. Urea Units

Scenario C: NO

CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Result = CLAB. Urea Result AND CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units <> CLAB. Urea Units

Scenario D: NO

CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Result <> CLAB. Urea Result AND CLAB. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units <> CLAB. Urea Units

Scenario 2

Scenario E: YES

SLCNR. Urea Upper Normal Range = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Upper Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Lower Normal Range = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Lower Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Unit = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units

Scenario F: NO

SLCNR. Urea Upper Normal Range <> SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Upper Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Lower Normal Range = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Lower Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Unit = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units

Scenario G: NO

SLCNR. Urea Upper Normal Range = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Upper Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Lower Normal Range <> SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Lower Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Unit = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units

Scenario H: NO

SLCNR. Urea Upper Normal Range = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Upper Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Lower Normal Range = SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Lower Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Unit <> SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units

Scenario I: NO

SLCNR. Urea Upper Normal Range <> SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Upper Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Lower Normal Range <> SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Lower Normal Range AND SLCNR. Urea Unit <> SLCNR. Blood Urea Nitrogen Units

Scenarios J: NO

Do not consider deleted records

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (Y/N set default to N)

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assessment.Country

2. Subject Assessment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subject Assessment.Subject Number

4. Display CLAB visit OR SLCNR display NRL

5. Display SLCNR/CLAB

6. Display SLCNR. Sequence Number/CLAB row#

7. Display duplicate result in CLAB/ Display SLCNR normal ranges as per note 3

8. Display unit

9. Display ‘Duplicate in CLAB’ if scenario 1 fulfills, display ‘Duplicate in SLCNR’ if scenario 2 fulfills
