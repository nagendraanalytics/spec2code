Report ID: RID282

Report Title:
Slide Cut Stability

Description:
This report displays samples with slide stability out of window in GEFCF records

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Screening Number

4. Subject Number

5. Vendor

6. Kit ID

7. Slide Cut Date

8.Slide Stain Date

Logic:
For the same GEFCF.Kit ID:

Logic 1.1:  GEFCF.Test Short Name = SLSTNDT

AND

Logic 1.2:  GEFCF.Test Short Name = SLDCUTDT

AND

Logic 1.3:  GEFCF.Result or Findings in Original Units of SLSTNDT > GEFCF.Result or Findings in Original Units of SLDCUTDT + Window of Days

Notes:

1. Window of Days is the Window of Days specified in parameter.

2. Report should not consider deleted records.

3. If Blinding Flag='B' for the test, then eteh following fields should display 'xxxx': Result or Findings in Original Units.

Report Scenarios:
Output=YES

If Slide Stain Date is more than Window of Days after Slide Cut Date.

Output=NO

If Slide Stain Date is less than or equal to Window of Days after Slide Cut Date.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Randomized Only(Y/N, default to N), Vendor (Textbox, separated by comma)r, Window of Days (default to 0)

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment form.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment form.Site Mnemonic

3. Subject Assignment form.Screening Number

4. Subject Assignment form.Subject Number

5. GEFCF.Vendor Name

6. GEFCF.Kit ID

7. GEFCF.Result or Findings in Original Units when GEFCF.Test Short Name=SLDCUTDT

8. GEFCF.Result or Findings in Original Units when GEFCF.Test Short Name=SLSTNDT
