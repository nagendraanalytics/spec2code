Report ID: RID354

Report Title:
OEDRA Reconciliation with SAE reporting Period.

Description:
This report reconciles data in OEDRA form  depending on SAE Reporting Period (Window of Days) when patient died

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. OEDRA Visit

5. OEDRA Date of assessment

6. OEDRA Status

7. Date/Time of Death

8. Study Medication Last Dose date.

9. Difference of days

10. Scenario

Logic:
Scenario 1:

Logic 1.1: DEATH. Date/Time of Death – MAX SM/SMAB/STR/SMAU. Stop Date/Time <= “Window of days”

AND

Logic 1.2: OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = Death

Scenario 2:

Logic 2.1: Logic 1.1: DEATH.Date/Time of Death – MAX SM/SMAB/STR/SMAU. Stop Date/Time > “Window of days”

AND

Logic 2.2: OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = Adverse Event

Note 1: Please exclude the deleted and erroneous records.

Note 2: For UNK day and month, please consider the first day and first month.

Report Scenarios:
A.- YES: DEATH. Date/Time of Death – MAX SM/SMAB/SMAU/STR. Stop Date/Time <= “Window of days” AND OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = Death

B.- YES: DEATH.Date/Time of Death – MAX SM/SMAB/SMAU/STR. Stop Date/Time > “Window of       days” AND OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = Adverse Event

A.- NO: DEATH. Date/Time of Death – MAX SM/SMAB/SMAU/STR. Stop Date/Time <= “Window of days” AND OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = Adverse Event

B – NO: DEATH. Date/Time of Death – MAX SM/SMAB/SMAU/STR. Stop Date/Time >= “Window of days” AND OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment = Death

C.- NO: OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment <> Death, Adverse Event

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (Y/N set default to N), Window Days (Numeric Value, default as 0)

Form.Item Names:
1. OEDRA. Country

2. OEDRA. Site

3. OEDRA. Subject Number

4. OEDRA. Visit Mnemonic

5. OEDRA. Date of assessment

6. OEDRA. Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment

7. DEATH. Date/Time of Death

8. SM/SMAB/STR/SMAU. Stop Date/Time.

9. Show the result of Date/Time of Death. DEATH – MAX SM/SMAB/STR/SMAU. Stop Date/Time

10. Error Message: If applies logic 1 please display “Scenario 1” If applies logic 2 please display “Scenario 2”
