Report ID: RID275

Report Title:
Imaging data dates not expected in subject transfer.

Description:
This report will output subjects with imaging data in Vendor database after the completion of OEDRA or PMS1A.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Disposition Form Visit

5. Disposition Form

6. Disposition Form Date

7. External data form

8. Seq#

9. Date of external data form

10. Test Name

Logic:
Scenario1: (Date of image compared to End of imaging Form= “Equal, Greater Than”)

[Logic 1.1: imaging external data form = CIMG

AND

Logic 1.2: CIMG. Date of Imaging <> NULL

AND

Logic 1.3: Date in Logic 1.2 >= End of imaging Form. Date of Assessment.

OR

Logic 2.1: imaging external data form = GEFCF

AND

Logic 2.2: GEFCF. Date/Time of Measurement <> NULL

AND

Logic 2.3: Date in Logic 2.2 >= End of imaging Form. Date of Assessment.

Scenario 2:( Date of image compared to End of imaging Form= “Greater Than”)

Logic 1.1: imaging external data form = CIMG

AND

Logic 1.2: CIMG. Date of Imaging <> NULL

AND

Logic 1.3: Date in Logic 1.2 > End of imaging Form. Date of Assessment.

OR

Logic 2.1: imaging external data form = GEFCF

AND

Logic 2.2: GEFCF. Date/Time of Measurement <> NULL

AND

Logic 2.3: Date in Logic 2.2 > End of imaging Form. Date of Assessment.

Notes:

Note 1: Disregard erroneous visits

Note 2: For Logic 1.3 and 2.3 when End of imaging Form is selected as PMS1A use the latest PMS1A. Date of assessment

Note 3: If CIMG is selected on “Imaging external data form”, evaluate logics that starts with 1 (1.1,1.2,1.3) and if GEFCF is selected on “Imaging external data form” section , evaluate logics that starts with 2 (2.1,2.2,2.3)

Note 4:  If “Equal, Greater Than” is selected on “Date of image compared to End of imaging Form” section , evaluate Scenario 1 and if  “Greater Than” is selected on “Date of image compared to End of imaging Form” section evaluate Scenario 2

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A: YES

A CIMG or GEFCF record was received with the following condition

A CIMG or GEFCF record was received with the following condition CIMG. Date of Imaging/GEFCF. Date/Time of Measurement AFTER OEDRA. Date of Assessment. OR PMS1A.Date of Assessment.

Scenario B: NO

A CIMG or GEFCF record was received with the following condition CIMG. Date of Imaging/GEFCF. Date/Time of Measurement BEFORE OEDRA. Date of Assessment. OR PMS1A.Date of Assessment.

Scenario C: NO

Do not consider erroneous visits

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Seq#

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Imaging external data form (CIMG/GEFCF), End of imaging Form (OEDRA/ PMS1A), Date of image compared to End of imaging Form (“Greater Than”, “Equal, Greater Than” Default as “Greater Than”)

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assessment.Country

2. Subject Assessment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subject Assessment.Subject Number

4. Display Disposition Form Visit

5. Display Disposition Form (PMS1A/OEDRA) from logic

6. PMS1A/OEDRA. Date of Assessment

7. External Data form (CIMG/GEFCF)

8. (CIMG/GEFCF). Sequence Number

9. CIMG. Date of Imaging/GEFCF. Date/Time of Measurement

10. GEFCF.Test/CIMG.Test Name
