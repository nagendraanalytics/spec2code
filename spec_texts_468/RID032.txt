Report ID: RID032

Report Title:
Missing Consent Date of Associated Person

Description:
This report will display subjects with missing Consent Date of Associated Person.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. Date of Visit

Logic:
Item Description of Associated Person is entered on APDM form AND Consent Date of Associated Person item is missing

Notes:

1) Erroneous visits should not be included.

2) Display blank for DOV if it is not provided

Report Scenarios:
Yes: Display output that satisfy the logic

No: Do not display output that do not satisfy the logic

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. APDM.Visit Mnemonic

5. Visits. Date of Visit
