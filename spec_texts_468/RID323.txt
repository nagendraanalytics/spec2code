Report ID: RID323

Report Title:
Baseline Index Lesion is Not Measurable

Description:
This report will list subjects who do not have at least one measureable Index Lesion at baseline.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Name

5. SM/SMAB/SMAU

6. First Dose Start Date

7. Lesion Number

8. Location (Organ)

10. Message

Logic:
Logic 1: LSNEI form. Lesion Category = Index

AND

Logic 2: SM/SMAB/SMAU form. Start Date/Time <> NULL and LSNEI form. Date of Lesion Evaluation is within 30 days prior to min SM/SMAB/SMAU form. Start Date/Time with First Dose is selected (Only date is considered)

AND

Logic 3: There is no measurement meet below criteria

LSNEI form. Location (Organ) = Lymph Node and Index Lesion Measurement Dimension 1 and Dimension 2 are greater than or equal to 15 mm

or

LSNEI form. Location (Organ) <> Lymph Node and Index Lesion Measurement Dimension 1 and Dimension 2 are greater than or equal to 10 mm

Please exclude deleted record and erroneous visit from programming.

Note: LCDM need to provide specific timeframe. The default timeframe is 30 days

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A, Output: Yes

Subjects who have a category lesion selected as Index, but there is no measurable baseline Index Lesion performed within 30 days prior to first dose

Scenario B, Output: Yes

Subjects who have a category lesion selected as Index  but do not have at least one LSNEI  Date of Lesion Evaluation within 30 days prior to the First dose Start Date with  the axis measurements that do not meet the following criteria for the baseline

(1) Location (organ) is Lymph Node and Dimension 1 and Dimension 2 is >= 15 mm, or

(2) Location (organ) is not Lymph Node and Dimension 1 and Dimension 2 is >= 10 mm.

Scenario C, Output: No

Lesion Category <> Index

Scenario D, Output: No

Subjects who have a category lesion selected as Index and have at least one LSNEI  Date of Lesion Evaluation within 30 days prior to the First dose Start Date with  the axis measurements meet the following criteria for the baseline

(1) Location (organ) is Lymph Node and Dimension 1 and Dimension 2 is >= 15 mm, or

(2) Location (organ) is not Lymph Node and Dimension 1 and Dimension 2 is >= 10 mm.

Scenario E, Output: No

Deleted record

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Lesion Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Bseline Timeframe, List of Subject

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment form.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects. Subject Number

4. Visit. Visit Mnemonic

5. SM/SMAB/SMAU

6. min SM/SMAB/SMAU. Start Date/Time

7. LESION EVALUATION - IRRC. Lesion Number

8. LESION EVALUATION - IRRC. Location (Organ)

10. Message:

If there is no LSNEI form. Date of Lesion Evaluation is within 30 days prior to min SM/SMAB/SMAU form. Start Date/Time, please display Missing Baseline

If LSNEI form. Date of Lesion Evaluation is within 30 days prior to min SM/SMAB/SMAU form. Start Date/Time, but no measurement meet criteria, please display Incorrect Baseline Assessment.
