Report ID: RID361

Report Title:
Relapse/recurrence reconciliation

Description:
This report will output if there is a ORAJ entry with Disease Recurrence Indicator= Yes there is no a PMS1 or PMST as recurrence for each treatment phase. The report will output if there is a PMS1 or PMST completed as Recurrence/Relapse without a corresponding ORAJ or BPSYA supporting the recurrence

Column Heading:
1.Country

2.Site

3.Subject Number

4.Recurrence Form

5.Seq #

6.Visit Mnemonic

7.Form

8.Subject Status

9.Scenario

Logic:
Scenario 1

There is at least one entry with ORAJ.Disease Recurrence Indicator = Yes

AND

(PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments) <> 'Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence'

Scenario 2

There is at least one entry with BPSYA.Biopsy Results = Positive for Malignancy

AND

(PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments) <> 'Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence'

Scenario 3

(PMS1.Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment/PMST.Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments) = 'Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence' AND

(There is not at least one entry with ORAJ.Disease Recurrence Indicator = Yes OR

There is not at least one entry with BPSYA.Biopsy Results = Positive for Malignancy)

Note 1:

The supporting document is required for all trial if treatment phase is applied to logic even if only one treatment phase is present.

This supporting document is only required to be created once per trial if it was already created and uploaded, there is no need to duplicate it.

The supporting document should be named as study_phase_supp_</Vxxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

One row for each Treatment Phase

1.      Phase Order: required, the order on which phases will appear on protocol

2.      Treatment Phase: required, name of each study phase (Initial Treatment Phase, Neoadjuvant Phase, Second Course Phase

3.      First Visitmnemonic of Treatment Phase: required, the first visit on which treatment is expected to be administered on each phase

e.g.

1, Initial Treatment Phase, Cycle 1

2, Second Course Phase, Cycle 1 Course 2

3, Crossover Phase, Crossover Cycle 1

The actual information provided by optional supporting doc can be outputted by selecting Y in the parameter “Study Phase Page”

Note 2:

The report logic should be checked within the same treatment phase defined in the supporting document.

For example if user provides two Treatment Phases in supporting document like below:

Phase Order, Treatment phase, First Visitmnemonic of Treatment Phase

1,Initial Treatment Phase,Cycle 1

2,Second Course Phase, Secound Course Cycle 1

The treatment phase will be identified as following:

Initial Treatment Phase: Cycle 1 <= Form.Date < Second Course Cycle 1

Second Course Phase: Second Course Cycle 1 <= Form.Date

Form.Date include: PMS1.Date of Assessment, PMST.Date of Assessment, ORAJ.Date of Assessment (Scan/Image/Physical Exam), BPSYA. Date of Biopsy

Note 3: In Logic scenario 3 will output only for the form (BPSYA/ORAJ) available in the study. If both are present, then output will appear for both forms.

Note 4: For Scenario 1, Scenario 2, Scenario 3: Only one form (PMS1 OR PMST) is available for each treatment phase, therefore, the report will use only the form that was completed during each treatment phase. It is not possible to have both forms completed in the same treatment phase.

Note 5: For Column Headings below, if there is any missing data, please leave it blank (Output in Excel cell).

Note 6: For Column 4: “Recurrence Form” display Form Mnemonic of the form used on logic (ORAJ or BPSYA), for example for scenario 1 display “ORAJ”, for scenario 2 display “BPSYA”.

Note 7: For Column 5: “Seq #” if column 4 is ORAJ display ORAJ.Formidx else if column 4 is BPSYA display BPSYA.Formidx.

Note 8:

IF Logic scenario 1 is used, then display “Scenario 1” OR

IF Logic scenario 2 is used, then display “Scenario 2” OR

IF Logic scenario 3 is used, then display “Scenario 3”.

Note 9: Erroneous visits and deleted records are excluded from programming.

Note 10: For Logic Scenario 3 Column headings 4 and 5 (Recurrence Form and Seq #) should be populated as blank.

Report Scenarios:
Report Scenarios: Scenario 1

Yes: there is at least one ORAJ with disease recurrence indicator as Yes and PMS1 Status of Study Medication or PMST Status of Subject at end of all Study treatment is different than Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence'

No: there is at least one ORAJ with disease recurrence indicator as Yes and PMS1 Status of Study Medication or PMST Status of Subject at end of all Study treatment is equal to Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence'

No: All entries in ORAJ disease recurrence indicator as No

Scenario 2

Yes: There is at least one entry with BPSYA Biopsy Results as Positive for Malignancy and PMS1 Status of Study Medication or PMST Status of Subject at end of all Study treatment is different than Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence'

No: There is at least one entry with BPSYA Biopsy Results as Positive for Malignancy and PMS1 Status of Study Medication or PMST Status of Subject at end of all Study treatment is equal to Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence'

No: All entries in BPSYA Biopsy Results are different than Positive for Malignancy

Scenario 3

Yes: PMS1 Status of Study Medication or PMST Status of Subject at end of all Study treatment is equal to Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence' and there is not at least an entry in ORAJ disease recurrence indicator as Yes or BPSYA Biopsy Results as Positive for Malignancy

No: PMS1 Status of Study Medication or PMST Status of Subject at end of all Study treatment is equal to Relapse/Recurrence' or 'Recurrence/Relapse' or 'Recurrence' and there is at least an entry in ORAJ disease recurrence indicator as Yes or BPSYA Biopsy Results as Positive for Malignancy
