Report ID: RID378

Report Title:
Overall Response Reconciliation

Description:
This report will output when for some specific cases, the Overall Response is incorrect based on Soft tissue response, Bone lesion response and Bone lesions in LSNPC

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Lesion Seq

5. Date of Lesion Evaluation

6. Lesion Identification

7. Response Seq

8. Date Response Determined

9. Soft Tissue Response

10. Bone Lesion Response

11. Overall Response

12.  Scenario

Logic:
Scenario 1

There is at least one LSNPC record with LSNPC.Lesion identification = BONE LESION

AND

There is a record in ORPC with Date Response Determined <> Null

AND

ORPC. Bone Lesion Response = “Not Evaluable”

AND

ORPC. Soft Tissue Response <> “Progressive Disease”

AND

ORPC. Overall Response <> “Not Evaluable”

Note: Display Scenario 1 if logic is met, and disregard deleted rows in LSNPC and ORPC.

Scenario 2

There is NOT at least one LSNPC record with LSNPC.Lesion identification = BONE LESION

AND

There is a record in ORPC with Date Response Determined <> Null

AND

ORPC. Soft Tissue Response = ["Stable Disease" OR "Partial Response" OR "Non-CR/Non-PD"]

AND

ORPC. Bone Lesion Response = ["Not Evaluable" OR No Bone Lesions Present]

AND

ORPC. Bone Lesion Response = "Not Evaluable"

AND

ORPC. Overall Response <> ["Stable Disease" OR "Partial Response" OR "Non Progressive Disease"]

Note: Display Scenario 2 if logic is met, and disregard deleted rows in LSNPC and ORPC. Please consider ORPC.Overall Response = Non Progressive Disease equivalent to ORPC.Soft Tissue Response= "Non-CR/Non-PD"

Report Scenarios:
LSNPC, ORPC

Scenario 1:

Output is produced when Bone Lesion Response is NE, there are Bone lesions entered in LSNPC, Soft Tissue Response is not PD but Overall Response is not NE

Scenario 2:

Soft tissue response is either SD, PR or Non-CR/Non-PD, Bone Lesion Response is NE, and there are no Bone lesions entered in LSNPC; however, overall response is not equal to soft tissue response.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (N/Y, default as N)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA Form. Site Country

2. SA Form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA Form. Subject Number

4. LSNPC. Sponsor ID Number

5. LSNPC. Date of Lesion Evaluation

6. LSNPC. Lesion Identification

7. ORPC. Sponsor ID Number

8. ORPC Form. Date Response Determined

9. ORPC Form. Soft Tissue Response

10. ORPC Form. Bone Lesion Response

11. ORPC Form. Overall Response

12. Display “Scenario 1” or “Scenario 2” depending on the logic that is met
