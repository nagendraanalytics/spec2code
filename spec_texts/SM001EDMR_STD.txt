Logic:
This report generates a listing of Study Medication data including Study Medication, Blinding, Scheduled Time, Dose Begin Date/Time, Dose End Date/Time, Dose Amount, Units Taken, Frequency, Location, Route, Formulation, and Reason for Dose Variation.

This report should not display deleted records. Records in erroneous visits should not be considered

The phase and period in which the medication is administered is based upon the Medication Start Date in relation to the Date of Visit.  The timing of any study medication administration at that period is not considered.  The Study Medication will fall into the phase and period where the medication start date is equal to or after the date of visit for that visit, and prior to the date of visit of the next visit. The ‘Period’ field on the Study Medication form will also output in the report to ensure consistency with the ‘Period’ field on the Date of Visit form.
