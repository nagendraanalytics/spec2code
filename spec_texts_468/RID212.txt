Report ID: RID212

Report Title:
Date Mismatch between assessment and visit

Description:
This report will list subjects whose Date of assessment for some clinical procedures do not match with the corresponding visit as expected.

Column Heading:
1.	Country

2.	 Site

3.	Subject Number

4.	Current Visit

5.	Current DOV Date

6.	Next Visit

7.	Next DOV Date

8.	Form

9.	Seq

10.	Item

11.	Form Date

12.	Schedule Time

13.	Window 1 of Days

14.	Window 2 of Days

15.	Scenario

Logic:
According to the values in the ““Supporting document””:

<Criteria Form1>.<Criteria Itemquestion1> = <Criteria Value1>  AND

<Criteria Form2>.<Criteria Itemquestion2> = <Criteria Value2> AND

<Criteria Form3>.<Criteria Itemquestion2> = <Criteria Value2> AND

<Form>.<Scheduled Time> = <Scheduled Time> AND

<Form>.<Date itemquestion> <> NULL

"This report will list subjects with date fields on given “Form” that is entered at initialized visit XX but,

Scenario 1:

1 a. The date is before the Date of Visit (current DOV) + “Window 1 of Days “of visit listed in ““Supporting document””. OR

1 b. The date is after or equal to the next Date of Visit (Next DOV) + “Window 2 of Days” of next visit listed in “Supporting document””.

Scenario 2:

2. The date is before the Date of Visit of visit (current DOV) + Window 1 of Days OR The date is after the Date of Visit of visit (current DOV) + Window  2 of Days

Note:

1. For Scenario 1 AND 2, “Supporting document” column Window 1 of Days AND Window 2 of Days should be a number, without sign should be count as “positive”, with sign “-” should be count as “negative”.

2: Scenario 1b should review only if DOV from the “Next visit” is complete.

3: This scenario 1 should review all “Form” done AND listed in “Supporting document”5: For Scenario 1 AND 2, do not output if DOV is marked as erroneous visit AND/OR DOV is blank AND/OR deleted records.

4. For Scenario 1 AND 2, only the date component will be considered. The time will be disregarded. The evaluation of partial dates (e.g.: day “UNK”) will not consider the windows of days.

5: Output is produced when “Scheduled Time” for the “Form” match with the “Supporting document” for “Form” in the “Visit”. If there is no scheduled time item on the form, the item will be disregarded.

6: If the form Date of visit is within at least one of the windows listed in the supporting document, no output is required. Records in visits without Date of Visit will not be considered.

7: The number of rows in the “Supporting document” will be represented in the Report Header as ""# of rows for customization"".

8: The actual information provided by the “Supporting document” can be outputted by selecting Y in the parameter “"Supporting doc Page"”.

Requires a “Supporting document” to be named “TQ027_supp_XXX.csv” and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1.	Group - numbers that identify the different groups of assessment. If the assessment only has 1 range, please give a different group number, if the assessment has more than 1 range, please give the same group number.

i.e. If two or more tests could be done within the same visit, i.e. complete test within -3 to 	3 days of DOV and 10 to 16 days from DOV, then those rows need to have the same Group 	number

2.	Criteria Form1 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Form Mnemonic SR/SRIVRS/TRIG. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA

3.	 Criteria Itemquestion1 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Itemquestion1. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Itemquestion1 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

4.	 Criteria Value1 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Value. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the itemValue1 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

5.	 Criteria Form2 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Form Mnemonic SR/SRIVRS/TRIG. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, or is not needed enter NA

6.	 Criteria Itemquestion2 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Itemquestion2. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Itemquestion2 is not applicable for the protocol, or is not needed enter NA

7.	 Criteria Value2 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Value2. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Value2 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

8.	 Criteria Form3 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Form Mnemonic SR/SRIVRS/TRIG. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, or is not needed enter NA

9.	Criteria Itemquestion3 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Itemquestion3. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Itemquestion3 is not applicable for the protocol, or is not needed enter NA

10.	 Criteria Value3 - Arm/Cohort/Stratification Value3. If applies for all subjects regardless the Arm/Cohort/Stratification, enter NA OR if the Value3 is not applicable for the protocol, enter NA

11.	Visit Mnemonic- Visit mnemonics from DOV form. Should not be Null.

12.	Next Visit Mnemonic – Next Visit mnemonic that should be considered for Scenario 1. For Scenario 2 enter NA

13.	Form - corresponding to the appropriate eCRFs.

14.	Scheduled Time - Scheduled Time name from each form. If not required, please provide it as NA.

15.	Date Itemquestion - Corresponding to the items on the formmnemonic previously selected.

16.	 Window 1 of Days - Unique value in each group. Provide the minimum number of days expected per protocol that assessment is allowed to be performed before or DOV. This apply for Scenario 1 and Scenario 2 .Number can be positive (e.g.: 3) or negative (e.g.: -3), according to protocol design

17.	Window 2 of Days - Unique value in each group. Number can be positive (e.g.: 3) or negative (e.g.: -3), according to protocol design. For Scenario 1, this column corresponds to the expected window for the next DOV Visit. For Scenario 2, provide the maximum number of days expected per protocol that assessment is allowed to be performed after or DOV.

e.g.

For Scenario1: If the assessment is part of a visit when it is within -3 days before Current DOV and -4 days before Next DOV visit:

-	use Window 1 of Days: -3; Window 2 of Days: -4

For Scenario 2: If the assessment can be provided -3 to 3 days and 10 to 16 days from DOV:

-	use Window 1 of Days: -3; Window 2 of Days: -3, and  Window 1 of Days: 10; Window 2 of Days: 16

18.	Scenario – “1” for Scenario 1 or “2” for Scenario 2

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1

1) Output=YES - Scenario 1a Date on “Form” < Current Date of Visit +  window 1 days given in “Supporting document” for the Current Visit

1) Output=YES - Scenario 1b Date on “Form” >= Next Date of Visit + window 2 days given in “Supporting document” for the next Date of Visit

2) Output=NO - Scenario 1 Date on “Form” >= Date of Visit of the visit where the record has been entered - Window 1 of Days AND Date on required “Form” < Next Date of Visit window 1 + window 2 days of next visit, according to the “Supporting document”.

3) Output=NO The Date of Visit where the “Form” record has been entered is null OR marked as erroneous.

4) Output=NO “Form” is not required in the visit according to the “Supporting document”.

5) Output=No-Scenario 1 is Scheduled Time on Form <> Scheduled Time reported in the “Supporting document”.

6) Output=No – If scenario is not included in the “Supporting document”.

Scenario 2

1) Output=YES - Scenario 2 Date on “Form” < Date of Visit of the visit where the record has been entered + Lower Window of Days YY, according to the “Supporting document”.

2) Output=YES - Scenario 2 Date on “Form” > Date of Visit of the visit where the record has been entered + Upper Window of Days ZZ, according to the “Supporting document”.

3) Output=YES - Scenario 2 Date on “Form” >= Date of Visit of the visit where the record has been entered + Lower Window of Days AND Date on required “Form” <= Date of Visit of the visit where the record has been entered + Upper Window of Days AND “Form” scheduled time <> Scheduled time specified in the “Supporting document”.

4) Output=NO - Scenario 2 Date on “Form” >= Date of Visit of the visit where the record has been entered + Lower Window of Days and Date on required “Form” <= Date of Visit of the visit where the record has been entered + Upper Window of Days

6) Output=NO The Date of Visit where the “Form” record has been entered is null OR marked as erroneous.

7) Output=NO “Form” is not required in the visit according to the “Supporting document”.

8) Output=No – If scenario is not included in the “Supporting document”.
