Report ID: RID156

Report Title:
Incorrect PMS1

Description:
This report lists subjects who have duplicate  PMS1 selected

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. Form

6. Trial Segment

7. Status

8. Status Value

Logic:
Report will output the subjects who meet following criteria.

Logic 1: For “Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment” in PMS1 and PMS1 variation forms across all visits which is not null have same 'trial segment reference' entered

OR

Logic2: For “Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment” in PMS1 and PMS1 variation forms across all visits which is not null have same 'trial segment reference' entered

OR

Logic3: For “Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment” in PMS1 and PMS1 variation forms across all visits which is not null have same 'trial segment reference' entered

Logic4: For “Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments” in PMST forms across all visits which is not null have same 'trial segment reference' entered

Note1: Erroneous visit and deleted data should be excluded from programming

Note2: Duplicate “Trial Segment Reference” must be output for each Logic. If some PMS1 and PMS1 variation forms meet more than one logic, then output them all. Ex. PMS1 in V1 and PMS1A in V2 meet logic 1 and logic 2 together, output PMS1 and PMS1A twice, and only display the corresponding “status” and “trial segment” in layout.

Report Scenarios:
A-Yes

There are more than one  'trial segment reference' for each “Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment” or “Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment “or “Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment”  in PMS1 forms or "Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments" in PMST forms across all visits.

B- No

There are more only  one  'trial segment reference' for each “Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment” or “Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment “or “Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment”  in PMS1 forms or "Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments" in PMST forms  across all visits.

C- No

No PMS1/PMST form initialized.

D-No

Erroneous visit

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Form(PMST, PMS1, PMS1A, PMS1B, etc),  Trial Segment, Visit order per Inform, Status

Parameter Selection:
Country(ies), Site(s) and Subject(s), Randomized Only, List of Subjects

Form.Item Names:
1. SA Form. Site Country

2. SA Form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA Form. Subject Number

4. PMS1 or variation Form or PMST Form. Visit Mnemonic

5. PMS1 or variation Form or PMST Form. Form Mnemonic

6. PMS1 or variation Form or PMST Form. Trial Segment reference

7. Hardcode the field name of “Status” per Logic: “Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment” or “Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment” or “Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment” or "Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments"

8. Display corresponding data value of “status” per Logic:

PMS1 or variation Form. Status of Study Medication at the End of This Trial Segment or

Status of Subject at End of This Trial Segment or

Select the Status of the Subject for the Next Trial Segment or PMST Form. Status of Subject at end of all Study Treatments
