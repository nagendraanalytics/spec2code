Report ID: RID115

Report Title:
Date/Time of Measurement entered after End Date/Time of Collection at GEFCF

Description:
This report displays all GEFCF records when Date/Time of Measurement is entered after End Date/Time of Collection at GEFCF.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Vendor

5. Specimen ID

6. Kit ID

7. Date/Time of Measurement

8. Date/Time of Collection

Logic:
Output is produced if patient has a GEFCF record with the field Date/Time of Measurement completed with a value greater than the value entered in End Date/Time of Collection.

Date/Time of Measurement > End Date/Time of Collection (both of these dates are completed)

Notes:

CDM Contact central vendor with output and request their research as necessary.

Do not consider deleted records or records with completion status is 'NOT DONE'.

1. If both of dates have time, please consider time

2. If any of dates don't have time, please calculate 00:00:00 to compare

Report Scenarios:
1）Output=Yes

Date/Time of Measurement > End Date/Time of Collection

2) Output=No

Date/Time of Measurement <= End Date/Time of Collection

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, List of subjects, Vendor(s)

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. GENERIC EXTERNAL FINDINGS Form. Vendor Name

5. GEFCF.Specimen ID

6. GEFCF.KITID

7. GEFCF.Date/Time of Measurement

8. GEFCF.Date/Time of Collection
