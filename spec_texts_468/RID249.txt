Report ID: RID249

Report Title:
Brain Metastasis History/Assessment Reconciliation

Description:
The report displays missing or incorrect BMH/BMA records.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. BMA/BMH Imaging Date/BMH Date of Assessment

5. BMA/BMH Brain Metastases?

6. BMH Brain Previous Metastases?

7. BMH Brain Current Metastases?

8. OSCN Sequence

9. OSCN Date of Scan

10. OSCN Location

11. Lesion Evaluation form

12. Lesion Evaluation form sequence

13. Lesion Evaluation form date

14. Lesion Evaluation form location

15. Scenario

Logic:
Report Logic:

Scenario 1

(BMH.Imaging Date <> Null AND BMH.Brain Metastasis = “YES”

OR

BMA.Brain Imaging Date <> Null AND BMA.Brain Metastases = “YES”)

AND

There is not at least 1 record in OSCN with Location or Anatomical Location entered as = Brain/Neck/Head and OSCN. Scheduled Time = "Scheduled"

Scenario 2:

There is at least 1 record in OSCN with Location or Anatomical Location entered as = Brain/Neck/Head and OSCN. Scheduled Time = "Scheduled" and with OSCN.Scan Date within "Timeframe" window.

AND

BMH.Imaging Date OR BMA.Brain Imaging Date <> OSCN.Date.

Scenario 3:

(BMH.Imaging Date <> Null AND BMH.Brain Metastasis = "No"

OR

BMA.Imaging Date <> Null AND BMA.Brain Metastases = "No")

AND

There is a record in Lesion Evaluation form with LSN. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) within "Timeframe" window and Location = "Brain"

Note 1: "X" value = Baseline window of days

Note 2: Timeframe = from BMH.Imaging Date - X to SR.Date of assessment

Note 3: Baseline window of days is inclusive, for example if user enters 7 days, then report would check for records with a date difference 7 days or less

Note 4: Items Evaluation form - Date - Location for scenario 3 will be retrieved from LSN global supp doc

Note 5: please exclude deleted records

Note 6: BMH.Brain Metastasis and BMA.Brain Metastases means following item questions, depends on which one is presented on the trial:

BMH.Does The Subject Have A Previous History Of Brain Metastasis(es)?

BMH.Does The Subject Have A Current Diagnosis Of Brain Metastasis(es)?

BMH.Does The Subject Now Have A Brain Metastasis(es) Or Have A History Of A Brain Metastasis(es)?

BMA.Does the Subject have a history of Brain Metastases or Now have a Brain Metastases

Note 7: Use BMH.Date of Assessment instead if BMH.Imaging Date does not present for the trial

Report Scenarios:
1.1 Output = Yes:

There is no record in OSCN  with location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck', however the BMH form is completed with Does the Subject Have a Previous History of Brain Metastasis(es)? As yes

or

There is no record in OSCN  with Location or Anatomical Location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck', however the BMA form is completed with Does the Subject Have a History of Brain Metastases or Now Have a Brain Metastases? As yes

1.2 Output = No

There is no record in OSCN  with location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck', however the BMH form is completed with Does the Subject Have a Previous History of Brain Metastasis(es)? As no

or

There is no record in OSCN  with Location or Anatomical Location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck', however the BMA form is completed with Does the Subject Have a History of Brain Metastases or Now Have a Brain Metastases? As no

2.1  Output = Yes

'Imaging Date' or 'Date of Assessment' in the BMH form is different than 'Scan Date' for a  OSCN record with Location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck' scheduled within timeframe of baseline.

or

'Imaging Date in' in the BMA form is different than 'Scan Date' for a OSCN record with Anatomical Location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck' scheduled within timeframe of baseline.

2.2 Output = No

'Imaging Date' or 'Date of Assessment' in the BMH form is different than 'Scan Date' for a  OSCN record with Location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck' unscheduled or not within timeframe of baseline.

or

‘Brain Imaging Date in' in the BMA form is different than 'Scan Date' for a  OSCN record with Anatomical Location 'Brain' or 'Head' or 'Neck' unscheduled or not within timeframe of baseline.

3.1 Output = Yes.

'Does the Subject Now have a Brain Metastasis(es) or Have a History of a Brain Metastasis(es)?' in the BMH form is answered as 'No', but there is a record in Response form with Location 'Brain' is present and within Timeframe date.

or

‘Does the Subject Have a History of Brain Metastases or Now Have a Brain Metastases?’ in the BMA form is answered as 'No', but there is a record in evaluation form LSNIR with Location 'Brain' is present and within Timeframe date.

3.2 Output = No

'Does the Subject Now have a Brain Metastasis(es) or Have a History of a Brain Metastasis(es)?' in the BMH form is answered as 'Yes', or there is a record in Response form

or

‘Does the Subject Have a History of Brain Metastases or Now Have a Brain Metastases?’ in the BMA form is answered as 'Yes', or there is a record in evaluation form LSNIR with Location different from 'Brain' is present and within Timeframe date.
