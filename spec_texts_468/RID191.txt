Report ID: RID191

Report Title:
Missing comment for reason for dose variation as Other

Description:
This report will output subjects that have "Other" as reason for dose variation but a bubble comment is not provided.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. eCRF

6. Seq

Logic:
This report displays SM/SMAU/SMAB records with Reason for Dose Variation="Other" and with is no item-level comment.

Item-level comments on other items or form-level comments are not considered.

Deleted records should not to be considered.

Records in erroneous visits should not be considered.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=YES

SM/SMAU/SMAB Reason for Dose Variation="Other" and

No comment entered on this record

2) Output=NO

SM/SMAU/SMAB Reason for Dose Variation="Other" and

There is at least one comment on this record

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, VISITORDER, eCRF, Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject, Randomized Only, List of Subjects, Visit, eCRF

Form.Item Names:
1. Subject Assignment.Site Country

2. Subject Assignment form.Site Mnemonic

3. Subjects.Subject Number

4. SM/SMAU/SMAB form.Visitmnemonic

5. SM/SMAU/SMAB form.Formmnemonic

6. SM/SMAU/SMAB form.Formidx
