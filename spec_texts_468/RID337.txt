Report ID: RID337

Report Title:
Duplicate NATS

Description:
This report will list duplicate NATS records.

Column Heading:
1.	Country

2.	Site

3.	Subject Number

4.	NATS Visit

5.	NATS Seq #

6.	Date of Assessment

7.	Associated Sponsor ID Number

8.	NATS Study Treatment Not Received

Logic:
Logic:

There is more than one entry of NATS record with the same Study Treatment Not received across within visits for the same patient.

Note 1. Do not consider deleted records and erroneous visit.

Note 2. Do not consider blank records.

Note 3. If NATS is set as a common form within a study then display "CCRF" on NATS visit column.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A: Yes

There is more than one entry of NATS with same Study Treatment not received for one patient

Scenario B: No

There is more than one entry of NATS with different Study Treatment not received for one patient

Scenario C: Yes

There is more than one entry of NATS with same Study Treatment not received within Add entry section of a patient.

Scenario D: No

There is more than one entry of NATS with different Study Treatment not received within Add entry section of a patient

Scenario E: No

There is no NATS entered

Scenario F: No

There is only one entry of NATS completed

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Study Treatment Not received, Visit, Seq

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number
