Report ID: RID342

Report Title:
Study Medication First Dose date vs Stop Date of Oncologic Regimen

Description:
This report  lists subjects where Stop Date of Regimen/Stop date of Medication is after MIN Start Date of First Dose of Study Medication or allowed window per protocol.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit Mnemonic

5. SM Form

6. SM Seq

7. First Dose Date

8. ODB Seq

9. Stop Date of Regimen/Stop Date Medication

Logic:
Logic 1: SM/SM01/SMAB/SMAU.Please Indicate if this is the First Dose for this Label Description is selected

AND

Logic 2: SM/SM01/SMAB/SMAU.Min Start Date/Time -  (ODB/ODBCS/ODBH/ODBHN.Stop Date of Regimen or Maximum ODB/ODBCS/ODBH/ODBHN.Stop date of medication)  <= Days Elapsed prompt

Note 1: Deleted records and erroneous visits should not be taken into account.

Note 2:

ODB/ODBCS/ODBH/ODBHN.Stop Date of Regimen is form main table,

ODB/ODBCS/ODBH/ODBHN.Stop Date of Medication is form add entry section.

For trials that has both Stop Date of Regimen/Stop date of medication, please use Stop Date of Medication.

For the trial only has Stop Date of Regimen, please use Stop Date of Regimen

For the trial only has Stop Date of Medication, please use Stop Date of Medication.

Note 3: Use "Imputed Stop Date of Regimen" or "Imputed Stop Date of Medication" in logic 2.

Report Scenarios:
A – Yes

First Dose of Study Medication is prior or equal to the Stop Date of Regimen/Maximum Stop date of Medication for any Oncologic Drug treatment.

B – No

First Dose of Study Medication is greater than Stop Date of Regimen/Maximum Stop date of Medication for any Oncologic Drug treatment.

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects,Country, Randomized only (Yes/No), Days Elapsed (Tex Box Default to Zero)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA.Country

2. SA.Site

3. SA.Subject number

4. SM/SM01/SMAB/SMAU.Visit Mnemonic

5. SM/SM01/SMAB/SMAU.Form name

6. SM/SM01/SMAB/SMAU.Formidx

7. SM/SM01/SMAB/SMAU.Start Date/Time (display only date)

8. ODB/ODBCS/ODBH/ODBHN.Formidx/Sponsor ID Number

9. ODB/ODBCS/ODBH/ODBHN.Imputed Stop Date of Regimen/Stop date of medication
