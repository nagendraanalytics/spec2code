Report ID: RID305

Report Title:
Lesion Evaluation Method Reconciliation

Description:
The report displays subjects who have inconsistencies in methods of assessment in a lesion.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Form Mnemonic

5. Lesion Number

6. Initial Date of Lesion Evaluation

7. Initial Method of Assessment

8. Date of Lesion Evaluation

9. Method of Assessment

Logic:
Please output:

Logic 1: There is a Date of Lesion Form for This Trial (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) / Date of Assessment in the Lesion Evaluation Form equal to Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) / Date of Lesion Evaluation

AND

Logic 2: There is a new Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) / Date of Lesion Evaluation on the Lesion Evaluation entry in Logic 1. (New entry means Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) / Date of Lesion Evaluation in Logic 2 is greater than Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) / Date of Lesion Evaluation in Logic 1)

AND

Logic 3: Method of Assessment of Lesion Evaluation entry of Logic 2 is different than method of assessment of Lesion Evaluation Entry of Logic 1.

Note 1: Deleted record will not be considered in the programming or output.

Note 2: Lesion Evaluation Form = Pulled from Global Supp doc LSN_Supp_Doc depending on the form present in the study

Note 3: If Scheduled time = Unscheduled, do not take into account in the programming or output.

Note 4: The associated Lesion Number in Logic 1 and Logic 2 should be the same.

Note 5: Please consider PET/CT, PET/CT Scan, Spiral CT and CT Scan as the same Method of Assessment.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A; Output: Yes

Method of Assessment is different to initial Method of Assessment

Scenario B; Output: No

Method of Assessment is equal to initial Method of Assessment

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Form Mnemonic, Lesion Number, Date of Lesion Evaluation

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (N/Y, default as N)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA. Country

2. SA. Site Mnemonic

3. SA. Subject Number

4. Lesion evaluation form

5. Lesion evaluation form. Lesion number or Sponsor ID Number if present

6. Lesion evaluation form. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) / Date of Lesion Evaluation of Lesion Evaluation Entry of Logic 1

7. Lesion evaluation form. Method of Assessment of Lesion Evaluation Entry of Logic 1

8. Lesion evaluation form. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) / Date of Lesion Evaluation of Lesion Evaluation Entry of Logic 2

9. Lesion evaluation form. Method of Assessment of Lesion Evaluation Entry of Logic 2
