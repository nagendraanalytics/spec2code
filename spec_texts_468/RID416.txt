Report ID: RID416

Report Title:
Overall Oncologic Response missing

Description:
This report displays subjects who have the Overall response items missing or have a section partially completed.

Column Heading:
1.Country

2.Site

3.Subject Number

4.Response form

5.Sequence

6.Date Response Determined

Logic:
Scenario 1:

Logic 1: Date Response Determined is completed AND Both Overal Response items are blank

Note 1: for ORPI form items are: "Overall Response (PCWG-Modified RECIST)" and "Overall Response (PCWG-Modified irRECIST)". for RRIR items are: "Overall RECIST Response" and "Overall IRRECIST Response".

Scenario 2:

Logic 2: Overal Response item is completed but at least one of the items in the corresponding section is blank.

Note 2: for ORPI form when "Overall Response (PCWG-Modified RECIST)" is completed then items "Non-Target Lesion Response", "Target Lesion Response", "Are There At Least 2 New Bone Lesions On The Bone Scan That Were Not

Present At Baseline/Re-Baseline?", "Bone Lesion Response", "New Lesions Since Last Evaluation" should be completed.

Note 3: for ORPI form when "Overall Response (PCWG-Modified irRECIST)" is completed then items "Non-Target Lesion irRECIST Response", "Target Lesion irRECIST Response", "Bone Lesion Response", "New irRECIST Lesions Since Last Evaluation", "Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline"  should be completed.

Note 4: For RRIR form when "Overall RECIST Response" is completed then items "Non-Target Lesion RECIST Response", "Target Lesion RECIST Response", "New RECIST Lesions Since Last Evaluation", should be completed.

Note 5: For RRIR form when "Overall IRRECIST Response" is completed then items "Non-Target Lesion (Identified At Baseline)

irRECIST Response", "Target Lesion (Identified at Baseline) irRECIST Response", "New irRECIST Lesions Since Last Evaluation", "Status of Prior irRECIST New Lesions Identified Since Baseline" should be completed.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1: Output YES --- EC

in forms ORPI, RRIR Both Overall RECIST and IRRECIST response are blank.

Scenario 2: Output YES

in forms  ORPI, RRIR Overall RECIST response is completed AND at least one of the items in the corresponding section is blank

OR

in forms ORPI, RRIR Overal IRRECIST response is completed AND at least one of the items in the corresponding section is blank

Scenario 3: Output Yes

Both Overall RECIST and IRRECIST response are completed

Scenario 4: Output No

only one between Overall RECIST and IRRECIST response are completed and corresponding section is completed.

Scenario 5: Output No

All items in the RECIST section are completed

Scenario 6: Output No

All items in the IRECIST/IRRECIST section are completed

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Randomized only, Country, Site Number, Subject Number, List of Subjects, Reponse Form

Form.Item Names:

2. SA.Site Mnemonic

3. SA.Subject Number

4. ORPI/RRIR

5. ORPI/RRIR. Sponsor ID Number

6. ORPI/RRIR. Date Response Determined
