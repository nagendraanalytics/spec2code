Report ID: RID161

Report Title:
Missing PRG02

Description:
This report outputs subjects who have the Current Pregnancy Outcome section on PRG01 completed, but PRG02 has not been completed after estimated date of delivery occurred.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Current PRG01 Seq

5. Current Estimated Conception Date

6. Current Estimated Delivery Date

7. Days Elapsed from Current Estimated Delivery Date

8. Have next  PRG01 record? (Y/N)

9. Next PRG01 with Min Estimated Conception Date

10. Next PRG01 Seq

Logic:
This report is to output:

In PRG01 form, Current Pregnancy Outcome section, there is/are record(s) with 'Estimated Date of Conception' and  'Estimated Delivery Date'  are not Null

AND

Scenario 1-

There is No 'next PRG01 record' for  'Current  PRG01 record'.

AND

In PRG02 form, there is NO PRG02 record with 'Date of Delivery/Termination' between Current PRG01 record 'Estimated date of conception' (not inclusive)  and Current PRG01 record Estimated Delivery Date+4 weeks (inclusive)

OR

Scenario 2-

There is at least one 'next PRG01 record' for  'Current  PRG01 record'.

AND

In PRG02 form, there is NO PRG02 record with 'Date of Delivery/Termination' between the Current PRG01 record 'Estimated date of conception'  (not inclusive) and the minimum estimated date of conception(inclusive) from all next PRG01 record.

Please program the report when system date> Current PRG01 'Estimated delivery date + 4 weeks'

* 'Next PRG01 record' definition: PRG01 record with Estimated Conception Date that is between current PRG01 Estimated Conception(not inclusive)Date and Current PRG01 Estimated Delivery Date (inclusive).

* Don't include deleted record into programming

* Please use calendar day to calculate.

* In PRG01 form, if day is UNK in Estimated Date of Conception, assume day is the 15th and then perform the calculation.

* In PRG02 form, if day is UNK in Date of Delivery/Termination, assume day is the 15th and then perform the calculation. If Month is UNK, please don't include the record into programming.

Report Scenarios:
A-No

Estimated Delivery Date=Null or Estimated Date of Conception=Null

B1-No

There is No 'next PRG01 record' for  'Current  PRG01 record'.

AND

In PRG02 form, there is PRG02 record with 'Date of Delivery/Termination' between Current PRG01 record 'Estimated date of conception' (not inclusive)  and Current PRG01 record Estimated Delivery Date+4 weeks (inclusive)

C1-Yes

There is No 'next PRG01 record' for  'Current  PRG01 record'.

AND

In PRG02 form, there is NO PRG02 record with 'Date of Delivery/Termination' between Current PRG01 record 'Estimated date of conception' (not inclusive)  and Current PRG01 record Estimated Delivery Date+4 weeks (inclusive)

B2-No

There is at least one 'next PRG01 record' for  'Current  PRG01 record'.

AND

In PRG02 form, there is  PRG02 record with 'Date of Delivery/Termination' between the Current PRG01 record 'Estimated date of conception'  (not inclusive) and the minimum estimated date of conception(inclusive) from all next PRG01 record.

C2-Yes

There is at least one 'next PRG01 record' for  'Current  PRG01 record'.

AND

In PRG02 form, there is NO PRG02 record with 'Date of Delivery/Termination' between the Current PRG01 record 'Estimated date of conception'  (not inclusive) and the minimum estimated date of conception(inclusive) from all next PRG01 record.

D-No

System date <=PRG01 'Estimated delivery date + 4 weeks'
