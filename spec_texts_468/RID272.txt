Report ID: RID272

Report Title:
Dates inconsistencies in Tumor Tissue and/or Biomarkers information between TSIF and GEFCF

Description:
This report will output inconsistencies present within dates reported in TSIF and GEFCF for Tumor Tissue and/or Biomarker information

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Screening Number

4. Subject Number

5. Inform Visit Name

6. Sequence Number

7. TSIF Item Name

8. TSIF Date

9. GEFCF Visit Name

10. GEFCF Sequence Number

11. GEFCG Requisition Number

12. GEFCF Category

13. GEFCF Subcategory

14. GEFCF Test Name

15. GEFCF Method of Test or Examination

16. GEFCF Date

17. SUPQUAL

18. SUPQUAL - B

19. Scenario

Logic:
LOGIC

Logic 1

Logic 1.1: TSIF Inform Visit Mnemonic = Inform Visit Mnemonic

AND

Logic 1.2: TSIF Date Itemquestion = TSIF Date Itemquestion form supp doc

AND

Logic 1.3: GEFCF. Vendor Name = Vendor from supp doc

AND

Logic 1.4: GEFCF.Visit = Visit ID or Number form supp doc

AND

Logic 1.5: GEFCF Test Name = GEFCF Test Name form supp doc

AND

Logic 1.6: TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date OR Sample Taken Imputed Date OR Date Sample Taken= GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement

AND

Logic 1.7: There is no GEFCF meet below criteria: TSIF Date Itemquestion.Date = GEFCF.Result or Finding in Original Units

Logic 2

Logic 2.1: TSIF Inform Visit Mnemonic = Inform Visit Mnemonic

AND

Logic 2.2: TSIF Date Itemquestion = TSIF Date Itemquestion form supp doc

AND

Logic 2.3: GEFCF. Vendor Name = Vendor from supp doc

AND

Logic 2.4: GEFCF.Visit = Visit ID or Number form supp doc

AND

Logic 2.5: GEFCF Test Name = GEFCF Test Name form supp doc

AND

Logic 2.6: TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date OR Sample Taken Imputed Date OR Date Sample Taken = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement

AND

Logic 2.7: TSIF Date Itemquestion. Date > GEFCF.Result or Finding in Original Units

Note 1: Please exclude deleted record and erroneous visit from programming. Note 2: Report should use Date Specimen Obtained/Date and Time Specimen Obtained/etc. as item present for each study. Note 3: By default, TSIF Date Itemquestion for Scenario 1 = ‘Site Slide Sectioning Date’ and for Scenario 2 = ‘Date/Time Specimen Placed in Fixative’ OR ‘Date/Time Specimen Placed in Formalin’ depending on the study design, unless other is specified by the LCDM Note 4: By default, GEFCF Test Name for Scenario 1 = ‘Slide Cut Date’ and for Scenario 2 = ‘Date/Time Specimen Removed From Formalin’ OR ‘Date/Time Tissue Removed From Formalin’ depending on the study design, unless other is specified by the LCDM Note 5: By default, GEFCF Visit Name = ‘Screening’, GEFCF Visit ID or Number = 1, unless other is specified by the LCDM

Requires supporting document to be named ONC_EVD_009_supp_.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1 row for each visit where TSIF samples should be collected per protocol

1. TSIF Date Itemquestion - TSIF Date Itemquestion

2. Inform Visit Mnemonic - Inform Visit Mnemonic

3. GEFCF Test Name - GEFCF Test Name

4. GEFCF Visit Name - GEFCF Visit Mnemonic

5. Vendor - GEFCF Vendor or NA, Specified as 'NA' if logic is applicable for all Vendors

6. GEFCF Visit ID or Number - GEFCF Visit ID or Number

7. SUPQUAL - Itemquestion of SUPQUAL or 'NA'

8. SUPQUALB - Itemquestion of SUPQUAL or 'NA'

9. Scenario - '1' or '2'

Note 6: SUPQUAL and SUPQUALB are optional values, please enter ‘NA’ when the items are not needed.

Note 7: Inform Visit Mnemonic is equivalent to GEFCF Visit Name example: Inform Visit Mnemonic = Cycle 4 Day 15 GEFCF Visit Name = C4D15 Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name = Cycle 4 Day 15 = C4D15

TSIF Date Itemquestion,Inform Visit Mnemonic,GEFCF Test Name,GEFCF Visit Name,Vendor,GEFCF Visit ID or Number,SUPQUAL,SUPQUALB,Scenario

Site Slide Sectioning Date,Screening,Slide Cut Date,Screening,Q2LAB,1,SUPQUAL1,SUPQUAL2,1

Site Slide Sectioning Date,Surgical Visit,Q2LAB,Slide Cut Date,Surgical Visit,57,SUPQUAL1,SUPQUAL2,1

Date/Time specimen placed in fixative,Screening,Q2LAB,Date/Time specimen removed from formalin,Screening,1,NA,NA,2

The number of rows in the supporting document will be represented in the Report Header as "# of rows for customization". The actual information provided by the supporting doc can be outputted by selecting Y in the parameter "Supporting doc Page".

Note 8. For TSIF.Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date OR Sample Taken Imputed Date in Logic 1.6 and Logic 2.6, when day and month are UNK then assume the actual date is the first day of the first of month of the year. i.e. UNK-UNK-1988 AS 01-Jan-1988. When only the day is UNK then assume the actual date is the first day of the present month of the year. i.e. UNK-NOV-1988 AS 01-NOV-1988.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario 1: For logic 1 Output = No Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date <> GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND TSIF Date Itemquestion = null OR completed as unknown, not applicable or not done Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND GEFCF Test Name Result or Finding in Original Units = null Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND TSIF Date Itemquestion matches with at least one GEFCF Test Name Result or Finding in Original Units

Scenario 2: For logic 2 Output = No Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date <> GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND TSIF Date Itemquestion = null OR completed as unknown, not applicable or not done Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND GEFCF Test Name Result or Finding in Original Units = null Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND TSIF Date Itemquestion is on or before GEFCF Test Name Result or Finding in Original Units

Scenario 3: For Logic 1 Output = Yes Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND TSIF Date Itemquestion does not match with at least one GEFCF Test Name Result or Finding in Original Units

Scenario 4: For Logic 2 Output = Yes Inform Visit Mnemonic = GEFCF Visit Name AND TSIF Date Specimen Obtained OR TSIF Date and Time Specimen Obtained OR  TSIF Date of Tissue Biopsy/Resection Imputed Date = GEFCF Date/Time of Measurement AND TSIF Date Itemquestion is after GEFCF Test Name Result or Finding in Original Units
