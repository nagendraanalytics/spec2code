Report ID: RID240

Report Title:
Reconciliation between AE and  DS01 information related to Malignant Neoplasm Progression terms

Description:
This report will provide output based on “The subject discontinued study participation due to this event = yes” if applicable, Adverse events reported as “Malignant Neoplasm Progression" and toxicity grade in AE form but the corresponding DS01 option is not selected.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq

5. AE Term

6. AE LLT

7. AE End Date

8. Discontinue due to this AE

9. Overall Toxicity

10. Outcome of AE

11. DS01 Status of the Subject

12. Date of Disposition

13. Error Message

Logic:
Requires supporting document to be named ONC_AE_004_supp_<xxxxxxx>.csv and will contain the following columns and associated content:

1 row for each Scenario and expected Reason subject discontinued from protocol in DS01

1. Scenario

2. Reason subject discontinued from protocol

If all available options in Reason subject discontinued from protocol are applicable for any scenario, please enter ‘NA’ in column #2.

If any scenario is not applicable for the study, please do not include it in the supporting doc.

e.g.

Scenario    Discontinued Reason

Scenario 1   Adverse Event

Scenario 2    Death

Sceanrio 2    Lost to Follow-Up

Scenario 2    Withdrawal by subject

Scenario 3    NA

Sceanario 4    Adverse Event

Sceanario 5    Death

Sceanario 5    Adverse Event

The number of rows in the supporting document will be represented in the Report Header as "# of rows for Supporting doc".

The actual information provided by the supporting doc can be outputted by selecting Y in the parameter "Supporting doc Page".

Logic 1: AE.LLT <> Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND AE.The subject discontinued study participation due to this event is checked AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Options provided in supporting doc for Scenario 1 OR AE. Outcome of AE <> Fatal

Logic 2: AE.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND AE.The subject discontinued study participation due to this event is checked AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Options provided in supporting doc for Scenario 2 OR AE. Outcome of AE <> Fatal

Logic 3: AE.The subject discontinued study participation due to this event is checked AND AE.End Date and Time < DS01.Date of Disposition

Logic 4: If AE.LLT <> Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND AE. Overall Toxicity = 5 AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Options provided in supporting doc for Scenario 4

Logic 5: If AE.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND AE. Overall Toxicity = 5 AND DS01.Select the Status of the Subject for the Study <> Options provided in supporting doc for Scenario 5

Logic 6: If AE.LLT = Malignant Neoplasm Progression AND AE. Overall Toxicity = 5 AND AE.End Date and Time < DS01.Date of Disposition

Note:

1. Do not consider deleted records.

2. Include only the scenarios provided in supporting doc (Logic 1 – Scenario 1, Logic 2 – Scenario 2, Logic 3 – Scenario 3, Logic 4 – Scenario 4, Logic 5 – Scenario 5, Logic 6 – Scenario 6). If any of these scenarios is not listed in the supporting doc, the corresponding logic should not be included.
