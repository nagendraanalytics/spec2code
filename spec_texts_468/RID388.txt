Report ID: RID388

Report Title:
Location(s) Reconciliation between Lesion Evaluation and Scan forms

Description:
This report will display subjects who have a Location in Lesion Evaluation form  but there is no corresponding anatomic region that was scanned recorded in OSCN with the same Date and Method

Column Heading:
1.Country

2.Site

3.Subject

4.Lesion Evaluation Form

5.Seq#

6. Date of Lesion Evaluation Item Name

7. Date of Lesion Evaluation

8.Location (Organ)

9.Method of Assessment

10.Expected OSCN location

Logic:
LSN. "Date of Lesion Evaluation” = OSCN. Scan Date and

LSN. “Method of Assessment” match OSCN. Type of Image (per OSCN_METHOD_supp_doc.csv) and

There is no LSN. “Location” / “Organ or System Assessment Location” match OSCN. Location

Note1: Do not show deleted records

Note2: Other Method of Assessment should not be considered.

Note3: Other Location Organ should be considered.

Note 4: If Randomized Only is Y, only output records for randomized subjects.

Note 5: Equivalent methods are obtained by using the 'global oscn_method_supp_doc.csv'  global supporting document. This supporting document will contain 2 columns, 1 is Group and 2 is Method. All the Methods under the same group (i.e. same number) will be considered as equivalent (i.e. Methods of Assessment that are equal). As an example, if First Column "Group" in the 'global oscn_method_supp_doc.csv' global supporting document is "1" and for Second Column "Method" is  "Ct scan", "Ct scan without contrast"  and "Contrast enhanced CT scan" but First Column "Group" is "2" and for Second Column "Method" is "Bone Scan" and "Scintigraphy". It means that "Ct scan", "Ct scan without contrast" and "Contrast enhanced CT scan" are equivalent (can be considered as equal) between them but different from "Bone Scan" and "Scintigraphy" which at the same time are both equivalent methods.

Note 6: LSN eCRF.” Location”/”Organ or System Assessment Location”  are going to be retrieved from the ‘LSN_supp_doc’ global supporting document column C.

Report Scenarios:
Report Scenarios

A-Yes

Subjects who have a Location  in LSN eCRF that does not have a corresponding Scan Location recorded in OSCN according to the supporting document when Scan Date and Date of Lesion Evaluation are the same, and when Type of Image and Method of Assessment are the same.

B-No

Subjects who have a Location in LSN eCRF that does have a corresponding Scan Location recorded in OSCN according to the supporting document when Scan Date and Date of Lesion Evaluation Exam are the same, and when Type of Image and Method of Assessment are the same.

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number
