Report ID: RID256

Report Title:
CM Analgesic Medication check against ANL

Description:
Output is produced if the CM eCRF is completed incorrectly for medications recorded in the ANL eCRF

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. CM sequence number

5. CM Medication Name

6. ANL sequence number

7. Error Message

8. Missing Item

Logic:
ANL.Associated CM Sequence Number (From Concomitant Medication form)= CM. Sequence Number AND (CM.Total Daily Dose and Units= Null OR CM. Dose Form= Null OR CM. Route of Administration= Null OR CM. Dosage Strength = Null OR CM. Frequency of Administration = Null)

Logic 2:

ANL.Associated CM Sequence Number (From Concomitant Medication form)= CM. Sequence Number AND CM.Medication Category <> “Category 1”

Logic 3:

Logic 3.1 : ANL.Associated CM Sequence Number (From Concomitant Medication form)= CM. Sequence Number

AND

Logic 3.2: (CM.Dose Form= PATCH AND CM.Total Daily Dose and Units <> ug/h) OR  (CM.Dose Form<>PATCH AND CM.Total Daily Dose and Units <> mg)

Note 1: For logic 2, Category  1=  ADVERSE EVENT OR ANALGESIC MEDICATION. If CM Medication Category  match one of these categories, do not show output.

Note 2: For logic 3 do not show outputs if CM.Dose Form= Null OR CM.Total Daily Dose and Units = Null.

Note 3: For all logics please exclude the deleted CM and deleted ANL records and deleted Analgesic Medication Entries.

Report Scenarios:
A –Yes

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form) but either of the following items are blank: Total Daily Dose and Units, Dose Form, Route of Administration, Dosage Strength or Frequency of Administration

B- No

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form) and all the following items are complete: Total Daily Dose and Units, Dose Form, Route of Administration, Dosage Strength or Frequency of Administration

C –Yes

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form) but the Medication Category is not Adverse Event or Analgesic Medication

D –No

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form) and the Medication Category is Adverse Event or Analgesic Medication

E –Yes

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form), the Dose Form is entered as Patch; however, the Unit in the item Total Daily Dose and Units is not Micrograms Per Hour

F –No

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form), the Dose Form is entered as Patch and the Unit in the item Total Daily Dose and Units is Micrograms Per Hour

G –Yes

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form), the Dose Form is not entered as Patch; however, the Unit in the item Total Daily Dose and Units is not mg

H –No

CM sequence number is entered in the ANL eCRF in the item Associated CM Sequence Number(From Concomitant Medication form), the Dose Form is not entered as Patch and the Unit in the item Total Daily Dose and Units is mg

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number

Parameter Selection:
Country, First Site, Last Site, First Subject, Last Subject, List of Subjects, Randomized Only (N/Y, default as N)

Form.Item Names:
1. SA. Country

2. SA. Site

3. SA. Subject Number

4. CM. Sequence Number

5. CM. Medication Name*

6. ANL. Sponsor ID Number (ANL. Analgesic Sequence Number)

7. Display 'Scenario 1' if Logic 1 is met, 'Scenario 2' if Logic 2 is met, 'Scenario 3' if Logic 3 is met

*Note: For CM Medication Name display the medication name of the CM with the same sequence number as the ANL.Associated CM Sequence Number
