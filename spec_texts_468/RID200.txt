Report ID: RID200

Report Title:
SMP-SM  Medication Mismatch

Description:
This report outputs subjects who have Dose Number and/or Date Prepared discrepancies between Study Medication (SMAU/SMAB/SM01/SM02/SM04) and Study Medication Preparation (SMPU/SMPB/SMP)

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. Visit

5. Dose Number

6. Form (SMA/SM)

7. Seq (SMA/SM)

8. Form (SMP)

9. Seq (SMP)

10. Start Date of SMA/SM

11. Date of Dose Prepared

12. Issue

Logic:
This report will produce output for all subjects who have:

1) missing dose numbers on (SMPB/SMAB and SMPU/SMAU) OR (SM01/SM02/SM04 and SMP) forms within the same visit. Issue: "Dose Number Missing"

2) For same Dose Number and visit, SMA/SM start date outside of SMP Date prepared and SMP Date prepared+XX hours. Issue: "Date/Time Mismatch"

* Mandatory prompt: Window of Hours prompt should be defaulted to zero.

*"Date/Time Mismatch" should calculate into minutes.

* Please don't include deleted data/erroneous visit data into programming.

Report Scenarios:
1) Output=YES

SMP has dose number that does not exist in SMA/SM at the same visit

Issue: "Dose Number Missing"

2) Output=YES

SMA has dose number that does not exist in SMP at the same visit

Issue: "Dose Number Missing"

3) Output=YES

SMA start date is outside of SMP Date prepared and SMP Date prepared+XX hours for the same dose number and visit

Issue: "Date/Time Mismatch"

4) Output=NO

Dose numbers at the same visits are present both in SMP and SMA/SM

5) Output=NO

SMA start date is within SMP Date prepared and SMP Date prepared+XX hours for the same dose number and visit

6) Output=NO

Deleted records

7) Output=NO

Records in Erroneous visits

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Visit, Form (SMA), Form (SMP), Dose Number, Seq (SMA), Seq (SMP)

Parameter Selection:
Country, First site, Last Site, First subject, Last subjects, Randomized only, Window of hours

Form.Item Names:
1.  SITECOUNTRY (CV_SAFM)

2.  SITEMNEMONIC (CV_SAFM)

3.  ENRMSUBJNUMIT (CV_SAFM)

4.  VISITMNEMONIC (CV_SMABFM, CV_SMAUFM, CV_SMPBFM, CV_SMPUFM, CV_SMFM, CV_SMPFM)

5. SMABEXDOSSEQIT (CV_SMABFM), SMAUEXDOSSEQIT (CV_SMAUFM), SMPBEXDOSSEQIT (CV_SMPBFM), SMPUEXDOSSEQIT (CV_SMPUFM)

6.  FORMMNEMONIC (CV_SMABFM or CV_SMAUFM or CV_SMFM)

7.  FORMIDX (CV_SMABFM or CV_SMAUFM or CV_SMFM)

8.  FORMMNEMONIC (CV_SMPUFM or CV_SMPBFM or CV_SMPFM)

9.  FORMIDX (CV_SMPUFM or CV_SMPBFM or CV_SMPFM)

10. TMS_SMABEXSTDTCIT (CV_SMABFM) or TMS_SMAUEXSTDTCIT (CV_SMAUFM) or DT_SMPEXPRPDTCIT (CV_SMPFM )

11. TMS_SMPBEXPRPDTCIT (CV_SMPBFM) or TMS_SMPUEXPRPDTCIT (CV_SMPUFM)

12.  hardcode: 1) "Dose Number Missing", 2) "Date/Time Mismatch"
