Report ID: RID292

Report Title:
Reconciliation between GVHD eCRFs and AE

Description:
This Report will reconcile when HyperAcute or Acute or Chronic events are entered in AE form and the corresponding GVHD eCRFs (OAGHD, AOGVH, CGVHD) are missing and that once initialized the seq number is provided in the correct eCRF. This report will also reconcile when  GVHD eCRFs are completed and there is no corresponding AE completed.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. AE Seq#

5. AE LLT

6. GVHD eCRF

7. GVHD Seq#

8. Associated AE Seq#

9. Scenario

Logic:
Logic1.1: There is an AE with LLT = "Acute graft versus host disease" or "Hyperacute graft versus host disease"

AND

Logic1.2: AOGVH or OAGVH eCRFs are missing.

OR

Logic 2.1: There is an AE with LLT = " Chronic graft versus host disease"

AND

Logic 2.2: CGVHD eCRF is missing.

OR

Logic3.1: There is an AE with LLT = "Acute graft versus host disease" or "Hyperacute graft versus host disease"

AND

Logic3.2: AOGVH or OAGVH eCRFs are completed.

AND

Logic3.3.1: The Seq # of the AE of Logic 3.1 is not present in any of the Associated AE Sequence Number (From AE Form) of AOGVH

OR

Logic3.3.2: The Seq # of the AE of Logic 3.1 is not present in any of the Associated AE Sequence Number (From AE Form) of the Associated AE Sequence Number (From AE Form) OAGVH.

OR

Logic4.1: There is an AE with LLT = " Chronic graft versus host disease"

AND

Logic4.2: CGVHD eCRF is completed.

AND

Logic4.3: The Seq # of the AE of Logic 4.1 is not present in the Associated AE Sequence Number (From AE Form) of CGVDH

OR

Logic5.1: AOGVH or OAGVH eCRF is completed

AND

Logic5.2:There is no AE with LLT = "Acute graft versus host disease" or "Hyperacute graft versus host disease"

OR

Logic6.1: CGVHD eCRF is completed

AND

Logic6.2:There is no AE with LLT = " Chronic graft versus host disease

Note: For Logics 1 and 2 leave blank GVHD columns. For Logics 5 and 6 leave blank AE columns.

Note: Do not include include deleted records.

Report Scenarios:
A-No

There is no AE AE for Acute, Hyperacute or Chronic GVHD.

B-No

There is a AE AE for Acute, Hyperacute or Chronic GVHD, the corresponding GVHD eCRF is completed and the AE Seq# is present.

C-Yes

There is an AE for Acute or Hyperacute GVHD AND AOGVH or OAGVH eCRFs are missing.

D-Yes

There is an AE for Chronic GVHD and CGVHD eCRF is missing.

E-Yes

There is an AE for Acute or Hyperacute GVHD AND AOGVH or OAGVH eCRFs, but the AE Seq# is not provided in the AOGVH or OAGVH eCRFs.

F-Yes

There is an AE for Chronic GVHD and CGVHD eCRF is completed, but the AE Seq# is not provided in the CGVHD.

G-Yes

AOGVH or OAGVH eCRF is completed and there is no AE for Acute or Hyperacute GVHD.

H-Yes

CGVHD eCRF is completed and there is no AE for Chronic GVHD.
