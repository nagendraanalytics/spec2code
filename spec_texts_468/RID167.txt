Report ID: RID167

Report Title:
Toxicity grade vs Death check

Description:
This report lists subjects whose Maximum Toxicity is equal to selected toxicity grade (4 or 5, but SAEI outcome is not fatal or serious criteria is not Death.

The report also lists subjects who have SAEI outcome that is fatal or Serious criteria is Death, but Maximum Toxicity is not equal to selected toxicity grade (4 or 5).

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. SAEI Seq

5. Maximum Toxicity Grade

6. SAEI Outcome

7. Serious Criteria

Logic:
Logic 1: when maximum Toxicity Grade is equal x.

And SAEI outcome is not Fatal or serious criteria is not Death.

Or

Logic 2: when SAEI outcome is Fatal or serious criteria is Death. And maximum Toxicitiy Grade is not equal to x.

Note: 1. Report should not display deleted record

          2. X can be 4 or 5.

Report Scenarios:
Scenario A, output Yes:

when max Toxicity grade=x. And SAEI outcome <> fatal or serious criteria <> death

Scenario B, output No:

when max Toxicity grade=x. And SAEI outcome= fatal or serious critieria=death

Scenario C, output Yes:

 when SAEI outcome= fatal or serious critieria=death. And max Toxicity grade<>x

Scenario D, output No:

 when SAEI outcome= fatal or serious critieria=death. And max Toxicity grade=x

Scenario E, output No:

Deleted record

Sorting:
Country, Site, Subject Number, SAEI Seq, Max Toxicity Grade, SAEI outcome, Serious Criteria

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site, Subject Number, Randomized only, List of Subjects, Maximum Toxicity Grade
