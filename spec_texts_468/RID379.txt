Report ID: RID379

Report Title:
Bone Lesion Reconciliation

Description:
This report will list subjects that do not have any Bone Lesions in LSNPC for a given timepoint and ORPC Bone Lesion Response is entered incorrectly. It will also show if there are Bone Lesions in LSNPC, but Bone Lesion Response in ORPC does not list any.

Column Heading:
1. Country

2. Site

3. Subject Number

4. LSNPC Seq

5. LSNPC Lesion Identification

6. LSNPC Date

7. LSPNC Lesion status

8. ORPC Seq

9. ORPC Date

10. ORPC Bone Lesion Response

11. Scenario

Logic:
Logic 1

There is LSNPC record with LSNPC.Lesion identification = Bone Lesion

AND

LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam <> NULL

AND

LSNPC. Bone Lesion Status = Present

AND

There is a record in ORPC with Date Response Determined <> Null

AND

ORPC. Date Response Determined is between LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) +/- X ‘Window of Days’ according to parameter

AND

ORPC. Bone Lesion Response = No Bone Lesions Present

Hardcode: Scenario 1

Logic 2

There is LSNPC record with LSNPC.Lesion identification = New Bone Lesion

AND

LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam <> NULL

AND

LSNPC. New Bone Lesion Status = Present

AND

There is a record in ORPC with Date Response Determined <> Null

AND

ORPC. Date Response Determined is between LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) +/- X ‘Window of Days’ according to parameter

AND

ORPC. Bone Lesion Response = No Bone Lesions Present

Hardcode: Scenario 1

Logic 3

There is LSNPC record with LSNPC.Lesion identification = Bone Lesion or New Bone Lesion

AND

LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam <> NULL

AND

All LSNPC records with the same LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) have  LSNPC. Bone Lesion Status = Absent and LSNPC. New Bone Lesion Status = Absent

AND

There is a record in ORPC with Date Response Determined <> Null

AND

ORPC. Date Response Determined is between LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) +/- X ‘Window of Days’ according to parameter

AND

ORPC. Bone Lesion Response <> No Bone Lesions Present

Hardcode: Scenario 2

Logic 4

There is NOT at least one LSNPC record with LSNPC.Lesion identification = Bone Lesion or New Bone Lesion

AND

There is a record in ORPC with Date Response Determined <> Null

AND

ORPC. Bone Lesion Response <> No Bone Lesions Present , Not Evaluable

Hardcode: Scenario 3

Notes:

Note 1: Window is inclusive, for example if user enters 14 days, then report would check for records with a date difference 14 days or less.

Note 2: For Logic 1,2,3 on column #4 display Sequence numbers separated by commas (,) for those outputs that share the same lesion identification (#5) and date (#6) (for example display as 1(3), 2(3), 3(2)).

Note 3: Please exclude deleted records and erroneous visits from programming.

Note 4: Default window of days should be 14 days (however this window can be modified by user)

Report Scenarios:
A-Yes

ORPC Bone Lesion Response = "No Bone Lesions Present" AND there is at least one corrsponding LSNCP entry with LSNPC Lesion Identification= "Bone Lesion" and LSPNC Bone Lesion Status for this lesion= "Present"

B- Yes

ORPC Bone Lesion Response = "No Bone Lesions Present" AND there is at least one corresponding LSNCP entry with LSNPC Lesion Identification= "New Bone Lesion" and LSPNC New Bone Lesion Status for this lesion= "Present"

C- Yes

ORPC Bone Lesion Response <> "No Bone Lesions Present"  AND there is at least one corresponding LSNCP entry with LSNPC Lesion Identification= "Bone Lesion" or "New Bone Lesion" and All LSNPC records with the same LSNPC Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam) have LSNPC Bone Lesion Status = "Absent" and LSNPC New Bone Lesion Status = "Absent"

D- Yes

ORPC Bone Lesion Response <> "No Bone Lesions Present" or "Not Evaluable" AND there is not at least one corresponding LSNCP entry with LSNPC Lesion Identification= "Bone Lesion" or "New Bone Lesion"

E-No Deleted record

Sorting:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, LSNPC Date

Parameter Selection:
Country, Site Number, Subject Number, Window of Days

Form.Item Names:
1. SA Form. Site Country

2. SA Form. Site Mnemonic

3. SA Form. Subject Number

4. LSNPC. Associated Sponsor ID Number

5. LSNPC. Lesion Identification

6. LSNPC. Date of Current Lesion Evaluation (Scan/Image/Physical Exam)

7. LSNPC. Bone Lesion Status or New Bone Lesion Status

8. ORPC. Form Index

9. ORPC. Date Response Determined

10. ORPC. Bone Lesion Response

11. Display Scenario 1 or Scenario 2 or Scenario 3 as hardcoded per logic.
